# Learnings for the hourly desk runs

Read this before choosing stories. The weekly numbers report rewrites it every week (latest:
`reports/weekly-2026-11-16.md`). Tags: **[data]** = Instagram numbers; **[reviewer]** = the blind reviewer's score
cuts; **[schedule]** = how posts went out. Follow it unless it clashes with a RUNBOOK hard rule; the RUNBOOK wins.

## This week (to 16 Nov 2026)

- [data] Still no reach, share or save numbers (Meta refuses insights on all five tokens). [data] lines rest on 51 likes over 221 posts: leanings, not proof.
- [data] 0 comments on 221 posts, though reply scored 2 on 198. Score reply 2 only if almost every follower can answer in a word or two; else reply 1.
- [data] All desks: ask an easy question from readers' own lives, tied to the card ("What do you pay for data now?"). Not ones only owners, players or a few can answer.
- [data] All desks: likes went to an Indian reader's stake with the number on the cover: ₹ price, discount end date, a ruling that changes family rights, a warning.
- [data] All desks: foreign stories with no Indian angle got 0 (US recalls, US-only drops, Qantas layoffs). Pick them only with an India price, date or effect.
- [data] All desks: posts from midnight to 6 AM IST got 3 likes from 43; 6 PM to midnight got 16 from 45. Lane: leave non-urgent approvals for the morning.
- [data] Scores don't predict likes (rank correlation 0.08 on 220 posts). Don't raise a score because a story "feels" big; score each check on what's on the card.
- [data] @idlynews: SC rulings on family rights (grandson's partition share) and cyclone/IMD warnings got the likes. Parliament procedure got 0 after 6 days.
- [data] @idlynews: lead with what changes for the reader and when (deadline, date, who is affected), not with the institution's step.
- [data] @idlytech: likes went to Indian plans and speeds (Jio ₹10,000 three-year plan, Airtel vs Jio) and India launch dates. Foreign layoffs and patch notes got 0.
- [data] @idlyhype: all 4 liked posts were drops sold in India with the ₹ price on the cover. US-only $ releases got 0; skip unless India date and ₹ price exist.
- [data] @idlyhype: "where to buy in India" lists got 2 likes from 4 posts; news got 2 from 26. Run a list when 3+ India-available drops share a theme.
- [data] @idlyhype: no post since 11 Nov has a like; 19 priced posts got 0, so price alone isn't enough. Try Indian labels (Comet) and lists over single US restocks.
- [data] @idlyautomotive: likes went to buyer-actionable India news: Duster ₹2.10 lakh off till 30 Nov, XUV 3XO CNG 5 Bharat NCAP stars, GLC sold out to Mar 2027.
- [data] @idlyautomotive: the US Ford Explorer probe got 0 after 6 days. Prefer discounts with end dates, safety ratings, waiting periods and India prices.
- [data] @idlynerd: big-number milestones fans follow got the likes (Ramayana ₹800 cr and ₹1,000 cr, Resident Evil 4 Golden Joysticks, Zelda 5.2M sold).
- [data] @idlynerd: lane posts did as well as desk posts (5 likes from 10 vs 8 from 30); keep using the lane for official stills.
- [reviewer] All desks: send and keep are now the most over-scored checks (cut 15 and 13 times in 165 posts); hook is now scored about right.
- [reviewer] All desks: send 2 needs a specific person who'd forward it today. Narrow audiences (RE buyers, base-PS5 owners) or yesterday's known result = send 1.
- [reviewer] All desks: keep 2 needs the date, price, deadline or link on a slide. If it's only in the caption, or missing, keep is 1.
- [reviewer] All desks: every slide earns its place: no mirror-image bullets, no stale past dates as the big stat, no unexplained acronyms (BLO).
- [reviewer] @idlynerd and @idlyhype: name the game or product on every detail slide and on the cover ("Tyler's Boot: ₹11,499" didn't name the boot).
- [schedule] All desks: before publishing, check `queue/approved/` for that account; if a lane item is approved, wait until it posts and 30 min pass.
- [schedule] Same page, same topic: at least 2 hours apart; don't lead two posts with the same film, product or story in a day, or reuse the same cover still.
