## Lessons for learnings.md (next weekly rewrite)
- **All desks:** check the image route at Gate 1, not after it. All three posts this run passed G1 with a framed, face-screened image already in hand, and none needed a picture fix later.
- **@idlynews:** a different frame from the same official photo series avoids repeating a cover while keeping the actual subject. When the face screen flags an empty room, move the focal point and re-screen instead of switching subjects.
- **@idlyhype:** side-on studio shots of low shoes leave about 280 px of white above the shoe in 4:5 and cost a hook point. Prefer 3/4 or on-foot-free lifestyle product images when the brand has them.
