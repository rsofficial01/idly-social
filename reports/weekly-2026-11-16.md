## What needs you

### 1. Turn on Instagram insights (about 10 minutes, browser). Second week asking.

**What's missing:** all five tokens (`IG_TOKEN_IDLYNEWS`, `_IDLYTECH`, `_IDLYHYPE`, `_IDLYAUTOMOTIVE`, `_IDLYNERD`,
all on graph.facebook.com) still have only `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_comments`,
`instagram_manage_messages`, `ads_management`, `ads_read` and `public_profile`. They lack
**`instagram_manage_insights`**, which Meta requires for reach, shares, saves, profile views and follows. Meta's docs
also list **`pages_read_engagement`** for these calls, so add both. (`instagram_business_manage_insights` is the name of
the same permission for Instagram-login tokens. Ours are Facebook-login tokens, so it's `instagram_manage_insights`.)
Meta's reply on every page: "(#10) Application does not have permission for this action".

1. **[browser]** Go to developers.facebook.com, then **My Apps**, and open **idly publisher**.
2. **[browser]** In the left menu open **Use cases**. Next to the Instagram use case, select **Customize**.
3. **[browser]** Under **Permissions and features**, find **instagram_manage_insights** and select **Add**. Do the
   same for **pages_read_engagement** if it isn't already added.
4. **[browser]** If the app has a **Facebook Login for Business → Configurations** page, open the configuration
   the tokens came from, tick the same two permissions, and save.
5. **[browser]** Go to **API setup with Unified login → 2. Generate access tokens**. Select **Generate token** next to
   each of the five accounts. In Meta's pop-up, leave the new permissions ticked. Copy each token.
6. **[browser]** On claude.ai/code, open the environment selector, then the **gear** on the idly environment.
   Under **Environment variables**, replace the five `IG_TOKEN_…` values, then select **Save changes**.
7. **[browser]** On GitHub, open idly-social, then **Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions**, and update the same
   five `IG_TOKEN_…` secrets. The official image lane posts with these
   ([docs/SETUP-GITHUB-SECRETS.md](../docs/SETUP-GITHUB-SECRETS.md) has the screens).

Nothing is needed on your phone. If Meta says either permission needs App Review, or you can't find it under
**Customize**, say so in a session and we'll work out the route. Standard access normally covers your own accounts.

**Meanwhile (optional, phone):** the Instagram app shows shares and saves for any single post. Open a post, then
**View insights**. Checking the three best posts below would tell us more than this whole report can.

## Do our 7-check scores match real shares?

**Shares still can't be tested: Meta won't give us the numbers.** Against what we can see:

- **Higher scores don't get more likes.** There's no link between a post's score and its likes (rank correlation
  0.08 across 220 posts; 1.0 would be a perfect match). The time a post has been up predicts likes better (0.31).
  The total barely varies anyway: 114 posts scored 12, 102 scored 13 and 4 scored 14.
- **No single check predicts likes either.** Posts scored 2 on hook got the same likes per post as posts scored 1
  (0.22 each). Send: 0.24 against 0.18. Keep: 0.22 against 0.20.
- **"Reply" is the check that's misjudged.** Desks and the blind reviewer gave reply full marks on 198 of 220 posts.
  The pages now have 121 followers, and not one of 221 posts got a comment. Last week this was too early to call. With
  a week of posts it isn't. The reviewer's own notes show why: many questions are fair but only a few readers can
  answer them ("have you had a visa cancelled?", "did you finish The Outer Worlds 2?"). Some are hypothetical
  opinions. From now on, full marks needs a question almost every follower can answer in a word or two.
- **Where the reviewer marks desks down now:** across 165 posts with both scores, the blind reviewer cut **send** 15
  times, **keep** 13, **hook** 11 and **read** 10. Last week hook was the most over-scored. This week the reviewer
  raised hook as often as it cut it, so desks have corrected. Send and keep are now the generous ones.
  - Send was cut for audiences too narrow to forward to anyone, and for news the reader has already seen.
  - Keep was cut when the actionable detail (a date, link or deadline) was only in the caption or missing.

## What changes for the desks

`state/learnings.md` now holds this week's guidance (24 lines). RUNBOOK step 3 already tells every run to read it
before choosing stories. The three main changes:

1. **Ask a question almost every follower can answer from their own life in a word or two,** tied to the card
   ("What do you pay for mobile data now?"). Score reply 1 when only owners, players or people with a rare experience
   can answer it.
2. **Lead with an Indian reader's stake:** a ₹ price for something sold here, a discount with its end date, a
   ruling or warning that changes daily life. Skip foreign stories with no Indian angle (US recalls, US-only drops).
3. **Score send and keep as strictly as the reviewer does:** send 2 needs a specific person who would forward it
   today; keep 2 needs the date, price or deadline on a slide, not just in the caption.

## How this was measured

- Read-only calls with `pipeline/publish.py`'s `IGClient` (Graph API v27.0, graph.facebook.com, each page's own
  token, sent only in the header). Nothing was posted, and no token was printed or saved.
- Per page: followers, following and post count, the token's granted permissions, account insights for both weeks
  (reach, profile views, follows and unfollows, accounts engaged, total interactions, views) and daily follower
  count. All insights were refused.
- Per post (last 14 days): type, time, link, first line of the caption, likes and comments. Post insights (reach,
  likes, comments, shares, saved, total interactions, views) were refused.
- The follower change is against last week's report (10 Nov, 16:22 IST), because follower history needs insights.
- Each post was matched to `state/posted.json` by media id: 220 of 221 matched (the Nvidia Reel was posted by hand).
  Each matched post was linked to its folder or lane item. The score is the recorded one: the lower of the desk's
  and the blind reviewer's, check by check. The format is the one the desk recorded (171 posts, nearly all of them
  since 11 Nov). For the other 49, mostly 10 Nov, it was inferred from the folder name and headline. Official-account
  tags are recorded for 174 posts.
- Instagram Stories aren't in the media list, and none were recorded in `state/posted.json` this week.
