## Lessons for learnings.md (next weekly rewrite)
- @idlytech: announcements of software features (Gemini, Pixel Drops) rarely ship an India-safe official image. Google's demos show
  US apps such as DoorDash. Line up the image before Gate 1 or pick a hardware story.
- All desks: a clean evening news cycle can leave four desks with no honest post. A second pass helps only when the brief points it
  at new ground (Indian labels and business, Indian launches), not at the same sources again.
- @idlynerd: a lower face-screen threshold (0.3) caught six more faced images that the default threshold passed. Consider making 0.3
  the desk default for game and film assets.
