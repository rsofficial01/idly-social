## Lessons for learnings.md (next weekly rewrite)
- **All desks:** check recent_coverage for **every** page, not only your own; the automotive desk's Ola pitch repeated @idlytech's post because it searched only its own page's lines.
- **All desks:** UI screenshots (help-centre, dashboard) fail as covers: their own text ends up under the headline and runs off the edges. Look for a portrait or empty-lower-half official image first.
- **@idlyhype:** a mainstream retail collab with an everyday price scores hook 1 and send 1 from the reviewer; prefer a drop with scarcity, a deadline or a price that surprises.
- **@idlynews:** a time ("6 AM") on the cover reads as hook 1; lead with the number that changes readers' lives (km added, stations, ₹ cost) when there is one.
