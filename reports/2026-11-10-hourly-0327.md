## Why #1719 was skipped

- **Photo.** The only relevant photos were Apple Newsroom press images of Logic Pro (Stem Splitter and
  hero shots, 3840 px, no people, no watermark). Their `LEGAL_NOTICE` says the images "cannot be altered or
  modified in any way" without Apple's permission. A hype cover crops the photo to 4:5 and lays a gradient
  and headline over it, so I treated the photo as not usable. Unsplash was behind a browser-check page,
  and Wikimedia Commons returned HTTP 429 to both its API and its image server, as in earlier runs.
- **Facts.** The idly article conflicts with its sources in two places:
  - It says "Apple said these models come from OpenAI and Google Gemini". Engadget says the third-party models
    aren't named. The Verge couldn't be read from here.
  - It says Apple hasn't said "how long the extra access lasts". Engadget, and other coverage found in
    a search, say unused usage rolls over and expires 12 months after purchase.
  The $49.99 one-time price, the "Buy More Usage" button and the monthly limit match Engadget and 9to5Mac.
  A card could have avoided both conflicting claims, but they're worth fixing on idly.news.
- **For the desk:** Engadget also lists India among the eight countries where the packs are on sale. That
  fact isn't in the idly article.
