## Notes for the setup

- `run_day.py render` only produces the classic template, so hourly hype posts go through `cardkit.render_cards(..., template="hype")` and `publish.py post`, the same route as the 10 Nov morning posts. The 12/14 gate and caption rules are still enforced by `publish_post`.
- `--slot` only accepts am/pm, so this post is recorded as `2026-11-10 am`. `publish.py post` doesn't block a second post in the same slot, but `run_day.py publish` would skip @idlyautomotive for the rest of the 10 Nov am slot.
- The container's `python3` (3.11) needed `requests`, `Pillow` and `playwright` installed with `python3 -m pip`. Cardkit ran with `CARDKIT_CHROME=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`.
- Wikimedia Commons rate-limited this container (HTTP 429) after a few requests, which narrowed the photo search.
