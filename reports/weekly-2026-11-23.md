## What needs you

### 1. Turn on Instagram insights (about 10 minutes, browser). Third week asking.

**What's missing:** all five tokens (`IG_TOKEN_IDLYNEWS`, `_IDLYTECH`, `_IDLYHYPE`, `_IDLYAUTOMOTIVE`, `_IDLYNERD`, all
on graph.facebook.com) still have only `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_comments`,
`instagram_manage_messages`, `ads_management`, `ads_read` and `public_profile`. Reach, shares, saves, profile views and
follows need **`instagram_manage_insights`**, and Meta's docs also list **`pages_read_engagement`**. (On Instagram-login
tokens the same permission is called `instagram_business_manage_insights`. Ours are Facebook-login tokens.) Meta's reply
on every page: "(#10) Application does not have permission for this action".

1. **[browser]** Go to developers.facebook.com, then **My Apps**, and open **idly publisher**.
2. **[browser]** In the left menu open **Use cases**. Next to the Instagram use case, select **Customize**.
3. **[browser]** Under **Permissions and features**, find **instagram_manage_insights** and select **Add**. Do the same
   for **pages_read_engagement** if it isn't already there.
4. **[browser]** If the app has a **Facebook Login for Business → Configurations** page, open the configuration the
   tokens came from, tick the same two permissions, and save.
5. **[browser]** Go to **API setup with Unified login → 2. Generate access tokens**. Select **Generate token** next to
   each of the five accounts. Leave the new permissions ticked in Meta's pop-up. Copy each token.
6. **[browser]** On claude.ai/code, open the environment selector, then the **gear** on the idly environment. Under
   **Environment variables**, replace the five `IG_TOKEN_…` values, then select **Save changes**.
7. **[browser]** On GitHub, open idly-social, then **Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions**, and update the same
   five `IG_TOKEN_…` secrets. The official image lane posts with these
   ([docs/SETUP-GITHUB-SECRETS.md](../docs/SETUP-GITHUB-SECRETS.md) shows the screens).

If Meta says either permission needs App Review, or you can't find it under **Customize**, say so in a session and
we'll work out another route.

**Meanwhile (optional, phone):** in the Instagram app, open any post and tap **View insights**. It shows that post's
reach, shares and saves. Checking two or three posts would tell us whether non-followers see anything at all.

## Do our 7-check scores match real shares?

**Shares still can't be tested: Meta won't give us the numbers.** Against likes and comments:

- **Higher scores still don't get more reactions.** Across the last 14 days (298 scored posts), the link between a
  post's score and its likes is 0.08 (1.0 would be a perfect match). How long the post had been up predicts likes
  better (0.34). This week alone it's 0.18, but that rests on 3 likes.
- **"Send" is the one check that matches.** Since 13 Nov, posts scored send 2 got 7 of the 8 reactions (56 posts), and
  posts scored send 1 got 1 (83 posts). In the launch days (10–12 Nov) the gap was small (0.30 vs 0.25 likes per
  post); it opened up once the first-day likes stopped. The numbers are tiny, but no other check shows even that much: hook 2 and hook 1 posts got the same likes per post (0.17 vs
  0.18), and keep 2 got fewer than keep 1 (0.15 vs 0.20).
- **More posts now score send 1.** This week 60 of 78 posts scored send 1, against about 6 in 10 scoring send 2 in the
  launch days. After last week's advice to score send strictly, the desks now under-score it: the reviewer raised send
  7 times and cut it once. So the desks are scoring send honestly but still picking stories nobody would forward.
- **"Reply" is still misjudged.** 69 of 78 posts scored full marks on reply after last week's stricter rule, and 1
  comment came. The questions got easier, but with this few followers, a question on the card isn't what's missing.
- **Where the reviewer marks desks down now:** hook (cut 9 times in 78 posts) and original (8).
  - Hook was cut when the cover led with a model code or a plain price or rating with nothing surprising (BMW R 12 G/S
    at ₹25.40 lakh, Sony WH-CH530 at ₹4,490, Verna 5 stars, a vague "after a price rise").
  - Original was cut when the card restated other outlets on the same official image everyone runs, or reused our own
    cover still.

## What changes for the desks

`state/learnings.md` now holds this week's guidance (21 lines). RUNBOOK step 3 already tells every run, hourly ones
included, to read it before choosing stories, so the RUNBOOK needed no change. The three main changes:

1. **Pick the story someone would forward today.** When two candidates both pass the gate, post the one that honestly
   earns send 2, even over a higher total. On @idlyhype and @idlyautomotive, skip any story that doesn't earn send 2.
2. **Lead with what people are living through now** (a cyclone over their state, a mass film's bookings), and stop
   follow-ups that have no new milestone or photo (third Ramayana weekend, more GTA VI sales numbers). On @idlytech,
   at most one "price went up" post a day.
3. **Fix the two checks the reviewer cuts most.** Hook: put the surprising fact or the reader's stake on the cover, not
   a model code or a plain price. Original: add idly's own comparison or arithmetic on a slide, and never reuse a cover
   still.
