## Lessons for learnings.md (next weekly rewrite)
- **All desks:** with a sign or lettered facade in the photo, check where the headline's top line lands. A 3-line headline starts at about y 950, a 2-line one at about y 1050. Shortening the headline can clear photo lettering when the crop can't move.
- **All desks:** Wikimedia's upload host is rate-limiting (429) the original files. Plan with the 1920 px thumbnail (still ≥ 1080 px for a 4:5 crop of a landscape photo) or a maker's own press image.
