## For Rahul: two rule decisions made mid-run

1. **Upscale limit.** This routine says "upscale no more than about 1.2x"; the RUNBOOK "Cover style" section committed
   at 10:08 UTC today (full-bleed only) allows up to 2x through `pipeline.framing` (Lanczos + sharpening) for hourly
   runs. Under full-bleed, every 1280×720 trailer thumbnail needs ~1.9x, so the 1.2x line would block nearly every
   trailer cover. This run applied: plain resizes ≤ ~1.2x; `pipeline.framing` enlargements up to 2x, with the reviewer
   holding anything soft at full size. Only the @idlynerd cover used it (1.41x, judged crisp). Please confirm which
   limit you want.
2. **Hype under full-bleed.** Square studio product shots with the product low in the frame (Balenciaga's, and most
   brand e-commerce images) can't be framed full-bleed clear of the headline, and extending the backdrop is now banned.
   @idlyhype will skip often unless brands publish portrait or campaign images, or the lane can take brand campaign
   stills with people (vans.com, adidas.com, balenciaga.com aren't allowlisted).
