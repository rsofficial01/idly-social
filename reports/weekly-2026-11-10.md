## What needs you

### 1. Turn on Instagram insights (about 10 minutes, browser)

**What's missing:** all five tokens (`IG_TOKEN_IDLYNEWS`, `_IDLYTECH`, `_IDLYHYPE`, `_IDLYAUTOMOTIVE`, `_IDLYNERD`,
all on graph.facebook.com) have `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `instagram_manage_comments`,
`instagram_manage_messages`, `ads_management`, `ads_read` and `public_profile`. They lack
**`instagram_manage_insights`**, which Meta requires for reach, shares, saves, profile views and follows.
Meta's docs also list **`pages_read_engagement`** for these calls, so add both. Meta's reply each time:
"(#10) Application does not have permission for this action".

1. **[browser]** Go to developers.facebook.com, then **My Apps**, and open **idly publisher**.
2. **[browser]** In the left menu open **Use cases**. Next to the Instagram use case, select **Customize**.
3. **[browser]** Under **Permissions and features**, find **instagram_manage_insights** and select **Add**. Do the
   same for **pages_read_engagement** if it isn't already added.
4. **[browser]** If the app has a **Facebook Login for Business → Configurations** page, open the configuration
   the tokens came from, tick the same two permissions, and save.
5. **[browser]** Go to **API setup with Unified login → 2. Generate access tokens**. Select **Generate token** next to
   each of the five accounts. In Meta's pop-up, leave the new permission ticked. Copy each token.
6. **[browser]** On claude.ai/code, open the environment selector, then the **gear** on the idly environment.
   Under **Environment variables**, replace the five `IG_TOKEN_…` values, then select **Save changes**.
7. **[browser]** On GitHub, open idly-social, then **Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions**, and update the same
   five `IG_TOKEN_…` secrets. The official image lane posts with these
   ([docs/SETUP-GITHUB-SECRETS.md](../docs/SETUP-GITHUB-SECRETS.md) has the screens).

Nothing is needed on your phone. If Meta says the permission needs App Review, mention it in the next session.
Standard access normally covers your own accounts. Next week's report will confirm whether insights work.

## Do our 7-check scores match real shares?

**This can't be tested yet: there are no share numbers.** Here's what can be said.

- **Scores barely vary.** The gate lets through only 12 to 14, so 17 posts scored 12, 19 scored 13 and 2 scored 14.
  Even with shares, the total won't separate posts much. Next week's test should compare single checks with the
  number each one predicts: **keep** against saves, **send** against shares, **reply** against comments.
- **Likes don't follow the score.** The rank correlation of likes with total score is −0.18, effectively zero.
  Likes follow how long a post has been up (+0.40). For now, age explains likes, not quality.
- **Reply is the first check to watch.** 33 of 38 posts scored 2 for a genuine reader question, yet no post on any
  page has a comment. With 9 to 66 followers that's expected so far. If comments are still zero next week with
  more followers, reply is being over-scored.
- **Desks are most generous on hook.** On the 36 posts with both a desk and a blind-reviewer score, the reviewer
  marked hook lower than the desk 6 times (higher once). Read was lower 3 times. Other checks mostly agreed. The
  reviewer's reasons:
  - the big number was on slide 2, not the cover (six airbags);
  - "TRAILER" lettering on a review cover (Scene);
  - a niche foreign label with no India release (Story mfg.);
  - a procedural court deadline with no number on the cover (NCR pollution). That post still got @idlynews's most
    likes, but 2 likes prove nothing.

## What changes for the desks

`state/learnings.md` now holds this week's guidance (14 lines). RUNBOOK step 3 tells hourly runs to read it before
choosing stories. The three main changes:

1. **Put the scroll-stopping number on the cover itself, not on slide 2,** and score hook 1 when it's only inside.
2. **Spread same-topic posts on one page at least 2 hours apart,** and never lead two posts on the same film,
   product or story the same day.
3. **Record each post's format** (news, list, special or review) in its meta.json or lane spec, so next week's report
   can compare formats with real share and save numbers instead of guessing.

## How this was measured

- Read-only calls with `pipeline/publish.py`'s `IGClient` (Graph API v27.0, graph.facebook.com, each page's own
  token, sent only in the header). Nothing was posted and no token was printed or saved.
- Per page: followers, following and post count, plus account insights for both weeks (reach, profile views,
  views, accounts engaged, total interactions, follows and unfollows, daily follower count). All were refused.
- Per post (last 14 days): type, time, link, caption first line, likes and comments. Post insights (reach, likes,
  comments, shares, saved, total interactions, views) were refused.
- Each post was matched to `state/posted.json` by media id (38 of 38 automated posts matched; the Nvidia Reel was
  posted by hand). Its 7-check score is the recorded one: the lower of the desk's and the blind reviewer's, from
  `score.json` or the lane's spec and approval files. Posts don't record a format, so I labelled them from
  their content: 31 news, 4 review, 2 special (Morning Brief, October bestsellers) and 1 list (six airbags).
- Instagram Stories aren't in the media list, and none were recorded in `state/posted.json` this week.
