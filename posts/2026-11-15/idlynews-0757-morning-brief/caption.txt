Morning Brief, Sunday 15 November.

The low over the southeast Bay of Bengal has strengthened into a well-marked low. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) expects it to become a depression on Sunday, a cyclonic storm around Monday evening and a severe cyclonic storm by 18 November. IMD has not named a landfall area; its Amaravati office told The Hindu most models point to the Andhra Pradesh coast. IMD warns of very heavy rain in coastal Andhra Pradesh on 19 November and in Odisha on 19-20 November, with thundersqualls. Fishermen have been told to return to shore by Sunday. It would be the Bay's third cyclonic storm since late October, after Arnab and Murasu, which both hit the Andhra coast.

Pakistan will send Climate Change Minister Musadik Masood Malik to the SCO Civilisational Dialogue in New Delhi on 20-22 November, its Foreign Ministry said on Saturday. He would be the first Pakistani minister to visit India since May 2023. India had invited Pakistan's culture minister.

India and the US are "almost at the finishing line" on a trade deal, with the bulk of the issues sorted, External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar said on Saturday; what remains is "not so much at our end". He gave no timeframe. PM Narendra Modi is expected to meet US President Donald Trump in Manila around the 16-17 November ASEAN summits, The Indian Express reports.

India beat Pakistan by one wicket to win the Under-19 Asia Cup in Oman on Saturday, chasing 338. Needing three off the final ball, Mohit Ulva hit a four. It is India's ninth title.

US negotiators asked whether Ukraine would leave the Donbas region if it joined NATO, the first time the idea was raised, President Volodymyr Zelensky said in remarks released on Saturday. He would put any concrete offer to a referendum. The US has not confirmed his account.
