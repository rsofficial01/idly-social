God of War: Sons of Sparta, the 2D side-scrolling God of War game from Mega Cat Studios and Santa Monica Studio, comes to PS4 on Monday, 16 November. Published by Sony Interactive Entertainment, it has been out on PS5 since 12 February 2026. It follows young Kratos and his brother Deimos through their Spartan training.

The same day, a free update adds two new modes on both PS4 and PS5. Hermes Mode is for speedrunners, with a split-based timer and a persistent scoreboard. It unlocks after you finish the game once, or straight away with this code on the start menu: Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, L1, R1, Touchpad. In Erebus Mode, you and every enemy go down in a single hit. You choose the mode when you start a new game and pick a save slot, and it can't be changed for that save.

On PS Store India today, the PS5 version costs ₹2,497 and the Digital Deluxe Edition ₹3,330. The PS4 version's price isn't listed yet.
