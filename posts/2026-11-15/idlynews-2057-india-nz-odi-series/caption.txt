India beat New Zealand by 22 runs in the fifth and final ODI at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on Sunday 15 November to win the five-match series 3-1. Batting first, India were all out for 209, with Ishan Kishan and Axar Patel making 46 each. New Zealand were bowled out for 187, with Arshdeep Singh taking 3 for 30.

Two days earlier, at the same ground, India were bowled out for 89 and lost by 244 runs. The third ODI, in Hamilton, was washed out without a ball bowled.

The result ends New Zealand's run of 11 straight home bilateral ODI series wins, according to PTI and Sky Sports; their last home ODI series defeat was also against India, in 2019. India had already won the T20I series 3-2.

The two-Test series starts on Thursday 19 November at the Basin Reserve, Wellington, and the second Test is at Hagley Oval, Christchurch, from 27 November. Play starts at 3:30 am IST, on Sony Sports Network and Sony LIV in India.
