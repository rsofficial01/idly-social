The India Meteorological Department (IMD) put the Andhra Pradesh and adjoining south Odisha coasts on a pre-cyclone watch on Sunday, the first of its four cyclone warning stages.

In its 3 pm bulletin, IMD said the depression over the southeast Bay of Bengal is likely to become a cyclonic storm on 16 November and a severe cyclonic storm on 17 November, then move northwest towards the Andhra Pradesh coast. Its forecast has the system as a very severe cyclonic storm over the sea by the night of 18 November, with winds of 120-130 km/h gusting to 145 km/h by midday on 19 November. IMD has not named a landfall point; its regional centre says models diverge on the track and landfall.

IMD warns of extremely heavy rain (21 cm or more) at isolated places in north coastal Andhra on 19-20 November, heavy to very heavy rain in north Tamil Nadu and Puducherry on 18-19 November, and in Odisha on 19-21 November. Fishermen at sea were told to return to the coast by Sunday. If it becomes a cyclonic storm, it would be named Akvan, after Arnab and Murasu, both of which have crossed the Andhra coast since late October.
