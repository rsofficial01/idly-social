Kick, the livestreaming site, is giving away 10,000 digital codes for Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. In India that edition costs ₹7,499 on PlayStation's store.

To enter, watch any Kick stream for 3 hours. That unlocks a case beside your avatar, and opening it is your entry; a new case unlocks every day. No purchase, payment or wager is needed. Kick doesn't state the odds, so each case is a chance, not a promise.

Indian players can take part. PS5 codes go only to PSN accounts registered in Kick's list of supported countries, which includes India; Xbox codes are open worldwide. Kick says codes won't go out before 19 November, GTA VI's launch day, and that it runs the promotion itself, with no tie to Rockstar Games or Take-Two. Its announcement gives no end date.

Twitch has its own plan: its 13 November blog post promises surprise daily drops of GTA VI codes on streams from launch to the end of November, without saying how many or where.
