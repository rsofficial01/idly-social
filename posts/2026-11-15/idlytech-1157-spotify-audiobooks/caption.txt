Spotify launched audiobooks in India on Thursday, 12 November. Its Audiobooks+ add-on costs ₹199 a month for 15 hours of listening, after a one-month free trial for new subscribers, the company says. Audiobooks+ can be added on top of a Premium plan or to Spotify's free plan.

The catalogue starts with more than 3.5 lakh English titles and about 7,000 audiobooks in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, including Kalki's Ponniyin Selvan, Premchand's Godaan and Tagore's Kabuliwala.

The fine print: unused hours expire at the end of each billing month, extra hours come as one-off top-ups, and single titles can be bought outright. Spotify counts time at normal playback speed, so listening at 2x doesn't stretch the 15 hours. Its Page Match feature lets you scan a printed book's barcode and pick up the audio from the same page.
