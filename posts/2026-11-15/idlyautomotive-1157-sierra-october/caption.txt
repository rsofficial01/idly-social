Tata Motors shipped 7,205 Sierras to dealers in October 2026, more than the Harrier (3,895) and Safari (2,287) combined, according to model-wise dispatch figures reported by Rushlane and GaadiWaadi. The Sierra was not on sale a year ago.

Tata dispatched 73,894 cars in October, 12,760 more than in October 2025 (61,134). The Sierra and the new Aeris sedan (4,005) account for 11,210 of those extra cars. The Harrier (down 13%), Safari (down 9%), Altroz (down 14%) and Tiago (down 9%) all shipped fewer than a year earlier; the Curvv rose 66% to 2,377.

The Nexon stayed Tata's best-seller at 21,966, down 1%. The Punch, up 22% to 20,525, finished 1,441 behind; in September the gap was 123.

These are factory dispatches to dealers, not registrations.
