## Key facts and sources
1. On 23 Nov RBI issued draft amendment directions, "Preventive Safeguards for Electronic Banking Transactions". Every
   bank must offer a **Stop Switch** that immediately disables all outward debits the customer starts. While it is on,
   no new mandates, no new standing instructions and no UPI registration on new third-party apps are allowed. Incoming
   credits, ongoing mandates and standing instructions, liens, and bank debits (loan instalments, charges) are not
   affected.
   https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/bs_viewcontent.aspx?Id=5221 (¶76FF-76FH) ;
   https://upstox.com/news/business-news/latest-updates/rbi-proposes-stop-switch-to-let-bank-customers-block-all-outgoing-digital-payments/article-203896/ ;
   https://inc42.com/buzz/rbi-proposes-stop-switch-to-block-digital-payment-frauds/
2. **Trusted Person** (voluntary): an adult aged 18 or over approves transfers above a limit the customer sets, within
   **one hour**. No reply means the transfer is rejected. It **excludes UPI, merchant payments and recurring mandates**.
   The trusted person gets no account rights and can't see the balance.
   Draft ¶76FQ-76FZB (above) ;
   https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/rbi-proposes-stop-switch-to-block-digital-banking-debits-instantly-strengthen-protection-against-fraud (IANS)
3. Comments close **14 Dec 2026**, through Connect 2 Regulate or by email to mcsdorfeedback@rbi.org.in.
   https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=64026 ;
   https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/Bs_Connect2Regulate.aspx ("Ends on Dec 14, 2026") ; Inc42 (date only)
4. Proposed start **1 July 2027**, or earlier if a bank chooses. That is 219 days from 24 Nov (idly arithmetic).
   Draft ¶3(2) ; Inc42 ; FPJ/IANS
5. New accounts: all digital payment channels are off by default until the customer opts in.
   Draft ¶76FK ; Inc42 ; Upstox
6. The Stop Switch can be operated through channels "such as branches, ATMs, internet banking, mobile banking, IVR".
   Draft ¶76FM ; Inc42
