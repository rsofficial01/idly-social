Morning Brief, Tuesday 24 November.

Madurai district is under prohibitory orders from 9 pm Monday to 10 am Wednesday on Karthigai Deepam day. Collector Nishant Krishna's order bars assemblies, protests, processions and public meetings, and bars anyone from climbing Thiruparankundram hill. Temple rituals, weddings, funerals, permitted events and steps to carry out the Supreme Court's 20 November order are exempt, DT Next and The Hindu report. Over 5,000 police are deployed.

The Supreme Court allowed a one-time lamp at the disputed hilltop pillar. The Tamil Nadu government's review plea was not listed on Monday. As of Monday night, officials had not said whether the lamp would be lit at the pillar or the traditional deepa mandapam, DT Next and Deccan Herald reported. In 2025 the then DMK government did not implement a High Court order to light it at the pillar.

West Bengal: the Supreme Court told SIR appeal tribunals to hear 22.21 lakh appeals by deleted voters first, 58% of all 38.31 lakh. CM Suvendu Adhikari says a proposal to make "Paschim Banga" the state's English name goes to the Assembly on 9 December.

India announced a ₹15,000 crore package for Nepal after Narendra Modi's talks with Balendra Shah. Air India, IndiGo and Akasa have kept Saudi flights cancelled till 30 November.
