Oppo's Find X10 costs ₹1,39,999 in India with 12GB of memory and 256GB of storage. Last year's Find X9 launched at ₹74,999 with the same memory and storage, so the price is up ₹65,000, or 87%. The top model, the Find X10 Pro Max, is ₹1,99,999; the Find X9 Pro was ₹1,09,999.

Research firm Counterpoint said on 20 November that Oppo's flagships have gone up the most of any brand, by 82-87% in a year. It expects premium flagship prices to rise by more than 30% by the end of 2026 compared with 2025, taking most brands' flagships past ₹1 lakh. Apple's iPhone 18 Pro starts at ₹1,64,900 in India, 22% more than the iPhone 17 Pro's ₹1,34,900 launch price, and Samsung raised Galaxy S26 prices by up to 17% in September.

Counterpoint blames memory chips. It estimates they will make up 43% of a phone's cost by the end of the year, up from 13% in early 2025.
