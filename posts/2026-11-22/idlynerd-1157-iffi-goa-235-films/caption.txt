The 57th International Film Festival of India (IFFI) is on in Goa till 28 November, with 235 films: 159 international titles from 86 countries and 76 Indian films in 18 languages, the organisers say.

Paweł Pawlikowski's Fatherland, which earned him a joint Best Director award at Cannes this year, opened the festival on 20 November. Dhurandhar opens the Indian Panorama, whose 25 feature films include Kantara: A Legend Chapter 1, and Ramayana opens the festival's BRICS cinema segment.

Spain is this year's Country of Honour and Russia the Partner Country. Filmmaker Aanand L. Rai chairs the international competition jury, and Anthony Chen's We Are All Strangers closes the festival on 28 November.
