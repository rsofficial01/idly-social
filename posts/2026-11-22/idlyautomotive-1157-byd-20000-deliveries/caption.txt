BYD India has now delivered 20,000 cars to customers in India, the company said on 20 November. The 20,000th was handed over at PPS BYD in Delhi, double the 10,000 BYD announced in September 2025.

Two SUVs make up most of that: the Atto 3 with more than 9,000 deliveries and the Sealion 7 with more than 5,000. The Seal sedan and the eMAX 7 MPV account for the rest.

The cheapest way into a BYD is the Atto 3 at ₹24.99 lakh ex-showroom; the Sealion 7 starts at ₹41.90 lakh. BYD now sells from 51 showrooms in 40 cities, and its first plug-in hybrid for India, the Seal U, is promised for 2026 with no date or price yet.
