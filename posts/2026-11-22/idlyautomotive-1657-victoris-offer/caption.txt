Maruti Suzuki's Arena dealers are offering benefits of up to ₹1.40 lakh on the Victoris strong hybrid this month, and the offers end on 30 November, according to CarDekho (22 November) and Autocar India (7 November). Autocar's earlier figure of ₹1.51 lakh included a Diwali booking discount of up to ₹11,000 that ended on 11 November.

The ₹1.40 lakh is a ceiling, not a flat price cut. Only the strong-hybrid Victoris gets a cash discount, of up to ₹20,000; the petrol and CNG versions get none. Other listed benefits include an exchange bonus of up to ₹50,000 (or a scrappage bonus of up to ₹45,000, not both), a loyalty bonus of up to ₹30,000 for existing Maruti owners, and up to ₹15,000 extra for trading in a Hyundai i10 or i20, a Tata Tiago or Nexon, or a Mahindra XUV 3XO. CarDekho does not say how the benefits combine to reach ₹1.40 lakh, and offers vary by city and dealer stock.

Separately, Maruti raised prices of select, unnamed models from 22 November, by about 0.31% on average, according to its exchange filing (PTI, Business Today).
