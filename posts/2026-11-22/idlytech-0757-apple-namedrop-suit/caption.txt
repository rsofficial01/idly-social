GG Technologies, the company behind the StayTouch contact-sharing app, has sued Apple in the Bombay High Court. It is seeking ₹1,500 crore in damages and wants Apple's NameDrop feature switched off in India while the case is heard.

NameDrop lets iPhone and Apple Watch users swap contact details by holding their devices close together. Apple introduced it with iOS 17 in June 2023. GG, founded in 2018 by IIT Delhi graduate Gaurav Goel, alleges that NameDrop infringes an Indian patent on proximity-based contact sharing that it was granted in July 2024.

These are GG's claims, and no court has ruled on them. The case has not yet been listed for a hearing, and neither TechCrunch nor Inc42 reported a response from Apple.

GG is also taking on Apple in the US. It sued there in April 2026, and the US International Trade Commission opened an investigation in August after GG's complaint.
