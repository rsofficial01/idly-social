The Hyundai Verna has a 5-star Bharat NCAP crash-test rating for both adults and children, according to Autocar India and MotorBeam (18 November); Hyundai announced it on 20 November. The sedan scored 28.22 of 32 for adult occupant protection and 44 of 49 for child occupant protection.

Bharat NCAP crash-tested two HX8 automatic versions (1.5 turbo-petrol DCT and 1.5 petrol CVT), and the rating applies to every Verna variant. The weak spot: Bharat NCAP rated pelvis protection for the driver and front passenger as marginal in the frontal test, according to Autocar India and CarDekho.

The Verna is the only midsize sedan with a Bharat NCAP rating so far, per Autocar India and MotorBeam. Prices start at ₹10.99 lakh ex-showroom, per Hyundai's price list.
