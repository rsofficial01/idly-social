Mechanised-boat fishermen at Chennai's Kasimedu harbour called off their four-day strike on Sunday after talks with Tamil Nadu Finance Minister N. Marie Wilson, according to UNI and Oneindia Tamil.

While about 800 boats stayed docked, fish prices rose by more than 50% in Chennai, Tiruvallur and Kancheepuram, DT Next and UNI reported.

The fishermen want back eight Kasimedu boats that Andhra Pradesh has held since 17 June despite a High Court order to release them, according to PTI and Daily Thanthi. A contempt plea is listed for 24 November. Wilson said Andhra Pradesh had asked Tamil Nadu to wait until 5 December, citing its local body polls, and the fishermen agreed, PTI reported.

Chief Minister Vijay announced ₹7 lakh each on 5 November for the families of the nine Kasimedu fishermen who died when their boat capsized off Andhra Pradesh in Cyclone Murasu. ₹2 lakh has been paid and the remaining ₹5 lakh is due on 24 November, Wilson said, according to PTI. The families want ₹20 lakh; he said a decision would come "very soon".
