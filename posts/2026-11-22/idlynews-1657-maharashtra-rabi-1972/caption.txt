Maharashtra has so far sown less rabi, the winter crop of wheat, jowar and gram, than it did in the whole of the 1972-73 drought year, state agriculture department records show. Under 15 lakh hectares have been sown, against about 47 lakh hectares in 1972-73 and a normal of about 58 lakh hectares, as reported by Scroll and IANS.

The state has now declared 310 of its 358 talukas drought-hit, adding six on 19 November. Its drought relief order stays recovery of farm loans, restructures crop loans, gives land revenue relief and waives school and college exam fees in those talukas. The June-September monsoon ended 19% below normal statewide, 40% below in Marathwada and 58% below in Solapur, according to India Meteorological Department data.
