Morning Brief, Sunday 22 November.

India declared their first innings at 720 for 9 on day four of the first Test in Wellington, a lead of 352 over New Zealand's 368. It is the highest total ever made in a Test in New Zealand, beating New Zealand's 715 for 6 against Bangladesh in Hamilton in 2019, and India's highest Test total away from home, beating 707 in Colombo in 2010. KL Rahul (136) and Rishabh Pant (128) made hundreds. At about 8 am IST, New Zealand were 28 for 1 in their second innings, 324 behind. The cover is a 2008 file photo of the ground, the Basin Reserve.

The NEET-UG 2026 paper-leak trial runs day to day from 25 November. A fast-track court at Delhi's Rouse Avenue Courts has framed charges against all 14 accused, including under anti-corruption and exam-cheating laws. The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) alleges that subject experts engaged by the National Testing Agency passed questions on for money. The 3 May exam was cancelled on 12 May and held again on 21 June.

In Manipur's Chandel district, at least 16 people have died and about 600 have been admitted to hospital after drinking suspected spurious liquor, police say; Hindustan Times quotes the state excise commissioner putting the toll at 17. Six people have been arrested, and police say the liquor came from neighbouring Kakching district. Lab tests are yet to confirm the cause; the state has set up an inquiry committee.

Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah arrives in India today on his first bilateral visit since taking office in March. The Ministry of Railways says he and Prime Minister Narendra Modi will flag off a weekly Ayodhya-Janakpur Express on Monday, 23 November. The 604 km route has 28 km in Nepal and stops including Sitamarhi, Raxaul, Gorakhpur and Basti; trains leave Janakpur Dham on Saturdays and Ayodhya Cantt on Mondays.
