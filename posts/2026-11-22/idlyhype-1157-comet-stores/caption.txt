Comet, the Bengaluru sneaker label, opened two stores this week: Pacific Mall in Jaipur on 17 November, and The Pavillion in Pune, Comet's first store in the city. It plans 20 stores by the end of FY27; its own store locator now lists 13 in nine cities.

The others are in Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Ahmedabad, and Comet's list adds Chennai and Indore. Broadway, a multi-brand store, also stocks Comet.

The pair on the cover is the Aeres Blizzard, a retro court-style low-top, at ₹5,299 on Comet's site, the same MRP Broadway lists.

Comet's revenue from operations rose 2.6 times to ₹74 crore in FY26, from ₹29 crore in FY25, according to its filings with the Registrar of Companies. Its loss before tax doubled to ₹9 crore, and it spent ₹21.5 crore on ads.
