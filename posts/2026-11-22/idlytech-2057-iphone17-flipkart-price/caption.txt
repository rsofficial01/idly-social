Flipkart is selling the iPhone 17 (256GB) for ₹89,100, its price on the evening of 22 November. That is ₹10,800 below Apple's own ₹99,900, but still ₹6,200 more than the ₹82,900 the phone launched at in India in September 2025. Apple raised the price by ₹17,000 after its iPhone 18 Pro event in September 2026, according to Beebom and Digit; Apple's India store now lists ₹99,900.

With a Flipkart Axis Bank or Flipkart SBI credit card, Flipkart gives ₹4,000 cashback, credited after the return period, which brings it to ₹85,100: still ₹2,200 above the launch price. Paying through Google Pay, PhonePe or CRED UPI takes ₹1,101 off instead.

Flipkart's prices and offers can change at any time.
