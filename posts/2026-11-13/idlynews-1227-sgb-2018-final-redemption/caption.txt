Sovereign Gold Bond 2018-19 Series III, issued on 13 November 2018, is due for final redemption today, Friday 13 November 2026. The Reserve Bank of India has set the redemption price at ₹14,024 a unit (one unit is one gram of gold): the simple average of IBJA's closing prices for 999-purity gold on 10, 11 and 12 November.

The RBI issued this series at ₹3,183 a gram, or ₹3,133 for investors who applied online and paid digitally. By idly's calculation, that is a price gain of about 341%, or about 348% at the online price, before interest. The bonds also paid 2.5% a year on the issue price, about ₹637 a gram over eight years in half-yearly instalments; the RBI says the last instalment comes with the redemption amount, into the bank account the investor registered.

Holders who took the early exit on 13 May 2026 got ₹15,102 a unit, the RBI's price for that date: ₹1,078 a gram more than today's.

Since 1 April 2026, the capital-gains exemption on redemption applies only to individuals who subscribed at the original issue and held the bonds to maturity, Business Today and Outlook Money report; bonds bought on the stock exchange are taxed on the gain.
