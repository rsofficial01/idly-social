BEST has signed up for 1,500 air-conditioned electric midi-buses for Mumbai, in an agreement finalised on Thursday 12 November 2026 under the Centre's PM E-DRIVE scheme, Autocar Professional and the Free Press Journal report.

Only about 225 are due by March 2027; the remaining 1,275 by March 2028. EKA builds 1,050 and Switch the other 450. Operators will run them for BEST on 12-year wet-lease contracts, with each bus guaranteed 64,800 km of running a year.

The 9-metre buses are meant for last-mile trips and Metro feeder routes. Each gets an ultra-low floor with an automatic wheelchair ramp, CCTV, fire detection, USB charging points and at least 200 km of range per charge.
