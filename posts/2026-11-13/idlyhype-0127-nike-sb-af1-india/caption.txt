Nike SB's skate version of the Air Force 1 is coming to India. Superkicks lists the Navy pair (style code HM8517-400) at ₹10,295, inclusive of all taxes, in UK sizes 7 to 11, with a start date of 17 November, the same date Nike launches it in the US.

Nike's US price is $120. At 12 November exchange rates that is about ₹11,280, before US sales tax, so the Superkicks price works out about ₹1,000 lower. The pair has a navy nubuck upper with a black Swoosh, heel tab and sole.
