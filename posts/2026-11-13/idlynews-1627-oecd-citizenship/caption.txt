More than 9.2 lakh Indians became citizens of OECD countries between 2020 and 2024, the OECD's International Migration Outlook 2026 says. That is 41% more than the 6.5 lakh of 2015-19. The OECD is a group of 38 countries, including the US, the UK, Canada and Australia.

Canada was the top destination: about 2.6 lakh Indians became Canadian citizens in 2020-24, up 150% on the previous five years. The top five were Canada, the US, Australia, the UK and New Zealand, and nearly 2.3 lakh Indians got passports from these five in 2024 alone.

Fresh migration is falling, though. The report says migration from India to OECD countries fell 19% in 2024, to under 5 lakh, with India still the top country of origin. Migration from India to the UK halved, to 66,000.

India's own count is a separate measure. The Ministry of External Affairs told the Rajya Sabha in December 2025 that 85,256 people gave up Indian citizenship in 2020, 1,63,370 in 2021, 2,25,620 in 2022, 2,16,219 in 2023 and 2,06,378 in 2024: 8,96,843 in all. Indian missions abroad say the Citizenship Act does not allow dual citizenship, and those who take another nationality must surrender their Indian passports.
