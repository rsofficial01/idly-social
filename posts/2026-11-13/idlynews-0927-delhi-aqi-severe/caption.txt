Delhi's air was in the "severe" band again on Friday morning. The city's average AQI stood at 402 at 5 am, Central Pollution Control Board data show, Outlook India and Open report. The IIT Delhi monitor read 488 (Scroll, Open) and ITO 455 (Outlook India, Open).

GRAP Stage III has applied across Delhi-NCR since 6 November, when the Commission for Air Quality Management invoked it, AIR News and The News Mill (ANI) report. Non-essential construction and demolition are halted, and BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel cars are barred in Delhi. Delhi schools let children up to Class 5 attend online or in person, Business Today and Outlook India report.
