SCS Software's Coaches DLC for Euro Truck Simulator 2 has been out since Thursday, 12 November. It puts you behind the wheel of a passenger coach instead of a truck, with three licensed models: the Scania Touring, the Volvo 9700 DD and the MAN Lion's Coach.

Quick Jobs earn money towards buying and customising your own coach; Contract Jobs are scheduled services with timetables and stops; Tourist Jobs are sightseeing trips to landmarks. SCS says passengers will let you know when the ride gets rough, so smooth corners and gentle braking matter.

On Steam India the DLC costs ₹750 and needs the base game, which is ₹242 (75% off ₹970) until 24 November: ₹992 for both at today's prices. SCS plans to add more coaches to this same DLC rather than sell them as new DLCs.
