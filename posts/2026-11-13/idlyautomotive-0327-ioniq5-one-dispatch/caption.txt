Hyundai dispatched 62,799 cars to its Indian dealers in October 2026, 17% more than the 53,792 of October 2025. Just one of them was an Ioniq 5, against six a year earlier.

The updated Ioniq 5 went on sale on 28 April at ₹55.7 lakh ex-showroom, with an 84 kWh battery and a claimed (ARAI) range of 690 km. Two of Hyundai's smaller SUVs grew fast: the Venue rose 11% to 13,017 and the Exter 35% to 8,509.

These figures count factory dispatches to dealers, not cars handed over to buyers.
