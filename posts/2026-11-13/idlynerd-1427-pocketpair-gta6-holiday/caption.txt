Pocketpair, the Japanese studio behind Palworld, is giving staff who plan to play Grand Theft Auto VI a "special five-day holiday" from Thursday 19 November to Monday 23 November. Its notice on X calls it "an urgent five-day business trip in Vice City". The window includes a weekend and Japan's Labour Thanksgiving Day public holiday on 23 November. CEO Takuro Mizobe says he is taking the break too.

Logitech is also giving every employee one "Logi Game Day" off to play a game of their choice, to use any time in the next 12 months.

In India, GTA VI unlocks at 12:00 am IST on Thursday 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, per PlayStation and Xbox India listings. Rockstar has confirmed the game goes live at midnight local time in each country.
