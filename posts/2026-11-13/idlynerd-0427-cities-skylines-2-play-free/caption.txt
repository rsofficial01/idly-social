Cities: Skylines II, Paradox Interactive's city-builder, is free to play on Steam from Thursday 12 November until Monday 16 November, and Steam's India store lists the offer too.

It is a Windows PC game, and even the free weekend needs a capable machine: at least 8 GB of RAM, an Nvidia GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon RX 480 graphics card, and 60 GB of free disk space.

The free weekend comes ahead of Park Adventures, a paid expansion out on 2 December on Steam and the Microsoft Store. It adds city parks, zoos, nature reserves and amusement park rides, with more than 100 new assets and new maps, and lets you set ticket prices and staff budgets. It is included in Expansion Pass II. The amusement park on the cover is from this expansion.
