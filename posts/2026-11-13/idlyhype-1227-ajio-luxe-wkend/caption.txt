Ajio Luxe Wkend, the luxury fashion and lifestyle fair presented by Ajio Luxe and organised by The Magnanimous Group, opens its sixth edition today at Jio World Garden in Mumbai's Bandra Kurla Complex. It runs until Sunday, 15 November.

Organisers list more than 250 brands across 14 categories: fashion, beauty, watches, jewellery, art, home design and cars, plus a section for homegrown Indian labels, runway shows and live music.

Tickets start at ₹1,000. On Friday, gates open at about 5 pm, per District's listing, and everyone aged six and above needs a ticket.
