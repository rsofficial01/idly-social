The Air Jordan 4 "Bred" is coming back in its original 1989 colourway of black, Fire Red and Cement Grey. Nike's Air Jordan 4 Retro OG "Bred" (style code IX8180-001) launches in the US on 27 November, Black Friday, on its SNKRS app at $230.

Nike says the shape, fit and feel have been "remastered to the original specs", with a nubuck upper. The 2024 "Reimagined" pair was leather. Nike hasn't announced an India date or price yet.
