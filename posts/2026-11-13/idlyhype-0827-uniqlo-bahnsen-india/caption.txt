Uniqlo's second collaboration with designer Cecilie Bahnsen, a fall/winter 2026 fleece collection, goes on sale in India on Fri 27 Nov 2026, online and in four stores, Uniqlo India says: Ambience Mall Vasant Kunj, Ambience Mall Gurgaon, Phoenix Palladium in Mumbai and Phoenix Marketcity in Bengaluru. The fleece jacket, pictured in black, is ₹3,990.

The range has fleece jackets, vests and skirts, knit gloves and fleece hats in black, dark navy, heather grey and pale pink, with small embroidered flowers. A kids' range adds peplum tops and A-line skorts.
