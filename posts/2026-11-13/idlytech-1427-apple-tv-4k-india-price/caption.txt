Apple's new Apple TV 4K (4th generation) goes on sale in India today, 13 November, at ₹39,900. There is only one model: 128GB with Wi-Fi and Ethernet.

The 2022 model launched at ₹16,900 for the 128GB Ethernet version and ₹14,900 for 64GB. In June, Apple raised those to ₹31,900 and ₹25,900, blaming the rising cost of memory and storage. The new box costs ₹8,000 more than the old one's latest price.

What you get: an A19 chip (Apple says up to 50% faster CPU), three far-field microphones for hands-free Siri, Find My on the Siri Remote, and surround sound from four HomePod speakers. Siri AI comes as a beta in English, and Apple says the beta also runs on older Apple TV 4K models (2nd generation or later).
