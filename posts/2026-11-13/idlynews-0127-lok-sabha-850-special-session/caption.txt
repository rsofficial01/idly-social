The Centre is planning a special session of Parliament to pass the Constitution amendment that would enlarge the Lok Sabha from 543 seats to as many as 850, government sources told PTI on Thursday. No dates or duration have been given.

The government links the bigger House to the 2023 law reserving 33% of seats in Parliament and state Assemblies for women, which it wants in force before the 2029 general election. The amendment needs two-thirds of MPs present and voting. A similar bill fell short on 17 April, with 298 votes for and 230 against.

Government sources now claim 15 to 18 votes more than needed, PTI reports; The Hindu reports their claim as at least 15-16. The seat formula being studied would raise every state's Lok Sabha seats by 50% and keep each state's share as fixed by the 1971 Census, to ease concerns raised by southern states. The sources say no final decision has been taken.

Congress's Jairam Ramesh called Home Minister Amit Shah "the master of bluff and bluster" and said the bill would fail again.

Parliament can be recalled at short notice because the Monsoon Session, adjourned on 13 August, has not been prorogued, now a record 91 days, PTI says. Extending that session needs two days' notice; a fresh session needs 14.
