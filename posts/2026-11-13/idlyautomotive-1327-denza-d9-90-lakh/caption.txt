Denza, BYD Group's luxury brand, launched the D9 electric MPV in India on Friday, 13 November, at ₹90 lakh ex-showroom, according to Denza India and Autocar India. It comes in one fully loaded front-wheel-drive version.

The seven-seat D9 (2+2+3) has a 115.07 kWh Blade battery, a range of 730 km claimed (NEDC), nine airbags and a claimed 0-100 km/h time of 8.5 seconds. At that price it costs about ₹10 lakh more than the MG M9 electric MPV (from ₹79.94 lakh).

Denza also launched the Z9 GT shooting brake at ₹1.57 crore ex-showroom. Bookings are open, and both cars will be sold from Denza's own showrooms, separate from BYD's, in Gurugram, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad.
