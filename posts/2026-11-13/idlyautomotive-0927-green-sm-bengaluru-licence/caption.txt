Green SM, the electric ride-hailing arm of Vietnam's Vingroup, has a Karnataka Transport Department aggregator licence that runs until September 2031, clearing it to start app-booked cabs in Bengaluru.

Green SM owns its cars, VinFast's seven-seat Limo Green electric MPVs, and hires its own drivers, the model BluSmart ran before it stopped. It has run in Delhi-NCR since June 2026.

Two driver unions, Karnataka Rajya Chalakara Parishath and Peace Auto Union, oppose the licence. They fear cheaper rides will cut local drivers' earnings, and they question whether the cabs were checked for the fare meters, display boards and GPS the state's rules require. The unions say Green SM cars are already parked in Kengeri and Electronics City.

Bengaluru fares are not announced yet. Reports say Green SM charges about ₹8 a km in other Indian cities.
