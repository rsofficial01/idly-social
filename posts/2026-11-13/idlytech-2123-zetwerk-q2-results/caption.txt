Zetwerk, the Bengaluru-based manufacturing company that listed on the stock market on 23 October, has reported its first quarterly results since listing. For July-September 2026, revenue from operations was ₹4,599 crore, up 18% from a year earlier and 12% from April-June.

Its net loss was ₹88 crore, 43% more than the ₹61.6 crore of a year ago but about 88% less than the ₹733 crore of April-June. Leaving out share-based payment costs, Zetwerk says it made a pre-tax profit of ₹28 crore. Its manufacturing order book is at a record ₹14,595 crore, up 67% in a year, and the company says orders usually turn into revenue over about two years.

The shares listed on the NSE at ₹159.25 against an IPO price of ₹151.
