Bethesda and id Software add Slaughter Mode to DOOM: The Dark Ages on Monday, 16 November: an endless mode of escalating, randomised rounds of demons, bosses and arenas, with weekly challenges. It is a free update, but only for owners of the Revelations DLC (₹1,399 on Steam India), the Premium Edition or the Premium Upgrade.

Clearing rounds earns Skulls, which unlock Slayer skins found only in this mode, weapon skins for every mode, profile icons and tracks from Andrew Hulshult's IDKFA soundtrack. At each Round Checkpoint you either secure your Skulls or choose Risk It for a bigger multiplier, and a death then costs you every unsecured Skull.

Steam India prices (13 November): base game ₹3,499, Premium Edition (includes the mode) ₹4,899.
