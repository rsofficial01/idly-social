Birkenstock and Etro's second collaboration went on sale worldwide on 12 November on etro.com, 1774.com and at selected stores, Birkenstock says: a shearling-lined Boston clog ($630) and the pair's first Arizona sandal ($590), at Birkenstock's US prices. Neither is listed on Birkenstock's India store, birkenstock.in, yet (checked 1:59 pm IST, 13 November).

birkenstock.in does list the first Etro release from September. It prices the embroidered Etro Boston at ₹50,990 including GST (Taupe in stock only in EU 36, Chocolate sold out, when checked) and the fringed Gizeh at ₹54,490. Birkenstock's US site lists the same Boston at $690 before sales tax, about ₹64,900 at ₹94.04 to the dollar (ECB rate, 12 November). By idly's maths, India's listed price is about ₹13,900 lower.
