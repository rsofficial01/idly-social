The first 7.5 km of Bengaluru Metro's Pink Line, an elevated stretch along Bannerghatta Road from Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere, opens to passengers at 6 am on Saturday, 14 November, Karnataka minister Krishna Byre Gowda says. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to inaugurate the stretch today, Friday 13 November.

Six stations open: Kalena Agrahara, Hulimavu, IIMB, JP Nagar 4th Phase, Jayadeva Hospital and Tavarekere. At Jayadeva Hospital, riders can change to the Yellow Line towards Electronic City.

Four trains will run at first with one on standby, about 8 minutes apart at peak hours, according to reports. BMRCL has not announced fares yet.

The elevated stretch cost about ₹3,470 crore (PTI, ANI) and takes Bengaluru's operational metro network from 96.1 km to about 103.6 km. The rest of the 21.26 km line, 12 underground stations up to Nagawara, is still under construction.
