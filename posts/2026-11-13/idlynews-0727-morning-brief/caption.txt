Morning Brief, Friday 13 November.

Diesel went up by only ₹7 a litre when a rise of at least ₹50 was needed, and the Centre bore the other ₹43 itself, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said at the Kisan Tak Bharat Agriculture Conference in New Delhi on Thursday. He said crude jumped from about $70 to $122 a barrel within weeks during the West Asia conflict, and that holding diesel down costs about ₹14,000 crore a month.

Climate activist Sonam Wangchuk said on Thursday that he is under house arrest in Leh, with armed guards outside his home; police say he was advised to stay at home under prohibitory orders in force for the election period. Police removed him from a protest site in Leh on Wednesday. He opposes holding elections to Ladakh's hill councils, due on 4 December, in midwinter.

AIMIM chief Asaduddin Owaisi and Aazad Samaj Party (Kanshi Ram) president Chandra Shekhar Aazad announced a new alliance, the Samajik Parivartan Morcha, in Lucknow on Thursday and named Aazad its chief ministerial candidate for Uttar Pradesh's 2027 Assembly election. They said the alliance is open to the Samajwadi Party and the Congress joining.

The two Special Police Officers who went missing on patrol in Jammu and Kashmir's Kishtwar district on Wednesday were found dead on Thursday with bullet wounds, and their service weapons were reportedly missing, PTI and Daily Excelsior report.

Russian President Vladimir Putin met US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner at the Kremlin for more than four hours on Thursday. He said "there is only one line of demarcation: the state border", called any foreign peacekeepers in Ukraine a "legitimate target", and proposed a humanitarian mission to Kostiantynivka, AFP and TRT World report. In Dublin, Kushner said "we cannot conclude this deal without Europeans being at the table"; the Kremlin has not confirmed that Europe will join the next round.
