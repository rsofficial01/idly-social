Pokémon Legends: Z-A players can claim a free Shiny Xerneas until Thursday 7 January 2027, The Pokémon Company says. From the main menu, pick Link Play, then Mystery Gift, then Get with Code/Password, enter CELEBRATE30TH and save your game. Serebii lists it at Level 100 with Geomancy, Moonblast, Focus Blast and Thunderbolt, and lists the code as global, so it works for players in India too.

Guides from VGC and All Things How say the Xerneas you meet in the story can't be shiny. The same password unlocks a furniture collection in Pokémon Pokopia, and a Shiny Yveltal follows in Z-A from 10 December; its code isn't out yet.
