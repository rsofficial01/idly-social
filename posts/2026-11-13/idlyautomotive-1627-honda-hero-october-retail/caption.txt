Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India sold more two-wheelers at retail than Hero MotoCorp in October 2026: 6,37,630 against 5,98,433, a gap of 39,197, according to dealer body FADA's monthly retail data. In October 2025 Hero was ahead by about 1.56 lakh.

These are retail sales to buyers, not factory dispatches to dealers.

Hero's retail fell 40.56% on the year and Honda's 25.06%. Shares: Honda 27.47%, Hero 25.78%, TVS 19.74%, Bajaj 9.03%, Royal Enfield 5.34%, Suzuki 5.21%.

All two-wheeler retail fell 28.35% to 23,21,419. FADA puts it down to the festive calendar: Dhanteras and Diwali fell in November this year, while October 2025 had both Navratri and Diwali. It still calls this the second-best October on record and says November's Diwali numbers will give the real verdict on demand.
