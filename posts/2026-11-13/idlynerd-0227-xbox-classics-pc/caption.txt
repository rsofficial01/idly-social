Microsoft added 21 more original Xbox games to Xbox Backward Compatibility on PC on Thursday 12 November, taking the library to 25 games for Xbox's 25th anniversary. The original Xbox versions of Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic I and II, Star Wars Battlefront I and II, Jedi Academy, Republic Commando, TimeSplitters 2 and Morrowind are among them, and they run on Windows 11 PCs in select markets, Xbox says.

On Microsoft Store India on 13 November, KOTOR was listed at ₹499, TimeSplitters 2 at ₹549 and Morrowind at ₹699. Morrowind and Grabbed by the Ghoulies are also in Game Pass, and Xbox says a digital licence you already own on console carries over to PC. Xbox lists a GTX 950-class GPU and 8 GB RAM as the minimum, and KOTOR, KOTOR II, BLiNX and Conker now have achievements.
