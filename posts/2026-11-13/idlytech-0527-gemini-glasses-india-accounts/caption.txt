Google and Samsung opened preorders on Thursday, 12 November, for their first Gemini glasses, made with the eyewear brands Warby Parker and Gentle Monster. They go on sale on 10 December in the US and South Korea only. Google's own fine print says the glasses work only with Google accounts from those two countries, for users aged 18 and over, so a pair bought in the US can't be set up on an Indian account.

Warby Parker's three frames start at $395; Gentle Monster's six cost $449 to $489. Each pair weighs about 41 g, runs up to 9 hours on a charge and has a 12 MP camera. Google and Samsung say bystanders' faces are blurred before Gemini processes what the camera sees. The glasses pair with Pixel 7 and later, Galaxy S and Z phones on One UI 9, and iPhone 13 Pro and above.

Neither company has named India among its launch markets. Meta's Ray-Ban Gen 3 glasses are already on sale here, from ₹44,300.
