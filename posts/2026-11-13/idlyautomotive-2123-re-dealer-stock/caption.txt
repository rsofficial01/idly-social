Royal Enfield dealers are holding only about 7-8 days of motorcycles, including bikes in transit, against a normal three to four weeks, Eicher Motors MD and Royal Enfield CEO B. Govindarajan said on the company's July-September earnings call on Friday, 13 November.

Bookings are up about 20-21% so far this financial year, some models have about two months of bookings waiting, and Royal Enfield says it will fill dealer stock over the next two months. Its Cheyyar plant can now build 16.5 lakh bikes a year; the company plans to take that to 18 lakh by March 2027 by bringing forward a second module, with output possibly from January, and to 20 lakh before the 2027 festive season.
