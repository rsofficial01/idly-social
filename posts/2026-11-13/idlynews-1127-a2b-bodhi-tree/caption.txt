Bodhi Tree Systems, the investment platform backed by James Murdoch's Lupa Systems and Uday Shankar, has agreed to buy a majority stake in Chennai's Adyar Ananda Bhavan (A2B) for $180 million, or over ₹1,600 crore. The deal was announced on Thursday 12 November. The size of the stake and a closing date have not been disclosed.

A2B started as a sweet shop in Washermanpet, Chennai, nearly 50 years ago. It now runs restaurants, sweet shops and a packaged-food business, with about 160 outlets in India and more than 30 abroad, in Singapore, the US, the UK, Australia and Malaysia, and over 15,000 employees.

Uday Shankar will chair A2B's newly formed board. According to the announcement, the plan includes expansion into western India and Europe, modernising A2B's factories in Chennai and Bengaluru, and preparing the company for a possible stock market listing, for which no date has been given. It is Bodhi Tree's third investment, after ALLEN Career Institute and JioStar.
