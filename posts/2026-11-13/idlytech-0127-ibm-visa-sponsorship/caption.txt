IBM is ending US work visa and green card sponsorship for most roles in IBM Consulting, its largest division, which employs more than 150,000 people. A company spokesperson told Reuters that IBM's use of high-skilled visa programmes had been declining for years, and that the decision came as skill availability and client needs changed.

Affected employees can keep working for IBM outside the US or leave the company by the end of 2026, according to an internal IBM document seen by Bloomberg. IBM hasn't said how many people this affects.

Why it matters in India: of the H-1B petitions the US approved in FY2023, 72% were for workers born in India, USCIS data show. IBM had 1,611 H-1B petitions approved in fiscal 2026, Al Jazeera reports, citing USCIS data. In October, the US suspended Tata Consultancy Services, Infosys, Cognizant, Microsoft and Adobe from the labour certification process that leads to employer-sponsored green cards.
