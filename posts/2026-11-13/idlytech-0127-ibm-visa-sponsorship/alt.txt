An older Wikimedia Commons photo (uploaded in 2007) of a metal sign on a stone base at IBM's headquarters in Armonk, New York, reading "IBM, Corporate Headquarters, Employee Entrance", under the headline "IBM Consulting Ends Most US Visa and Green Card Sponsorship". The next card lists the two options for affected IBM Consulting staff that Bloomberg reports from an internal IBM document: keep working for IBM outside the US, or leave by the end of 2026; IBM hasn't said how many people are affected. The last card shows that 72% of US H-1B approvals in FY2023 were for workers born in India, that IBM had 1,611 H-1B approvals in fiscal 2026, and that the US suspended TCS and Infosys from the green card process in October.
