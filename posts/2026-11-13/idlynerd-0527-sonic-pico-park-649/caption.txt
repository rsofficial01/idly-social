SONIC PICO PARK, a co-op puzzle game that brings Sonic the Hedgehog and his friends into the indie series PICO PARK, came out on Thursday, 12 November. It costs ₹649 on Steam in India.

Two to eight players work together to reach the goal in each stage, on one shared screen or online. It is out on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Switch 2, Switch and PC, online play supports cross-play, and up to seven players on one PC can join an online game. Every character gets Sonic's Spin Dash, and the stages add rings, loops and springs.

On PC there is a cheaper way in: Steam's Remote Play Together lets the one player who owns the game stream its local co-op to friends who don't. Valve says it supports up to four players, or more on fast connections. It is a Steam feature, so it doesn't work on consoles.

The game is made by TECOPARK and published by PICO PARK Inc., in a collaboration with SEGA.
