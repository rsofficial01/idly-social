Onitsuka Tiger India has launched a winter Fur Series, putting faux fur on three signature styles: the laceless Mexico 66 Paraty, the Mexico 66 TGRS slip-on with an instep strap, and the high-cut HMR Peak, based on the brand's 1966 'Himarayan Peak' trekking shoe.

The furry Paraty is listed at ₹11,500 on Onitsuka Tiger's India site, in Birch/Cream and Black/Cream only; the HMR Peak is listed at ₹22,000 in Cream and Ginger Peach (checked 4:58 pm IST, 13 November). Onitsuka's UK site lists the same Paraty at £120, about ₹14,960 at 12 November's ECB rate. Both are list prices including tax, so by idly's maths India's is about ₹3,460 lower.
