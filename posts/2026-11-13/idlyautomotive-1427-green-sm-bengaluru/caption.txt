Green SM, the electric cab company of Vietnam's Vingroup (the group that also owns VinFast), has a Karnataka aggregator licence to run app-based cabs, valid until September 2031, transport department officials told Moneycontrol. MotorBeam and Lapaas Voice report it was issued under the state's 2016 aggregator rules and clears the way for Green SM to enter Bengaluru.

Unlike Uber, Rapido and Namma Yatri, which connect independent drivers with riders, Green SM owns its fleet and employs its own drivers. Its Indian fleet includes the VinFast Limo Green, a seven-seat electric MPV. It started in Delhi-NCR in June 2026, then Hyderabad. No Bengaluru fares or start date have been announced.

For comparison, Karnataka's 2024 app-cab fare rules tie the fare to the car's price: ₹100 for the first 4 km and ₹24 a km after that for cars up to ₹10 lakh, and ₹130 for the first 4 km and ₹32 a km for cars above ₹15 lakh. Surge pricing is not allowed.

Two taxi unions, Karnataka Rajya Chalakara Parishath and Peace Auto Union, have asked the state to review the licence and question whether the cabs have cleared mandatory inspections. The company was unavailable for comment, Moneycontrol reported.
