Adyar Ananda Bhavan (A2B), the Chennai-born sweets and vegetarian restaurant chain, is selling a majority stake to Bodhi Tree Systems for $180 million, in a deal announced on Thursday, 12 November 2026. The size of the stake was not disclosed.

Bodhi Tree is the investment platform set up by James Murdoch's Lupa Systems and Uday Shankar, who will chair A2B's newly formed board. A2B runs about 160 outlets in India, with its strongest presence in Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, and more than 30 abroad, in Singapore, the US, Australia, Malaysia and the UK.

The new owners plan to expand A2B in India and overseas, invest in technology and management, and work towards a stock market listing; no timeline has been given. Co-founder K T Srinivasa Raja said the business began as a sweet shop he and his brother started 50 years ago.
