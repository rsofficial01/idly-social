Kia India's first CNG models, the Carens CNG and the Carens Clavis CNG, have crossed 6,200 wholesale units in the two months since their September 2026 launch, the company said on 19 November. That figure counts cars dispatched to dealers, not registrations.

The Carens CNG costs ₹12.12 lakh (ex-showroom) and comes only in the Premium (O) trim. The Carens Clavis CNG starts at ₹12.39 lakh in the HTE and HTE (O) trims. Both pair a 1.5-litre petrol engine and six-speed manual with a factory-fitted 60-litre CNG cylinder. Kia's Assured Buyback offers up to 75% of the car's value after three years, subject to mileage limits and conditions.
