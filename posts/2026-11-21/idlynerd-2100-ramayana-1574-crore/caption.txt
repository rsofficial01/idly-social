Ramayana has grossed ₹1,573.95 crore worldwide in its first 16 days in cinemas, box-office tracker Sacnilk estimates: ₹1,050.95 crore gross in India and ₹523 crore overseas. That makes it the fifth-highest-grossing Indian film ever. Pushpa 2, at ₹1,742 crore by Sacnilk's count, is the next mark above it.

In India, Sacnilk counts about ₹880 crore net up to Friday, 20 November, and expects Ramayana to pass ₹900 crore net on Saturday, its 17th day. Only three Indian films have got there before, all sequels: Baahubali 2, Pushpa 2 and Dhurandhar 2. Pushpa 2 (11 days) and Dhurandhar 2 (14 days) did it faster. Other trackers count lower, and the makers haven't confirmed a figure.

Nitesh Tiwari's film stars Ranbir Kapoor as Rama and Yash as Ravana. The second film, Ramayana: Part Two, is now set for Diwali 2028, a year later than first planned.
