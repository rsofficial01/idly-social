Dell has launched the XPS 16 Creator Edition in India, its first XPS laptop built on Nvidia's RTX Spark chip. Prices start at ₹4,49,990, taxes included, for the base model with 32GB of memory and a 512GB SSD.

The laptop went on sale on Dell's online store on 20 November. Dell says its Exclusive Stores, large-format retailers and online retail partners will stock it by mid-December. Launch offers include up to ₹15,000 cashback with Axis Bank and ICICI Bank cards.

The 16-inch XPS has a 3.2K OLED screen that runs at up to 120Hz, a 99Wh battery and up to 128GB of memory shared by the processor and graphics. It is 17.8mm thick and has an SD card slot and an HDMI port, for people who edit photos and video.
