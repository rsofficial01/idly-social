D Gukesh begins the first defence of his world chess title on Tuesday, 24 November, at 7:30 PM IST. His challenger is Uzbekistan's Javokhir Sindarov, who in April won the Candidates, the tournament that decides who plays the champion.

The match is at the Fondation Martin Bodmer, a library and museum in Cologny, just outside Geneva, and opens with a ceremony on Sunday. Up to 14 classical games are scheduled, with rest days on 27 November and 1, 5 and 9 December. The first player to 7.5 points is champion. The last scheduled game is on 11 December; at 7-7, faster tiebreak games decide it on 12 December.

FIDE, the world chess body, has set a $2.5 million prize fund, with $200,000 to a player for each game won. Sindarov leads their classical head-to-head with two wins and a draw. Gukesh won the title in Singapore in December 2024.
