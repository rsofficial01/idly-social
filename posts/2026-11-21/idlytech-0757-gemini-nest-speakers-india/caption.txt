Google will switch off Google Assistant on its Google Home and Nest speakers and smart displays on 2 August 2027, the company said on 20 November. Its new voice assistant, Gemini for Home, takes over.

India is in the next wave: Google's help page lists early access to Gemini for Home in India as "coming in December". Basic features, including smart home controls, music, alarms, timers, lists and reminders, are free. Gemini Live needs a paid Google Home Premium plan. Google says a home that switches to Gemini can't go back to Google Assistant.

Google's early-access language list includes English but no Hindi or other Indian language yet; Google says other languages will follow "over time". The Nest Mini, Nest Audio and Nest Hub get the full Gemini, while the original Google Home gets most features but not Gemini Live. Separately, the Nest app closes on 25 January 2027, and Nest devices move to the Google Home app.
