Hero Splendor+ prices have gone up. The base Drum variant now costs ₹78,912 ex-showroom Delhi, ₹1,135 more than its earlier ₹77,777, according to the new price list reported by Autocar India and BikeDekho. Hero hasn't announced the change.

The i3S and the 125 Million Edition also rise by ₹1,135, to ₹80,045 and ₹81,666. The Special Edition rises the most, by ₹3,086 to ₹81,666, and now also comes in Candy Blazing Red and Matt Vernier Grey.
