GTA VI's day-one retail sales reached $3.79 billion worldwide, Take-Two Interactive said on 20 November. The figure is the publisher's own estimate through the end of launch day, Thursday 19 November, and Take-Two calls it a record for entertainment in any medium.

At ₹93.44 to the dollar (the rupee's 18 November close), $3.79 billion is over ₹35,000 crore. That is about 2.6 times what Indian cinemas grossed in all of 2025, a record year of ₹13,395 crore by Ormax Media's count.

Take-Two also says GTA VI sold 32.5 million units before launch, a record across all platforms, and scored 99 on Metacritic. Its count is well above market data firm Sensor Tower's earlier estimate of $2.36 billion. On PlayStation Store India, GTA VI costs ₹5,999 and the Ultimate Edition ₹7,499.
