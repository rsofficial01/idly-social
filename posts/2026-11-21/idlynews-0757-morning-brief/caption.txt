Morning Brief, Saturday 21 November.

Nitish Setu, the six-lane Ganga bridge between Kachchi Dargah in Patna district and Bidupur in Vaishali, opens today. The Prime Minister's Office (PMO) says PM Narendra Modi will visit it at about 1:15 pm and dedicate it to the nation. The PMO puts its cost at about ₹4,280 crore, with a 9.7 km main bridge plus 10 km of approach roads, and says it will ease traffic on Mahatma Gandhi Setu and JP Setu. Bihar's government named it after former chief minister Nitish Kumar; the Rashtriya Janata Dal (RJD) objects to naming it after a living leader. The cover shows the bridge crossing the Ganga and Raghopur Diara island on 17 November, seen by the Sentinel-2 satellite.

Eastern Railway said on Friday, citing a Railway Board advisory, that playing music or videos on speaker mode or making loud calls on trains and at stations may draw action under Section 145 of the Railways Act. As amended this year, the section sets a ₹1,000 fine for causing a nuisance; carrying on after being told to stop can mean up to six months' jail, a fine of up to ₹5,000, or both. Southern Railway issued a similar warning in October.

In Rajasthan's panchayat elections, the Congress won more Panchayat Samiti (block council) seats than the ruling BJP in 33 of the 37 districts counted, State Election Commission figures reported late on Friday show. The BJP led in Ajmer, Balotra, Salumber and Udaipur.

Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah arrives on Sunday on his first India visit since taking office in March. The Ministry of External Affairs says he will hold talks with Prime Minister Modi on 23 November.

In the first Test in Wellington, KL Rahul made his first Test hundred in New Zealand on Day 3 as India replied to New Zealand's 368. Devdutt Padikkal was out for 80.
