Over 1 crore 14-year-old girls have received a free HPV vaccine dose in under nine months of the national campaign, the Union Health Ministry said on Saturday. That is nearly 87% of the year's target of about 1.2 crore girls. The campaign, launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in February, aims to prevent cervical cancer.

Who can get it: girls aged 14, with a parent's consent. It is a single dose, free and voluntary, given at designated government health facilities. Parents can book a slot and download the vaccination certificate on the U-WIN portal.

Six states have covered 100% of their target: Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Mizoram, Madhya Pradesh and Bihar. Kerala is at 77.02% and Tamil Nadu at 76.12%, the ministry's figures show.
