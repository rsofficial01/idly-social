The Week in Hype, Saturday 21 November: this week's drops you can actually buy in India, with prices.

Out today: the Air Jordan 3 'Not Nice List' (IF4396-001). Superkicks lists it at ₹19,295 in UK sizes 7 to 11. Nike's US launch is also today, at $215: about ₹20,090 at ₹93.44 to the dollar (the rupee's 18 November close), before US sales tax. Pull the Nike Air heel tab down and it reads 'Not Nice'.

Also new at Superkicks for the holidays: the kids' Jordan 3 'Santa', a red upper with a gold Jumpman on the heel, at ₹5,695 in toddler sizes and ₹6,695 in little kids' sizes. Nike releases the kids' pairs in the US today too.

adidas Originals' Tokyo Ballerina, made with the label Fruition, is ₹9,999 at both Superkicks and VegNonVeg, in two colourways. Superkicks lists women's UK sizes 3 to 7.

Nike's Air Force 1 Low Retro Camo arrived at Superkicks this week at ₹11,995, in two colourways and UK sizes 6 to 12.

Complex's biggest drops this week, One Block Down x Nike Air Max 95 and Corteiz x Nike Shox R4, aren't listed at Superkicks or VegNonVeg.
