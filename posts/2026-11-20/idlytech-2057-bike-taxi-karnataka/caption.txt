The Supreme Court on Friday (20 November) dismissed the Karnataka government's appeal against the Karnataka High Court's 23 January 2026 ruling that lifted the state's bike taxi ban. Appeals by five auto and taxi driver associations were dismissed too, so bike taxis on Rapido, Uber and Ola can keep running in Karnataka.

Under the High Court ruling, motorcycle owners can apply to register their bikes as transport vehicles and run them as contract carriages. Regional transport authorities, which decide the permits, can set conditions under Section 74(2) of the Motor Vehicles Act, but can't refuse a permit just because the vehicle is a two-wheeler.

Karnataka had raised passenger safety and insurance concerns. Justice Satish Chandra Sharma, who heard the case with Justice N Kotiswar Singh, said it is better to regulate bike taxis than to ban them, and that they already run in 25 states.
