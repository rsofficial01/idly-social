Sony has launched two wireless headphones in India: the WH-CH530 at ₹4,490 and the WH-CH730N at ₹12,990, Gadgets 360 and FoneArena report.

The WH-CH530 is an on-ear pair without noise cancelling. It replaces the WH-CH520, which also launched in India at ₹4,490, in April 2023. Sony rates the new pair's battery at up to 55 hours, up from 50, and says a 3-minute charge gives 1.5 hours of playback. It comes in six colours, including new Cobalt, Coral and Mint.

The over-ear WH-CH730N has noise cancelling and replaces the WH-CH720N, which launched in India at ₹9,990 in March 2023, so the new model costs ₹3,000 more. Its battery lasts up to 50 hours with noise cancelling on, against 35 hours on the older pair, and up to 75 hours with it off. It now folds, and comes in five colours.
