BMW Motorrad has launched the R 12 G/S in India at an introductory ₹25.40 lakh, ex-showroom. That is ₹5.50 lakh more than its sister model, the R 12, which uses the same 1,170cc boxer and launched in India at ₹19.90 lakh in July 2024. The G/S comes in a single Pro variant, is imported fully built, and deliveries have already begun.

Its boxer twin is rated at 109 hp and 115 Nm. Two packages are standard: Enduro (off-road tyres, engine guard, handlebar risers) and Comfort (quickshifter, cruise control, heated grips). The warranty runs three years and can be extended to five.
