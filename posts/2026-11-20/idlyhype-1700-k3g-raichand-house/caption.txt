Airbnb has rebuilt the Raichand House from Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham in Mumbai for the film's 25th year, and the visit is priced at ₹0.

Booking requests open on Monday, 23 November, at 12 PM IST at airbnb.com/k3g. The two-hour experience runs from 1 to 6 December for 180 guests in total; Airbnb says each spot covers one guest plus one companion. Guests pay for their own travel to Mumbai.

Inside: the grand staircase, the family study and Poo's closet, with an audio tour of the film's best-known scenes. Airbnb says it is not a contest: you need an active Airbnb profile with a history of positive reviews, and a government ID. The film's stars won't be there in person, Airbnb says.
