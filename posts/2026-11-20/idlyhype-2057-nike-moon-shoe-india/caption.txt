Nike's Moon Shoe OG, a retro of its 1972 racing flat, is on sale in India for ₹8,995. The Black/Sail pair costs ₹8,995 at both Superkicks and VegNonVeg. Superkicks has three colourways, all at ₹8,995; VegNonVeg lists 12, three of them at ₹10,995. On Nike's US site, the Moon Shoe OG is $105.

Nike co-founder Bill Bowerman designed the original for runners at the 1972 Olympic trials, and only about 12 pairs were made. Sotheby's says the idea for its waffle sole came from tinkering with his wife's waffle iron. In July 2019, the only pair known to exist unworn sold at Sotheby's for $437,500, a sneaker auction record at the time.
