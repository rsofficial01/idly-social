The Air Jordan 4 'Bred' now has an Indian price: ₹19,995, the same MRP (taxes included) at Superkicks and VegNonVeg.

VegNonVeg is taking early reservations now; its page says all orders will be shipped from 27 November onwards. Superkicks lists the pair as Coming Soon, with 27 November on its launch calendar.

In the US, Nike's SNKRS app releases it on 27 November, Black Friday, at $230. Nike says the pair returns in the original 1989 'Bred' colourway, with Nike Air on the heel. The style code on Nike's and VegNonVeg's listings is IX8180-001.
