Avengers: Doomsday has sold about 5.5 lakh tickets in India for its first four days of shows, 17 to 20 December, trade trackers Sacnilk and Koimoi report. That count leaves out seats cinemas have blocked but not sold. The film opens here on Thursday, 17 December, with shows from 12:01 AM, a day before its US release.

Those tickets are worth about ₹35 crore gross, or ₹29.6 crore net. Around 2.4 lakh of them are for opening day alone, at an average of about ₹690 a ticket, and English shows bring in most of the money. The film also plays in Hindi, Tamil and Telugu. These are advance figures as of Friday evening, 20 November, and will keep changing.
