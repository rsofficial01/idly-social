Malaysia will require Indian passport holders to get a visa before travelling from 1 January 2027, ending the visa-free entry it has allowed since December 2023. Malaysia's Immigration Department announced the change in a notice dated 17 November.

Applications open on the department's MyVISA online portal from 1 December 2026. Until 31 December, Indians can still enter visa-free for social visits of up to 30 days. The department says visa processing fees are not refunded, whether an application is approved or rejected, or if the policy or travel plans change.

In September, the High Commission of India in Kuala Lumpur had warned Indians against taking up jobs while on visa-free tourist entry.
