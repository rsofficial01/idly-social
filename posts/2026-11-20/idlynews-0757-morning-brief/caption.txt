Morning Brief, Friday 20 November.

Cyclone Akvan has weakened to a cyclonic storm and is crossing the north Andhra Pradesh-south Odisha coast near Gopalpur this morning, with winds of 70-80 kmph gusting to 90, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) says. IMD expects landfall to be complete by about 11 am. Odisha says it moved nearly 3 lakh people out of vulnerable areas. East Coast Railway says it has cancelled about 45 trains from Thursday to Saturday; South Eastern Railway cancelled at least 28 on Friday, mostly to or through Puri. The cover shows Akvan on 19 November, seen by NASA's Terra satellite.

The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) board on Thursday approved a voluntary plan letting retiring members draw their provident fund (PF) in monthly, quarterly or half-yearly instalments while the rest keeps earning. It also cut the wait for a final withdrawal after leaving a job from 12 months to two, and the minimum balance members must keep from 25% to 10%. The changes apply once the Labour Ministry notifies them.

Indians will need a visa to enter Malaysia from 1 January 2027, Malaysia's Immigration Department says. Applications open on 1 December on MyVISA, its online visa portal, and fees are non-refundable. Malaysia's High Commission told the Times of India the 30-day visa-free entry "tentatively" ends on 31 December.

Madhya Pradesh's Cabinet on Wednesday scrapped a 25-year-old rule that barred people with more than two children from state government jobs and allowed action against employees with a third child, citing falling birth rates. Past dismissals won't be reopened.

In the first Test in Wellington, India bowled New Zealand out for 368 on Day 2. Henry Nicholls made 175 not out and Glenn Phillips 118 after New Zealand were 70 for 5; Jasprit Bumrah took four wickets and Akash Deep three.
