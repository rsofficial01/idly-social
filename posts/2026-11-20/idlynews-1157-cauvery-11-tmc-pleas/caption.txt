The Supreme Court on Friday dismissed two Tamil Nadu applications seeking a direction to Karnataka to release about 11 TMC (thousand million cubic feet) of Cauvery water, measured at Biligundlu on the inter-state border. The bench of Justices Vikram Nath and Aparesh Kumar Singh said a third Tamil Nadu application, to place more documents on record, would also be dismissed. The court's reasons have not been reported.

Tamil Nadu says the shortfall built up to early October, and it had asked for the water at 1.3 TMC a day over 10 days. Karnataka told the court 2026 is a rain-deficit year with several areas declared drought-hit, and says it has complied with the Cauvery Water Management Authority's orders.

With the pleas dismissed, there is no court order for the extra water. Under the Supreme Court's 2018 verdict, Karnataka must release 177.25 TMC a year at Biligundlu in a normal year; in a deficit year, shares are cut pro rata, and the Cauvery Water Regulation Committee and Management Authority order the releases.
