GTA VI reached about 24.6 million copies and $2.36 billion by the end of its launch day, Thursday 19 November, market data firm Sensor Tower estimates. The count covers digital copies on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, pre-orders included. About three in four are on PS5.

That is nearly three times GTA V's first day in 2013, when Take-Two counted more than $800 million in retail sell-through. GTA V needed three days to pass $1 billion.

Take-Two hasn't released official GTA VI sales yet. CEO Strauss Zelnick says the company will share them soon. In India, the PS Store lists the Standard Edition at ₹5,999 and the Ultimate Edition at ₹7,499.
