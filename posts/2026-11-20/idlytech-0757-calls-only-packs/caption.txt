All four mobile operators now sell prepaid packs with unlimited calls and SMS but no mobile data. For 28 days, BSNL's ₹172 pack is the cheapest, against ₹177 at Jio and ₹185 at Airtel and Vi, according to Jio's own plan list and reports by Beebom, The Hans India and TelecomTalk.

That works out to about ₹6.14 a day on BSNL, ₹6.32 on Jio and ₹6.61 on Airtel and Vi. BSNL gives 100 SMS a day; the other three give 300 SMS for the whole pack.

For a calendar month, which renews on the same date each month, BSNL charges ₹190, Jio ₹197, and Airtel and Vi ₹206. At today's prices, 12 monthly packs add up to ₹2,280 on BSNL, ₹2,364 on Jio and ₹2,472 on Airtel and Vi, while Airtel and Vi's 365-day pack costs ₹1,849 and Jio's ₹1,748 pack lasts 336 days. For 84 days, Jio charges ₹448, Airtel ₹469 and Vi ₹470. Jio also sells a ₹147 pack that lasts 22 days.

Airtel and Vi launched their packs in October and BSNL followed in November, after TRAI, the telecom regulator, told operators to offer plans without data. Jio and Vi list them under "TRAI Mandated Packs" on their websites. Prices can vary by circle, so check your operator's app before you recharge.
