Two confirmed launches and one expected reveal fill India's week. Toyota launches the facelifted Innova Hycross on Wednesday, 25 November. Its teaser shows new LED headlamps, prices come at launch, and today's Hycross starts under ₹20 lakh ex-showroom.

MINI launches the second-generation, all-electric Cooper SE and its Paul Smith Edition on Friday, 27 November. Bookings are open at MINI dealers. Prices come at launch; outlets estimate ₹50 lakh to ₹58 lakh ex-showroom for a car with a 54.2 kWh battery and 402 km of claimed range.

Royal Enfield's Himalayan 750, a 743 cc twin, is expected to get its India price at Motoverse in Vagator, Goa, from 27 to 29 November. Royal Enfield has not yet confirmed the India launch or the price.
