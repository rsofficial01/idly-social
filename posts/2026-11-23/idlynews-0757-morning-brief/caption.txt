Morning Brief, Monday 23 November.

Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah holds talks with Prime Minister Narendra Modi at Hyderabad House in New Delhi today, his first bilateral visit abroad since taking office in March. Agreements are due to be exchanged at 12:25 pm, followed by a joint press conference, and he meets President Droupadi Murmu at 3:15 pm, according to the Ministry of External Affairs schedule reported by the Free Press Journal and OnlineKhabar. The two leaders are also due to flag off the weekly Ayodhya-Janakpur train virtually, IANS reports.

In Wellington, New Zealand were five wickets down in their second innings at tea on day five of the first Test (8:10 am IST), about 150 runs behind: India's 720 for 9 declared gave them a 352-run lead over New Zealand's 368. Washington Sundar removed Devon Conway for 65 and Prasidh Krishna removed Rachin Ravindra for 37, ANI and AFP reported, and Anshul Kamboj had Tom Blundell caught for 15.

The India Meteorological Department's Chennai centre forecasts heavy rain at isolated places today in nine districts, among them Chennai, Tiruvallur and Chengalpattu, and on Tuesday in the delta and southern coastal districts. Its outlook has heavy rain at places in Tamil Nadu through 27 November, with thunderstorm gusts of 40-50 km/h.

The NSE and BSE are closed on Tuesday, 24 November, for Guru Nanak Jayanti, according to the exchanges' holiday lists and News On AIR.

UN climate talks at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, ended on Sunday without an overall agreement after two days of overtime, with countries split over language on moving away from fossil fuels, UN News and Reuters reported. The European Union and Panama rejected the final draft. Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav said India "engaged constructively and tirelessly till the very end", PTI reported.
