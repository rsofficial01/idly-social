Drops in India this week, Monday 23 November: what's new and what's coming that you can buy here, with dates and prices.

On sale now: Comet's Kaali Peeli, a ₹5,299 tribute to Mumbai's black-and-yellow Premier Padmini taxis, built on the Bengaluru label's X Lows. It went on sale on 22 November in limited pairs on Comet's website. The tongue carries Comet's take on the Premier Automobiles Limited badge, the heel an MH-01 number plate, and the back tabs read 'For Hire' and 'Nahi Jayenge'.

Tuesday, 24 November: Golden Goose × D'YAVOL X reaches Golden Goose's stores in New Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru, and its India website. D'YAVOL X is the streetwear label co-founded by Shah Rukh Khan, Aryan Khan, Leti Blagoeva and Bunty Singh. The capsule reworks Golden Goose's Super-Star sneaker in silver, white and black leather, alongside a jacket, T-shirts and hoodies. Prices come at launch; none are listed yet.

Friday, 27 November: the Air Jordan 4 OG 'Bred' releases at ₹19,995, the same MRP at Superkicks and VegNonVeg. VegNonVeg is taking early reservations now, and Superkicks lists the pair for pre-order.

Which of these three would you buy?
