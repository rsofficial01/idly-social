The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) said on Sunday, 22 November, that it has sanctioned prosecution against Bisleri International over a sample of "Packaged Drinking Water (other than Mineral Water)" that it found non-compliant with the bromate limit and declared unsafe under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. After an appeal, a referral food laboratory also declared the sample unsafe after detecting polynuclear aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), FSSAI said.

Bisleri said on Monday that its packaged drinking water is safe and that it will cooperate with the regulator. By the company's account, FSSAI took six samples at its Goa plant, one of its 143 plants, on 30 September 2025; five met all standards and the sixth recorded 12 micrograms of bromate per litre against a limit of 10. Bisleri says the referral lab found bromate within the limit but flagged PAHs, that an FSSAI-approved lab it hired also found bromate within the limit, and that it sent those results to FSSAI's Western Regional Office on 2 February 2026. It says it has not received any notice from FSSAI.

FSSAI has collected samples from all major packaged drinking water brands in an ongoing drive and they are being tested, sources told PTI and ANI. No other brand has been named.
