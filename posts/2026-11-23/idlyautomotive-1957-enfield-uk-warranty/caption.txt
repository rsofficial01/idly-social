Royal Enfield now gives UK riders free warranty cover for up to 10 years or 70,000 miles (about 1,12,654 km), whichever comes first. Under the scheme, which started on 20 November 2026, each yearly service at an authorised Royal Enfield UK dealer adds another year once the 3-year factory warranty ends. The cover itself is free; the yearly services are not.

It applies to bikes still under warranty and to new ones bought from 20 November, but not to the old UCE 500s (Classic 500, Bullet 500), the Himalayan 411 or the Scram 411. Only UK-registered bikes qualify, and wear parts such as tyres, brake pads and chains are not covered.

In India, Royal Enfield's standard warranty is 3 years; on the Classic 350 it is 3 years or 30,000 km, whichever comes first. The UK cap of 1,12,654 km is about 3.8 times that distance. Royal Enfield hasn't announced the UK scheme for India.
