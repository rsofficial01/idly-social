Two phone launches are dated for India this week. Motorola's Edge 70 Neo launches on Tuesday, 24 November, and will sell on Flipkart. Motorola has confirmed a 6.8-inch 1.5K AMOLED screen at 144Hz and a Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 chip, and Beebom and Digit list a 7,000mAh battery.

Vivo launches the X500 Pro Max and X500 Pro on Thursday, 26 November, at 12 PM, along with the Vivo Watch GT 2 smartwatch. The Pro Max gets a 200MP ZEISS telephoto camera and works with a 400mm-equivalent ZEISS extender sold separately; both phones run MediaTek's Dimensity 9600 Pro.

Neither company has announced prices. For scale, last year's Vivo X300 Pro launched in India at ₹1,09,999 and the X300 from ₹75,999 on 2 December 2025.
