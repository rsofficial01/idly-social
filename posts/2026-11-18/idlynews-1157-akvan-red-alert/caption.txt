Cyclone Akvan has intensified into a severe cyclonic storm over the Bay of Bengal, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) says. At 5:30 am on Wednesday it was about 610 km southeast of Machilipatnam, moving north-northwest at 3 km/h. IMD expects it to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Nellore and Visakhapatnam around noon on Friday, 20 November, as a severe cyclonic storm with winds of 100-110 km/h gusting to 120 km/h. On Tuesday IMD had forecast a very severe cyclonic storm with gusts up to 145 km/h.

Seven Andhra districts are on red alert for extremely heavy rain on 19 and 20 November, Deccan Chronicle and News24 report: Kakinada, Konaseema, East Godavari, West Godavari, Eluru, Krishna and Bapatla. IMD also warns of a storm surge of 0.4 to 1.2 metres above the tide in low-lying coastal areas at landfall, peaking in Bapatla, Krishna, West Godavari and Kakinada districts from Friday morning to afternoon.

Andhra Pradesh has 13 NDRF and 11 SDRF teams on standby, The Hans India and Daijiworld report. The state disaster management authority's helplines are 112, 1070 and 1800 425 0101, and its WhatsApp line is 95523 00009.
