Morning Brief, Wednesday 18 November.

India are SAFF champions for a record 10th time. Sandip Mandi's 88th-minute header gave India a 2-1 win over Pakistan in the final at the National Stadium in Dhaka on Tuesday night, the All India Football Federation reports. Pakistan led through Alamgir Ghazi just before half-time; after Pakistan's Junaid Shah was sent off following a video review, Suhail Ahmad Bhat equalised. The federation says it is the first time India have won three editions in a row. It was Pakistan's first SAFF final.

Uttar Pradesh has published its draft voter list for the 2027 Assembly election: 13.58 crore names, about 18.8 lakh more than April's final list, Chief Electoral Officer Navdeep Rinwa said. Voters can add a name (Form 6), seek a deletion (Form 7) or correct an entry (Form 8) until 16 December, including at camps at every polling station on 21, 22, 28 and 29 November, 10 am to 4 pm. The final list is due on 4 January 2027.

The Commission for Air Quality Management revoked Stage III of the Graded Response Action Plan, the third of its four stages of pollution curbs, across Delhi-NCR on Tuesday, as the daily average air quality index eased to 204. Stage I and II measures stay in force, and the commission's 30 October order restricting dust-raising construction and demolition continues.

In Manila, PM Narendra Modi met US President Donald Trump, Australia's Anthony Albanese and Japan's Sanae Takaichi at the first Quad leaders' summit in two years. The four countries plan their first joint exercise of maritime patrol aircraft in 2027 and a first Quad business leaders' forum early that year.
