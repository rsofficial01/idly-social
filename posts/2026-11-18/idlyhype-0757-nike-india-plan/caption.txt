Nike says it wants to triple its India business in the next five years. Executives said it on stage at Nike's investor day in Beaverton, Oregon, on 17 November (US time), its first since 2017. India wasn't in Nike's written press release. Chief operating officer Venkatesh Alagirisamy gave CNBC the same five-year goal; Sportico reported it as four years.

Nike named running, training, sportswear and cricket as the sports that will lead the push. When cricket returns to the Olympics at LA 2028, for the first time in more than 100 years, Nike says it will be ready with "a complete cricket footwear line". Nike already sells cricket shoes in India, such as the Domain 3.

Nike's first running shoe built for India, the Vegam Run, has been on sale since 1 November at ₹3,795, on Flipkart and in select stores. For context: Nike becomes the kit partner for Mumbai Indians' men's and women's teams from the 2027 season, a deal announced on 21 October.

The picture is Nike's own image for that Mumbai Indians deal: Wankhede Stadium with a Swoosh in the outfield.
