Gemini Tasks, the AI agent inside Google's Gemini app, is now free in India. Google says it is rolling out from 17 Nov at no charge in India, the US, Japan, Korea, Canada and Australia, on its new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

Google earlier called it Spark. When Spark reached India in July, it was only for Google AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers. AI Pro costs ₹1,950 a month in India.

To try Gemini Tasks, open the Gemini app, flip the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task and describe the job. Gemini asks before it clicks Buy, and you can set a spending cap. Three features start in the US only for now: Gemini calling businesses for you, texting Gemini (it needs a US phone number) and forwarding emails to Gemini.

Jio ended its free Google AI Pro offer for new users on 16 Nov. Google made Gemini Tasks free on 17 Nov.
