Apple has rolled out a new Apple Maps for India, with more than 300 3D landmarks, Look Around street-level imagery and directions that use landmarks such as petrol pumps on roads with no names, Apple, Business Standard and FoneArena report. Apple says the new map is available from today, 18 November.

In places such as Delhi and Mumbai, map labels now show two languages: on a phone set to English, Hindi and English around Delhi, and Marathi and English around Mumbai. Apple lists Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Odia and Urdu alongside English and Hindi. The two-language labels and the landmark directions need iOS 27.2 or later.

Transit directions cover Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and Pune, plus Indian Railways, with an alert when it is time to get off. Maps also adds augmented-reality (AR) walking directions, lane guidance before turns and elevated roads, and routes of up to 15 stops.
