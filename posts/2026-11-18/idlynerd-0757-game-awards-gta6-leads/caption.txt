GTA VI isn't out yet, and it already leads The Game Awards 2026 with eight nominations, the most of any game. The nominees were revealed on Tuesday night, 17 November.

Rockstar's game is up for Game of the Year, Game Direction, Narrative, Art Direction, Score and Music, Audio Design and Action/Adventure, plus Best Performance for the actor who plays Lucia. It unlocks in India at 12 AM IST on Thursday, 19 November.

The other five Game of the Year nominees are 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem and Nintendo's remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

Fan voting is open on thegameawards.com until 7:30 AM IST on Thursday, 10 December. The show streams free from Los Angeles at 6 AM IST on Friday, 11 December.
