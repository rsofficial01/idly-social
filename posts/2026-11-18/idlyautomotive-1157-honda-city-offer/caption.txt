Honda's own offers page lists benefits of up to ₹1,85,900 on the Honda City this month, valid till 30 November 2026. That is a ceiling, not a flat cut: Honda says offers are variant, grade and location specific, and does not say which City variant gets the top amount. The car pictured is the City e:HEV hybrid.

The updated City went on sale on 22 May from ₹11.99 lakh ex-showroom. In the same offers, the new Amaze gets up to ₹66,800 and the older second-generation Amaze up to ₹57,600, while the Elevate and ZR-V are not on Honda's November offer list. Selected corporate buyers can get extra offers on all models, and the dealer confirms the final figure.
