The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has moved Cyclone Akvan's expected landfall north. In bulletins issued at 1 pm and 2:30 pm on Wednesday, it said the severe cyclonic storm is likely to cross the north Andhra Pradesh, Yanam and south Odisha coasts between Narsapur and Puri around noon on Friday, 20 November, with winds of 90-100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h. Its 9:30 am bulletin had said Nellore to Visakhapatnam.

IMD warns of isolated extremely heavy rain (21 cm or more) over coastal Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha on 19 and 20 November, gales of 70-80 km/h along the Odisha coast from Thursday morning, and a storm surge of 0.5 to 1 metre above normal tide in low-lying parts of south coastal Odisha at landfall.

Odisha districts have started shutting schools. Gajapati has closed all schools from 19 to 21 November, Odisha TV and Sambad English report. Khordha and Malkangiri have shut them on 19 and 20 November, and Ganjam's schools and colleges close from 19 November, Pragativadi, Odisharay and Ommcom News report. Odisha's Special Relief Commissioner has also halted sea bathing, boating and water sports at vulnerable beaches, IANS and ETV Bharat report.
