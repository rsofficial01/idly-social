IndiGo has extended its suspension of flights to four Saudi cities again. Its flights to Jeddah and Madinah stay suspended until 21 November, and to Riyadh and Dammam until 30 November, both dates inclusive, according to the airline's travel advisory of 10 November.

IndiGo cites a review of safety and security considerations and says it is alerting affected customers on the contact details they gave at booking. It has not linked the decision to any single attack. The Saudi-led coalition says it has shot down Houthi missiles over Riyadh, and Saudi officials say debris from an attempted attack damaged an Aramco building there on 8 November.

IndiGo first paused these four routes in mid-October and has pushed the restart back more than once since.
