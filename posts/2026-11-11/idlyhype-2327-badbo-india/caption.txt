Two new colourways of Bad Bunny's adidas BadBo are on sale in India. Superkicks lists the BadBo 1.1 "Simple Brown" (LM5057) at ₹11,999 and the BadBo 1.0 "Grey Three" (LC5290) at ₹15,999, both in UK 7–12 and in stock (checked 12 Nov).

adidas says both pairs release worldwide on 12 November on its CONFIRMED app and at select retailers, at $120 and $160 in the US. The company describes the 1.1 as the low-profile version, with three wave-like overlays and a T-toe, and the 1.0 as tonal leather and suede.

The BadBo launched earlier in 2026 as Bad Bunny's first signature shoe with adidas; the 1.1 was added in September.
