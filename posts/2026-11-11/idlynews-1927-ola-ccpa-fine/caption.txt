India's consumer regulator has fined Ola ₹10 lakh over in-app prompts that asked riders to pay an add-on on top of the upfront fare before any driver had accepted the booking, with lines such as "Improve your chances of quicker ride confirmation!"

In an order dated 10 November, the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) held the prompts to be a misleading advertisement and a dark pattern. It told ANI Technologies, which runs Ola, to stop them at once and to file a compliance report within 15 days, according to the order published by Bar & Bench.

The CCPA says Ola's own data showed that ride requests with an add-on were accepted less often than those without. The suggested amounts started at ₹10 to ₹40 and were later raised, the order says.

Ola argued that the add-on is optional and goes entirely to the driver, and that it is used for rides drivers find less viable. The CCPA rejected that. In September, the regulator fined Rapido ₹10 lakh over similar pre-ride payment prompts.

Riders can take complaints to the National Consumer Helpline on the toll-free number 1915, or through its app and website.
