A Mercedes-Benz EQS 450+ drove 867 km (539 miles) on a single charge in Edmunds' real-world electric-vehicle (EV) range test, the longest any sedan has gone in the US car site's testing. The 2027 EQS 450+ beat its official US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) estimate of 752 km by 116 km and pushed the Lucid Air off the top of Edmunds' sedan list. Only the Cadillac Escalade IQ, an electric SUV, has gone farther, at 898 km.

The updated EQS has a bigger 122 kWh battery (up from 118 kWh), a two-speed rear gearbox and 800-volt charging; in Edmunds' test it added about 161 km of range in just over 7 minutes. Mercedes hasn't announced a launch or price for the updated EQS in India.
