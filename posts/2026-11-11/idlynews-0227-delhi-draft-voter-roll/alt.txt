A dark blue signboard on a white pillar reads, in Hindi and English, "Chief Electoral Officer, Delhi", with the address "Old St. Stephen's College Building, Kashmere Gate, Delhi", the national emblem on the left and the Election Commission of India logo on the right. The headline reads "47.5 Lakh Names Left Off Delhi's Draft Voter Roll". The next slides say 25 November is the last day for claims on Delhi's voter roll: use ECINET or the ECI website with your voter ID number, add Annexure B if your address is the same or Annexure C if you moved within Delhi, and skip it if you already filed Form 6. They also say Delhi's final voter roll comes out on 22 December, with claims and objections decided by 15 December; the poll office is issuing fresh forms for everyone left off, and you can also hand the form to your BLO.
