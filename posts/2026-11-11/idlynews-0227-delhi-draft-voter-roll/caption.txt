Delhi's draft voter roll, published on 31 August under the Special Intensive Revision (SIR), did not include about 47.5 lakh electors, according to official data reported by PTI and The Indian Express. They had been marked absent, shifted, dead or duplicate by the poll office.

The Election Commission has extended the deadline for claims and objections to 25 November. Claims are to be decided by 15 December, and the final roll will be published on 22 December.

Delhi's poll officials say they are generating fresh enumeration forms for everyone left off. The form can be filled in on the ECI website or the ECINET app using your EPIC (voter ID) number. If your address has not changed, it goes with a statement (Annexure B) explaining why the form wasn't submitted earlier; if you moved within Delhi, Annexure C gives the new address and constituency. Anyone who has already filed Form 6 for fresh enrolment need not file again.
