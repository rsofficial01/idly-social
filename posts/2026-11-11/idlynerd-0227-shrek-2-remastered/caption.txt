Shrek 2 Remastered, Aspyr's remaster of the 2004 console game (PS2, Xbox, GameCube), came out on 10 November on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Switch and PC, through Steam and the Epic Games Store.

In India, Steam lists it at ₹1,599, with a 10% launch discount and no end date listed. On PlayStation Store India the PS5 version costs ₹2,997, about ₹1,400 more. Both prices were checked on 11 November.

Aspyr says everything has been rebuilt, with better textures, an enhanced interface, 4K resolution and redone cutscenes. Up to four players can play together on one couch, online or both, and there are new Photo and Cheat modes.
