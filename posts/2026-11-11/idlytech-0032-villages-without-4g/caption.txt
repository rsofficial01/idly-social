Department of Telecommunications (DoT) data, obtained by The Hindu under the RTI Act and reported on 9 November, counts 11,372 villages in India without 4G, down from 45,180 in 2022, a fall of about three-fourths. The reports don't say what date the count is from.

That fall is bigger than the 24,680 villages BSNL's 4G saturation project, approved in 2022 at ₹26,316 crore, set out to cover. Odisha has the most villages still without 4G, 1,789, followed by Arunachal Pradesh with 1,518; many of those villages have few or no residents. About 40 lakh people are estimated to live in villages without 4G, around 46% of them in Scheduled Tribe-majority villages, and the Centre has sanctioned 26,000 more tower sites. In January, Telecom Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia said 4G would reach every village by June 2026.
