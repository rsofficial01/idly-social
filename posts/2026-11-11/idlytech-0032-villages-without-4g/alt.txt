A tall red-and-white BSNL 4G telecom tower, its antennas and two platforms against a clear blue sky, rises above teak trees in Todabhim, Rajasthan. The headline reads "11,372 Indian Villages Still Have No 4G, DoT Data Shows". The next slides say 45,180 villages had no 4G in 2022, down about 75% in four years; Odisha has the most villages without 4G (1,789), then Arunachal Pradesh (1,518); and about 40 lakh people are estimated to live in villages without 4G, around 46% of them in tribal-majority villages.
