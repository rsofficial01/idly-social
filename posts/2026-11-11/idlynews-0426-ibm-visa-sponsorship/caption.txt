IBM is ending US visa and green card sponsorship for most roles in IBM Consulting, its biggest division with more than 1,50,000 of the company's 2,64,300 staff, a spokesperson told Reuters on Tuesday (10 November). In a statement to Newsweek, IBM said its use of US high-skilled visa programmes "has been declining for a number of years".

USCIS data cited by Reuters show 1,611 H-1B petitions approved for IBM in US fiscal 2026. IBM hasn't said which roles are exempt or from when.

Separately, in October the US Labor Department suspended firms including TCS and Infosys from PERM, the labour certification step for green cards.
