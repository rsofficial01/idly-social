Square Enix has unveiled Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle, a new turn-based Dragon Quest for iOS and Android that launches in 2027. Series creator Yuji Horii is general director, and WFS is developing it.

A closed beta runs from 8 to 15 December for 25,000 players picked by lottery. Applications are open on the game's official site until 23 November, 8:29 PM IST (2:59 PM UTC), and Square Enix emails the results on 4 December. Phones need Android 9 or iOS 15 or later, and beta progress won't carry over to the full game.

Square Enix's beta page lists no country limit.
