Diljit Dosanjh is going back to Coachella. The festival's 2027 lineup is out, and Diljit is on the Friday bill on both weekends: 9 and 16 April 2027. Dua Lipa headlines both Fridays, Tame Impala headlines Saturday and SZA closes on Sunday.

Diljit made his Coachella debut in 2023 as the first Punjabi artist to play the festival.

Coachella's website lists passes on sale on Friday 13 November at 12 pm Pacific Time, which is 1:30 am IST on Saturday 14 November. At current Tier 2 pricing, general admission is $779 for either weekend, shuttle pass included; VIP is $1,399 for Weekend 1 and $1,349 for Weekend 2. Both weekends are at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California.
