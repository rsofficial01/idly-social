The Union Cabinet on Wednesday, 11 November, approved a ₹62,370 crore subsidy on phosphatic and potassic (P&K) fertilisers, including DAP and NPKS grades, for the Rabi 2026-27 season, PTI and IANS report. The rates cover 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.

That is about 64% more than the ₹37,952 crore approved for Rabi 2025-26 in October last year, according to IANS and ANI. The Centre pays the subsidy to fertiliser makers and importers at the notified rates, not to farmers.

Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw told reporters that fertiliser prices have "shot up immensely" because of the West Asia crisis and the Russia-Ukraine war. He said DAP will stay at ₹1,350 for a 50 kg bag and called the subsidy a "shield to farmers from geopolitical turmoil", PTI reports.
