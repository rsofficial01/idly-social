Krafton India has dated BGMI's winter. The 4.7 update goes live on 18 November with Arctic Chronicles, a mode in which Jormungandr, the giant serpent of Norse myth, reshapes the terrain on Erangel, Livik and Vikendi until 18 January 2027.

Erangel Subzero, a frozen Erangel for ranked matches, follows on 3 December, then a Snow Festival event on 17 December and an in-game New Year countdown from 8 PM IST on 31 December.

BGMI Air, the lighter version for phones short on storage or power, launches on 10 December on Android and iOS. Its first download is about 1 GB, and existing BGMI accounts work without a transfer. It passed 14 million pre-registrations on Google Play before Krafton set the date, and iOS pre-registration opened on 11 November.
