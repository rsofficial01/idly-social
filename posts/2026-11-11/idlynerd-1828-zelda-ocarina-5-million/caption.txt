Nintendo's remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time sold more than 5.2 million copies worldwide in its first four days, Nintendo said on Wednesday, 11 November. That includes 8,10,000 in Japan, and counts both physical and digital copies.

Nintendo says that is the highest four-day global total for any Switch 2 game it has published. The full remake of the 1998 Nintendo 64 game came out on 5 November on Nintendo Switch 2.

For scale, that four-day figure is more than Donkey Kong Bananza's 5.05 million total as of 30 September, per Nintendo's investor data, which has Mario Kart World on top of the Switch 2 list at 16.48 million.
