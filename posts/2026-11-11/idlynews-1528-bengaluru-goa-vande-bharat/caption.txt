A new daytime Vande Bharat will link Bengaluru and Goa via Mangaluru and the Karnataka coast. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is due to flag it off in Bengaluru on Friday, 13 November, and regular runs begin on 15 November, Daijiworld and Metro Rail News report.

The timetable, as reported by Metro Rail News and Oneindia: train 26655 leaves Yesvantpur at 7:35 AM and reaches Madgaon at 8:55 PM, about 13 hours 20 minutes. The return, 26656, leaves Madgaon at 8:20 AM and reaches Yesvantpur at 9:15 PM. It runs six days a week, with no departure from Yesvantpur on Wednesdays and none from Madgaon on Thursdays.

The 12 stops are Shravanabelagola, Hassan, Sakleshpur, Subrahmanya Road, Kabaka Puttur, Mangaluru Junction, Udupi, Kundapura, Mookambika Road Byndoor, Murdeshwar, Gokarna Road and Karwar. The train extends the Mangaluru–Madgaon Vande Bharat, which stops running on 15 November, and takes about an hour longer than it on that stretch, according to Coastal Digest and Pune Pulse.

The Karnataka Railway Vedike, a rail users' group, says the train suits Bengaluru–Mangaluru trips but that running it on to Goa makes the journey too long, the two outlets report. Commuters also object that it skips Mangaluru Central.
