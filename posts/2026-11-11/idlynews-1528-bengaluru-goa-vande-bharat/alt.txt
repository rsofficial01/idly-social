A white-and-blue Vande Bharat train, the Mangaluru–Madgaon service, waits at a platform at Madgaon station in Goa under a grey sky, with red-tiled platform roofs, a palm tree and goods wagons on the next line, under the headline "New Bengaluru–Goa Train: About 13 Hours". Slide 2: regular runs of the new Vande Bharat begin 15 Nov, after a 13 Nov flag-off in Bengaluru; no run from Yesvantpur on Wednesdays or from Madgaon on Thursdays. Slide 3: it leaves Yesvantpur at 7:35 AM and reaches Madgaon at 8:55 PM; the return leaves Madgaon at 8:20 AM and reaches Yesvantpur at 9:15 PM; it calls at Mangaluru Junction at 3:05 PM going and 1:50 PM returning; a rail users' group says the Goa extension makes the trip too long. Slide 4: the 12 stops, inland from Shravanabelagola to Puttur and on the coast from Mangaluru to Karwar, and that Mangaluru–Goa riders face a trip about an hour slower than today's train.
