Qualcomm has started laying off staff in India, Moneycontrol's sources say, in reports carried by Business Today, Digit and Jagran English. About 1,000 of the chipmaker's estimated 20,000 employees in India could be affected, though Business Today notes other reports put the figure lower. Qualcomm has not said how many roles it is cutting.

The first exits began on 9 November in Bengaluru, and the cuts are expected to reach Hyderabad, Chennai and Delhi-NCR. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, RF and GNSS teams are named in the reports, with senior engineers among those affected.

The reported severance is at least three months' salary plus pay linked to years of service, with one more month's salary for those who sign exit papers within three days. Qualcomm has not confirmed these terms.

The cuts are part of a "performance-focused" global reduction that CEO Cristiano Amon announced to staff in October, with notices due from November, Reuters reported on 20 October.
