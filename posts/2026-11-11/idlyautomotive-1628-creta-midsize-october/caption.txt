Hyundai dispatched 17,955 Cretas to dealers in October 2026, enough to stay India's No. 1 mid-size SUV. The Maruti Suzuki Victoris finished 1,163 units behind, at 16,792.

The gap is narrowing from both sides. The Creta slipped 2.3% from 18,381 a year earlier, while the Victoris rose 24.4% from 13,496. The Kia Seltos grew fastest of the top seven, up 88.3% to 13,423 from 7,130.

Behind them: Maruti Grand Vitara 11,200 (up 7.6%), Toyota Hyryder 7,797 (down 32.5%), newcomer Tata Sierra 7,205 and Honda Elevate 3,673 (up 68%).

All figures are dispatches to dealers (wholesale), not sales to buyers.
