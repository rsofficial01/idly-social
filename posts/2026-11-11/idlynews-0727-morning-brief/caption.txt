Morning Brief, Wednesday 11 November.

India's unemployment rate fell to 5.1% in July-September from 5.4% in April-June, the government's Periodic Labour Force Survey shows (people aged 15 and above, current weekly status). Rural joblessness eased to 4.3% from 4.8%, while the urban rate edged up to 6.8% from 6.7%.

Bangladesh says it will not issue Sheikh Hasina a new passport and wants her extradited. Asked about it on Tuesday, India's External Affairs Ministry said travel needs "a travel document".

The Election Commission has told Goa, Manipur, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand, where Assembly elections are due in 2027, that poll-code curbs on government servants also apply to retired officials the states re-employ. They may not summon election officers once polls are announced.

In Washington, the US House voted 211-204 on Tuesday to send a resolution to impeach Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Republican-led Judiciary Committee, which Reuters says effectively derails a planned vote on the charges. Its eight articles accuse Hegseth of waging the Iran war without congressional approval, among other charges. Republican Thomas Massie, who led the effort, was the only member of his party to vote against the referral.

The US Justice Department says it has charged three men it calls Iran-backed operatives over alleged plots in the US and Europe, including an incident near RAF Fairford in England in September. Iran has denied involvement in the Fairford incident.
