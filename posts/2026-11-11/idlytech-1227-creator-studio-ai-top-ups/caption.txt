Apple now sells AI top-ups for Creator Studio, its ₹399-a-month bundle of creative apps. Each "intelligence usage pack" is a one-time purchase that adds AI usage once a subscriber runs through the monthly limit. It costs $49.99 in the US; no rupee price has been reported. Engadget and 9to5Mac reported the launch on 9 November.

India is one of eight countries where subscribers can buy them, with the US, UK, Canada, Brazil, France, Germany and Mexico. Logic Pro is the only app selling them so far. Unused usage rolls over from month to month and expires 12 months after purchase. A pack works only on the Apple Account that bought it; there is no Family Sharing.

Creator Studio costs ₹399 a month or ₹3,999 a year in India. Students and educators pay ₹199 a month or ₹1,999 a year.
