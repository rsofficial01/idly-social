Renault's Duster comes with benefits of up to ₹2.10 lakh this November, Autocar India and GaadiWaadi report. The offers run till 30 November and vary by city, dealer and stock.

Up to ₹1.5 lakh of that is an exchange bonus, so it needs a car to trade in. The rest is a cash discount of up to ₹50,000 and a loyalty bonus of up to ₹10,000. Scrapping an old car instead of exchanging it gets up to ₹25,000. A 4.99% loan offer is also available.

The Duster starts at ₹10.49 lakh ex-showroom. Renault's other November offers: up to ₹65,000 on the Kiger, ₹55,000 on the Triber and ₹40,000 on the Kwid.
