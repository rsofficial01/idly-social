Tata's November offers on its electric cars are biggest on older Curvv EV stock: up to ₹3.35 lakh on pre-facelift (pre-Series X) versions. That is Tata's own "green bonus" of up to ₹3 lakh plus up to ₹35,000 for an exchange or scrappage. The Creative trim from that older stock gets up to ₹2.85 lakh, while a current Curvv EV Series X gets up to ₹65,000 (₹35,000 exchange or scrappage plus ₹30,000 loyalty).

Across Tata's other EVs: the Harrier EV gets up to ₹2.75 lakh on select variants, the Punch EV and Tiago EV up to ₹1.45 lakh on pre-facelift stock, and the Nexon EV up to ₹45,000 on all variants. The new Sierra EV is not part of the offers.

Autocar India reports the offers run till 15 November; CarWale's offer listings show 30 November, so confirm the date with your dealer. These are maximum dealer benefits: they vary by city, variant and stock, depend on exchange or scrappage eligibility, and exchange and scrappage don't stack.
