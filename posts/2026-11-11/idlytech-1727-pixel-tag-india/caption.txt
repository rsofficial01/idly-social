Google's Pixel Tag tracker is now on sale in the US. In India, Google Store still lists it as "Coming November 2026", at ₹3,799 for one tag or ₹12,900 for a four-pack, and for now offers only a sign-up for alerts. The India page gives no exact sale date.

That puts a single Pixel Tag ₹9 above Apple's AirTag (2nd generation), which costs ₹3,790 on Apple's India store. Both four-packs cost ₹12,900, which works out to ₹3,225 a Pixel Tag.

Pixel Tag needs an Android phone on Android 9 or later and finds lost things through Google's Find Hub network of more than a billion Android devices. Google says its CR2032 coin cell lasts about a year and you can replace it yourself. It is rated IP67 for dust and water, and comes in one colour, Fog.
