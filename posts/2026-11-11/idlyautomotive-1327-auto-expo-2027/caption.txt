Auto Expo 2027 runs from 4 to 9 February 2027 at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, as part of the third Bharat Mobility Global Expo.

Organisers expect more than 10 lakh visitors and over 1,750 exhibitors across 11 shows. The Auto Expo motor show is at Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, and the components and technology show is at Yashobhoomi in Dwarka.

At the Expo's curtain raiser in New Delhi, Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal said the exhibition space is likely to be fully booked and a return to temporary hangars could not be ruled out. Public days and ticket prices have not been announced yet.
