Maruti Suzuki dispatched 20,649 Dzires to dealers in October 2026. That is more than the next seven on the top-10 sedan dispatch tables reported by GaadiWaadi and Autopunditz, put together.

Those seven add up to 20,588 units, 61 short of the Dzire: the Hyundai Aura (7,349), Tata Aeris (4,005), Honda Amaze (3,337), Skoda Slavia (2,638), VW Virtus (1,888), Hyundai Verna (775) and Honda City (596). The Dzire alone took 49.4% of the top-10 total of 41,773, which rose 12.3% from 37,211 a year earlier.

The Slavia grew fastest, up 60.1%, and moved ahead of its sibling, the Virtus. The Aeris, Tata's renamed facelift of the Tigor, launched in September, Autocar India reported.

All figures are dispatches to dealers (wholesale), not sales to buyers.
