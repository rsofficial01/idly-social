Rockstar says GTA VI is targeting 30 frames per second on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it has not announced a 60 FPS mode, PS5 Pro included. The statement came with the game's pre-load on 10 November.

What the PS5 Pro adds, per Rockstar and Sony's PlayStation Blog, is a sharper picture through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) upscaling, and ray-traced shadows that no other console gets.

There is no Xbox One version. Rockstar says Xbox One owners can stream the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming at Xbox Series S graphics settings, and that connection speed can affect how it plays. Xbox Cloud Gaming has been available in India since November 2025.

GTA VI launches on 19 November.
