Navi Mumbai has topped the Super Swachh League in Swachh Survekshan 2025-26, the Centre's annual cleanliness survey of cities. President Droupadi Murmu presented the awards at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi, on Wednesday, 11 November. Surat came second and Vijayawada third, ETV Bharat and ANI report.

The Super Swachh League is for cities that ranked in the top three in recent editions. Indore led it in 2024-25, with Surat second and Navi Mumbai third. This year Indore is not in the top three, which ends its run of eight surveys at the top, The Tribune and The Federal report. Indore and its partner town Depalpur came second in the new Swachh Shehar Jodi category for mentor and mentee cities, behind Ahmedabad and Sanand.

In the main ranking for cities of 10 lakh and more, Greater Visakhapatnam came first, followed by Jabalpur and Lucknow. Ujjain led cities of 3 to 10 lakh, ahead of Chandigarh and Guntur, The Tribune and The Federal report. Special awards went to coastal towns (Puri, Alappuzha, Ratnagiri), riverfront towns (Rewa, Bilaspur, Varanasi) and Himalayan cities (Rudrapur, Jammu, Shopian).

The survey ranked 4,751 cities on 43 indicators this year, up from 73 cities in 2016, according to ETV Bharat and ANI.
