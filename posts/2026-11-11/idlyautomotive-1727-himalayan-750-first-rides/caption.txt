Royal Enfield's twin-cylinder Himalayan 750 has its first reviews. BikeWale and ZigWheels published first-ride reports on 11 November, and both say the engine pulls strongly from low revs and the bike is agile on the move; ZigWheels adds that it feels heavy at parking speeds.

The 743cc air/oil-cooled twin makes 55 PS at 7,000 rpm and 63 Nm at 5,000 rpm, through a six-speed gearbox. That is about 15 PS and 23 Nm more than the single-cylinder Himalayan 450. It gets cruise control, cornering ABS, lean-sensitive traction control and five riding modes.

The India-spec bike weighs 224 kg, 28 kg more than the 450, with a 17.8-litre tank and an 815 mm seat.

In the UK it starts at £6,999. Royal Enfield hasn't announced an India price or launch date; Autocar India expects India details at Royal Enfield's Motoverse festival, 27-29 November in Vagator, Goa.
