Sony's PlayStation 5 has passed 100 million consoles shipped worldwide. Sony's business data page now lists the PS5 at "more than 100 million" as of 10 November 2026, Gematsu reports; Sony's quarterly figures had it at 97.9 million on 30 September. These are sell-in numbers: consoles shipped to shops, not sold to players.

The PS4 got there sooner. It launched in November 2013 and had shipped 100 million by June 2019, about five years and seven months in. The PS5, launched in November 2020, was still short of the mark on 30 September 2026, more than five years and ten months in.

Sony's all-time list now puts the PS5 fourth: behind the PS2 (more than 160 million), the PS4 (more than 117 million) and the original PlayStation (more than 102.4 million), and ahead of the PS3 (more than 87.4 million).
