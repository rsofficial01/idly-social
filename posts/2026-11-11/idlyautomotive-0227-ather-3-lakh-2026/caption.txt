Ather Energy's scooter sales have crossed 3 lakh in 2026. Vahan registrations show 3,01,133 Ather e-scooters from 1 January to 6 November 2026, up 67% on the same period of 2025, Autocar India and BikeAdvice report.

That is 16.3% of India's electric two-wheeler sales, Ather's highest share yet (16.1% in 2025). Only TVS and Bajaj have sold more e-scooters this year; Hero's Vida is fourth. Ather's best month was March, at 36,630, and both outlets expect it to end 2026 at about 3.5 lakh.
