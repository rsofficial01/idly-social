The Outer Worlds 2 gets its first story expansion, Side Effects Guaranteed, on 2 December. Obsidian Entertainment's add-on opens a new region on Eden, Elysium Valley, raises the level cap from 30 to 36 and adds 15 new perks and four new flaws.

It is part one of two. Part two follows in spring 2027, and the choices you make in part one carry over.

In India, the Premium Upgrade costs ₹2,000 on Steam and includes both expansions; the base game is ₹5,499 there. The expansion's own India price isn't listed yet. It has been announced for PC on Steam, PS5 and Xbox.
