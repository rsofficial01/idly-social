Maharashtra's committee drafting a Uniform Civil Code (UCC) is taking public feedback until 15 November. Its website, launched on 15 October, carries 37 questions on personal laws: the minimum age of marriage (options of 18, 21, 24 or 27), whether a person may marry while already married, whether live-in relationships should be registered when they begin and when they end, and whether inheritance without a will should follow common rules across religions. Most questions take a yes, no or no comment; a separate section invites written suggestions. Individuals and organisations can respond online, by email or by post.

The seven-member committee is led by retired Supreme Court judge Ranjana Desai, who also headed Uttarakhand's and Gujarat's UCC panels, and it plans public hearings across the state. Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis has said the government aims to bring a UCC Bill in the winter session in December.

On 10 November, AIMIM chief Asaduddin Owaisi said the questionnaire had leading questions and called the proposed code a "Hindu Civil Code" imposed on non-Hindus. Fadnavis said the Constitution's Directive Principles direct states to work towards a UCC.
