Marathon, Bungie's sci-fi extraction shooter, is free to play from 8 to 15 December. The Open Play Week starts the day the Symbiosis update lands, PlayStation Blog and Game Informer report, and Game Informer says new players get all of the game's content, not a trial slice.

Symbiosis, an update for all players that Bungie had already set for 8 December, adds Expedition, Marathon's first PvE-only mode (Phase 1 of a three-run series, playable in co-op), a 3v3 Deathmatch beta where no permanent loadout is at risk, and Bishop, a fast, blade-wielding Runner shell. On PS5 and Xbox, online play still needs a paid subscription, Sony's PlayStation Blog notes. Marathon costs ₹2,449 on Steam India today.
