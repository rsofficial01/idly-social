Two High Courts have disowned the purported court orders that India's Department of Telecommunications (DoT) acted on when it ordered 129 betting websites blocked this year, a Reuters investigation published on 11 November found.

DoT told internet providers to block 34 sites on 21 July and 95 more on 2 September. Both directions rested on emailed documents presented as High Court orders: one in the Rajasthan High Court's name, the other in the Allahabad High Court's. When Reuters checked, neither document showed up in either court's online case records.

The Rajasthan High Court's registrar told Reuters the order in its name "appears to be fraudulent". At the Allahabad High Court, the judge's office said "no such order has been passed", and the court filed a police complaint on 6 November. Uttar Pradesh Police have registered a case against unknown persons for alleged forgery and cheating, Reuters and Bar & Bench report. No arrests have been reported.

DoT did not respond to Reuters' questions. Reuters says both blocking directions were still on DoT's website on 11 November.
