Headline: "DoT Blocked 129 Betting Sites on Orders High Courts Disown". Slide 2: 34 plus 95 betting sites were blocked in two DoT directions: 34 on 21 July, on a purported Rajasthan High Court order, and 95 on 2 September, on a purported Allahabad High Court order; Reuters says both directions were still on DoT's website on 11 November. Slide 3: Reuters found no court records matching either order; the Rajasthan High Court's registrar said its order "appears to be fraudulent"; the Allahabad High Court found its order was never passed; the Allahabad order's QR code opened an unrelated web page. Slide 4: on 6 November, after the Allahabad High Court's complaint, Uttar Pradesh Police registered a case against unknown persons for alleged forgery and cheating; no arrests have been reported and who made the orders is unknown; DoT did not respond to Reuters' questions.
