WhatsApp head Kunal Shah, in charge since June, set out three aims in a note posted on X on 10 November: richer voice notes, groups, stickers and video; making it as simple as sending a message to pay a bill, borrow money or stay connected while travelling, working with financial-services and telecom partners; and AI that should "make sure you never miss a booking or an appointment". The note gives no dates or timelines for any of it.

People send over 10 billion voice messages a day on WhatsApp, Shah wrote. Personal messages and calls stay end-to-end encrypted, Shah says.

Bill payments have been on WhatsApp in India since September: Meta lists 22,722 billers across 30 categories through Bharat Connect, including electricity, gas, water, FASTag, credit card and loan repayments, under the ₹ icon at the top of the home screen, paid by UPI, debit card or credit card. Meta said it would roll the feature out gradually.
