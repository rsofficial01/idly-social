The Hindu reports that Meta has set India prices for Muse, its personal AI agent, ahead of an India launch: ₹1,799 for the Power plan and ₹8,999 for the Maximum plan.

In the US, the same two plans cost $20 and $100 a month. Meta says Muse is free for most of what people need, with the paid plans for people who want it to do more.

Meta has not confirmed the India prices, whether they are monthly or yearly, or when Muse launches here.
