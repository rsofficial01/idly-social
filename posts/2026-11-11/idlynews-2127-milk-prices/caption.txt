Amul and Mother Dairy both raise milk prices from Thursday, 12 November, by ₹2 to ₹4 a litre.

Amul's marketing federation, GCMMF, said in a statement on 11 November that Amul Gold (6% fat) goes up ₹4 a litre, about 5.5%, and Amul Taaza (3% fat) ₹2, about 3.3%. At a litre a day, that is about ₹120 more a month for Gold and about ₹60 more for Taaza.

GCMMF puts the rise down to costlier cattle feed, fodder, packaging film and fuel, and to low, uneven rainfall. It says its member unions have raised what they pay farmers by 4–5% over the past year, and that its 36 lakh milk producers get about 80% of what consumers pay.

In Delhi-NCR, Mother Dairy's full cream milk goes from ₹72 to ₹76 a litre, toned from ₹60 to ₹62, double toned from ₹54 to ₹56 and cow milk from ₹62 to ₹65. The company confirmed the rise in a statement on 11 November, ANI reports; PTI had reported the same rates earlier in the day, citing sources.

Both brands last raised prices on 14 May, by ₹2 a litre.
