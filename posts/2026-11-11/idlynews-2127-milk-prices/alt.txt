Photo of stacked yellow plastic milk crates on a delivery truck behind white metal bars, each crate stencilled "Amul" or "अमूल" with batch dates, and milk pouches visible inside. Headline: "Amul, Mother Dairy Raise Milk Prices ₹2–₹4 From 12 Nov". Slide 2: Amul Gold goes up ₹4 a litre and Amul Taaza ₹2; a litre of Gold a day costs about ₹120 more a month, a litre of Taaza about ₹60 more. Slide 3: Mother Dairy full cream milk in Delhi-NCR goes to ₹76 a litre from ₹72, toned from ₹60 to ₹62 and cow milk from ₹62 to ₹65; Mother Dairy confirmed the rates in a statement, ANI reports. Slide 4: GCMMF says the prices its unions pay farmers rose 4 to 5% in a year; its reasons are costlier feed, fodder, packaging and fuel, and uneven rain; it says about 80% of the price reaches farmers; Amul's last rise was ₹2 a litre on 14 May 2026.
