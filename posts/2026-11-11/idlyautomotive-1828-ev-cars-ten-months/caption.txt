India sold nearly 3 lakh electric cars in the first ten months of 2026, going by Vahan registration data reported by Autocar India and GaadiWaadi. All of 2025 ended just under 2 lakh.

Autocar India counts 3,04,701 from 1 January to 9 November and says it is the first time electric car sales have crossed 3 lakh in a calendar year. It expects 3.5 lakh to 3.6 lakh by 31 December.

Tata leads with 39.7% of electric cars sold this year. Mahindra has overtaken JSW MG for No. 2 with about 23%, and MG is third at 18.7%. Together the three sell about four in every five electric cars in India. VinFast is fourth with about 5%.
