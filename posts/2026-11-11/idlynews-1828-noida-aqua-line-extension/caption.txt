The Union Cabinet on Wednesday, 11 November, approved a ₹2,390 crore extension of the Noida Metro's Aqua Line from Noida Sector 51 to Greater Noida Sector 4 in Greater Noida West, Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced, according to Hindustan Times and The Economic Times.

The elevated stretch will be 8.449 km long with five stations and will meet the Delhi Metro's Blue Line at Noida Sector 61, the Press Information Bureau says. The government lists Gaur City 1 and 2, Cleo County, Gaur City Mall and offices in Noida Sector 60 among the areas it will serve, and says Noida and Greater Noida will then have 71.878 km of metro.

Mint and The Economic Times report that the extension is expected to take about four years to build. Through the Sector 61 interchange, riders will be able to reach Delhi's airport and railway stations by metro, the government and The Economic Times say.
