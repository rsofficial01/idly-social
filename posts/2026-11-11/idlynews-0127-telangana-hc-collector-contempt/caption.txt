The Telangana High Court has sentenced Ranga Reddy district Collector C Narayana Reddy, an IAS officer, to six months' simple imprisonment and a ₹2,000 fine for contempt of court, according to reports on 9 November. The sentence is suspended for four weeks so he can appeal.

Justice K Lakshman held that the Collector wilfully disobeyed an August 2025 order to decide, within 60 days, a plea to take some plots off the prohibitory list kept under Section 22-A of the Registration Act. Plots on that list can't be registered for sale. According to the reports, notices went out only after a contempt case was filed on 15 October 2025, and the plea was rejected on 26 February and again on 8 July 2026. The court called part of his counter-affidavit "palpably false" and said: "Right or wrong, the order has to be obeyed." No response from the Collector or the state government has been reported.
