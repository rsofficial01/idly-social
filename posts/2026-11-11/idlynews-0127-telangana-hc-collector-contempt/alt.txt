The headline reads "Telangana HC Gives District Collector 6 Months' Jail for Contempt". The next slides say the sentence is suspended for four weeks so Ranga Reddy Collector C Narayana Reddy can appeal, and he was fined ₹2,000; the court had given him 60 days in August 2025 to decide a plea to take plots off the Section 22-A list, which stops them being registered for sale; and he rejected the plea on 26 February and again on 8 July 2026, with the court calling part of his affidavit "palpably false" and saying "Right or wrong, the order has to be obeyed".
