A pair of 1972 Nike Moon Shoes sold for $437,500 at Sotheby's, New York, on 23 July 2019, a sneaker auction record at the time. Designed by Nike co-founder Bill Bowerman for the 1972 Olympic trials, it is one of only 12 pairs made and the only one known to be unworn.

Its remake, the Nike Moon Shoe OG, is in stock in India at ₹8,995. Superkicks has the Black/Sail pair (IX3952-001) in UK 6–12, and VegNonVeg lists it in UK 7–11 (both checked 11 Nov). Nike sells the same pair for $105 in the US.

Nike calls it a modern take on the shoe Bowerman made with his wife's waffle iron: a nylon upper, a leather Swoosh and a waffle sole.
