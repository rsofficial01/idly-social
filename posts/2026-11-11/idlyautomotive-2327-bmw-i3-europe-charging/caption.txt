BMW says a production-spec i3 50 xDrive, its new electric 3 Series sedan on the Neue Klasse platform, drove 5,887 km from North Cape in Norway to Tarifa in Spain with 2 hours 46 minutes of charging in total (2 h 45 min 54 s). The run crossed eight countries using only public chargers, with one engineer at the wheel for 3 days, 2 hours and 41 minutes. BMW announced it on 10 November.

BMW says the car averaged 275 kW while plugged in and peaks at 400 kW. It used 12.5 kWh per 100 km, below its official WLTP figure of 13.4 to 18 kWh, so each charge went further. BMW says the total beat the previous record, set in 2017, by more than seven hours; the timing is BMW's own, not independently checked.

The i3 sedan is not confirmed for India. BMW has confirmed the long-wheelbase iX3, built on the same Neue Klasse technology, for India in 2027, Autocar India reports.
