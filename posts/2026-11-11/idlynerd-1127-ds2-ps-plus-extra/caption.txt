Death Stranding 2: On the Beach joins the PlayStation Plus Game Catalogue on 17 November, according to Sony's November catalogue on the PlayStation Blog (10 November). The catalogue comes with the Extra and Deluxe tiers. Essential doesn't include it.

On PlayStation's India site, Extra costs ₹979 a month, Deluxe ₹1,109 and Essential ₹649. Bought on its own, the Standard Edition lists at ₹4,999 on PS Store India.

Atomfall and Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake join the catalogue on 17 November too. Sony says catalogue lineups may differ by region, so check PS Store on the day.
