Wales Bonner's adidas Originals autumn-winter 2026 pairs are available at two Indian sneaker stores, Superkicks and VegNonVeg. The collection released worldwide on 29 October; Superkicks added its five pairs on 11 November.

Listed prices on 11 Nov:
Gazelle Croc, green croc-embossed leather (KK0735): ₹21,999 at Superkicks and VegNonVeg
Karintha Croc, dark brown croc-embossed leather (KK0733): ₹21,999 at Superkicks and VegNonVeg
BW Army Pony, black (KK2813) or khaki (KK2809) ponyhair: ₹19,999 at Superkicks and VegNonVeg
Adizero Adios Suede, white (KK0774): ₹19,999, Superkicks lists

On Wales Bonner's own UK store, the croc pairs cost £180 and the other three £170.
