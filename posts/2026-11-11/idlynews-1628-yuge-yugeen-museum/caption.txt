The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs, chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, approved ₹3,230 crore on Wednesday, 11 November, to turn North Block and South Block on Raisina Hill into the Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum, according to the Press Information Bureau and ThePrint. The cost was appraised by the Public Investment Board.

The first gallery, on the theme of Adornment, is expected to open in North Block in the later part of 2027, and the project will be built in phases through 2030-31, the government release and ETV Bharat say. The museum is designed for 1 crore visitors a year, and about 18 lakh objects have been identified for it from the National Museum, 52 ASI site museums, state museums and private collections.

Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said the museum will have "the largest display area in the world", ETV Bharat reports. The Prime Minister first announced the museum on 18 May 2023. The PMO moved out of South Block to the new Seva Teerth complex in February 2026, ThePrint and DD News reported at the time.
