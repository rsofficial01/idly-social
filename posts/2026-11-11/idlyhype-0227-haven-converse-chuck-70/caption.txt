Converse India's online store lists the Converse x HAVEN Chuck 70 at ₹10,999 in two colours, Jet Black/Cloud Dancer/White and Bungee Cord/Cloud Dancer/Black, in UK sizes 7 to 11. HAVEN, a Canadian menswear retailer, launches the pair on its own site and in its stores on 12 November; Converse's wider release, through its own channels and select retailers, follows on 17 November.

The high-top pairs smooth cashmere suede with long-pile hairy suede panels, swaps the usual rubber toe bumper for a leather toe cap and adds a side zip alongside the laces.
