Mahindra's XEV 9S electric SUV has a 5-star rating from Bharat NCAP, the Indian government's crash-test programme. Mahindra announced the result on 17 November.

The XEV 9S scored 31.15 out of 32 for adult occupant protection and 45 out of 49 for child occupant protection. Its 76.15-point total is the highest of any seven-seater Bharat NCAP has tested, petrol, diesel or electric, ahead of the Mahindra XUV 7XO (75.73), Toyota Innova Hycross (75.47) and Tata Safari (74.62). Mahindra says it is India's first seven-seat EV to earn five stars, and the rating applies to every variant.

In the frontal test, the front passenger's chest protection was rated adequate; the side and pole tests rated every body region good. Six airbags are standard, and Pack Three trims get seven. Mahindra's website lists prices from ₹21.40 lakh for the Pack One Above with the 59 kWh battery; 70 kWh and 79 kWh packs are also offered.
