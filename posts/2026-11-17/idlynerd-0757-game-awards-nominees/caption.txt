The Game Awards reveals its 2026 nominees tonight, Tuesday 17 November, in a livestream at 10:30 PM IST (noon in New York). Host Geoff Keighley announced the time.

Games had to be on sale by Friday, 20 November, 9 PM Pacific time to qualify. That makes Rockstar's GTA VI, out on 19 November, eligible, along with Nintendo's Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake.

The show itself is on Thursday, 10 December, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. The stream starts at 7:30 PM in New York, which is 6 AM IST on Friday, 11 December, and it is free on YouTube and Twitch.
