OpenAI has extended its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers programme to India, FoneArena and Careers360 report. Each grant gives a research team three ChatGPT Plus seats, free for 12 months: the applicant plus up to two collaborators.

To apply, you must be research faculty or a postdoctoral researcher in science, mathematics or engineering at an eligible institution, with a paper posted on arXiv, bioRxiv or ChemRxiv in the last three years, the reports say. OpenAI is giving 50,000 new grants worldwide, first come, first served.

The opening date differs by report. OpenAI's global notice of 16 November says the new round opens on 23 November; reports in India, citing the company, give 30 November. Until then, applications are closed.

ChatGPT Plus is widely reported to cost ₹1,999 a month in India, so three seats for a year would be worth about ₹72,000.
