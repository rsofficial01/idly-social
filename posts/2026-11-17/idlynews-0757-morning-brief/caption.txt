Morning Brief, Tuesday 17 November.

India play Pakistan in the final of the SAFF Championship, the men's football title of the South Asian Football Federation, at the National Stadium in Dhaka at 7:30 pm IST tonight. The All India Football Federation says it streams on the Veto app. India, the holders, are chasing a record-extending 10th title after beating Bhutan 2-1 in the semi-final; Pakistan, who beat hosts Bangladesh 1-0, are in their first final.

The Supreme Court has awarded ₹30 lakh to Devilal Gond, a Chhattisgarh man who stayed in jail for more than seven years after the High Court acquitted him in January 2018, because the order never reached the prison. He was freed in May 2025. The court noted that India has no law to compensate people for wrongful jailing.

Retired employees acquitted in a criminal case cannot have their gratuity held back just because the State has appealed the acquittal, the Supreme Court ruled on Monday, dismissing appeals by the Delhi Development Authority. The acquittal stands until a higher court overturns it; the two retirees have undertaken to refund the money with interest if it does.

PM Narendra Modi attends the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit, with the 11-nation Southeast Asian bloc, and the 21st East Asia Summit in Manila today. A Quad leaders' meeting of India, the US, Japan and Australia is planned on the sidelines, PTI reports; it has not been officially confirmed.

Iran's Guard Corps navy says sea mines have damaged six oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz since Sunday, AP reports; the claim has not been independently verified. Before the war, about a fifth of the world's oil passed through the strait.
