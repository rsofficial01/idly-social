## Story
Vivo has reportedly raised Indian prices by ₹5,000 on all three Vivo V80 variants (8+128GB ₹62,999 -> ₹67,999; 8+256GB ₹74,999; 8+512GB ₹84,999), about six weeks after the 6 Oct launch. The Vivo S2 is up ₹5,000 (8+128GB ₹47,999, ₹8,000 above its ₹39,999 August launch after a September rise; 8+256GB ₹53,999). The Vivo Y11 is up ₹3,000 (4+64GB ₹21,999). Vivo has not announced the change; the cover and slides say "Reportedly" / "Reported New Price". MyMobileIndia gives 15 Nov as the effective date; that date is not put on any slide.
