Vivo has raised the price of the V80 in India by ₹5,000 on every variant, My Mobile India, Mobiletelco and PaidFreeDroid report. Vivo has not announced the change itself. The 8GB+128GB model now costs ₹67,999 (was ₹62,999), the 8GB+256GB ₹74,999 and the 8GB+512GB ₹84,999. The phone launched on 6 October.

The Vivo S2 is reportedly up ₹5,000 too, to ₹47,999 for 8GB+128GB. That is ₹8,000 above its August launch price of ₹39,999, after an earlier rise in September. The Vivo Y11 is up ₹3,000, to ₹21,999 for 4GB+64GB.

The reports say the new prices apply in shops and online, and some put the rise down to costlier memory and storage chips.
