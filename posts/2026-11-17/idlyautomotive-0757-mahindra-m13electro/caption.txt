Mahindra Racing has revealed the M13Electro, its car for the 2027 Formula E season and the first built to the electric series' new Gen4 rules. Mahindra is the only Indian manufacturer in the championship.

These are the figures for Formula E's new Gen4 cars, per Mahindra and Formula E: up to 600 kW (about 804 bhp), 0-100 km/h in about 1.8 seconds, a top speed of 335 km/h and active all-wheel drive. Regenerative braking goes up to 700 kW.

Nyck de Vries and Edoardo Mortara drive for Mahindra for a fourth season together, and testing began on 16 November at Jarama, near Madrid. The season now starts in Mexico on 16 January 2027, Autocar India reports, after Formula E moved the Jeddah double-header from December to 16-17 April 2027.
