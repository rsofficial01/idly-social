Advance bookings for Avengers: Doomsday opened in India on the night of 16 November, a month before release. The film reaches Indian cinemas on Thursday, 17 December, a day before its US release on 18 December.

Shows from 12:01 AM on release day are already on sale, most of them in Infinity Vision 3D. Bollywood Hungama and Koimoi both report early shows filling fast, with listings in cities including Mumbai and Delhi-NCR. The film releases in English, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu.
