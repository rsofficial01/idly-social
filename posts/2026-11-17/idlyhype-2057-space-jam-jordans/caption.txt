Jordan Brand's Space Jam pairs are on Indian shelves as the film turns 30. These are the MRPs Superkicks and VegNonVeg list on Tuesday 17 November; both stores show the same price for each pair.

Air Jordan 1 Low SE "Magic Ball", the pair on the cover: ₹11,495, in Black, Metallic Silver and Action Green. Nike says its details celebrate 30 years of Space Jam.

Air Jordan 1 Mid SE "Space Jam": ₹12,495, in Black, Yellow Zest and Action Green.

Jordan Hydro XI "Space Jam" slides: ₹4,995, in Black, Varsity Royal, White and Blue Tint.

Jordan Brand says its 30th-anniversary Space Jam collection has been on sale since 5 November at jordan.com and select retailers.
