Triumph's new Street Triple 798 RS is priced at ₹14.39 lakh (ex-showroom) in India, Autocar India reports, and Triumph India's website lists it from ₹14,39,000. That is ₹1.2 lakh more than the Street Triple 765 RS, which Triumph India lists at ₹13.19 lakh.

Triumph says the new 798cc three-cylinder engine makes 135 PS (metric horsepower) and 86 Nm, and the bike weighs 183 kg wet. It also gets a 7-inch touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. No India delivery date has been announced yet.
