Samsung has raised Galaxy A phone prices in India again. From Monday, 16 November, the Galaxy A57 5G costs ₹66,999 for 8GB+256GB (was ₹62,999) and ₹76,499 for 12GB+256GB (was ₹72,499), TelecomTalk and Cashify report.

It is the A57's third price rise since August. The phone launched in India in March at ₹56,999, so the base model now costs ₹10,000 more than at launch.

Every other Galaxy A model is up ₹2,000 per variant. Base prices now: Galaxy A07 ₹22,999, A17 ₹29,999, A36 ₹38,999, A56 ₹46,999 and A37 ₹48,999. The Galaxy F17 and F70e also cost ₹2,000 more.
