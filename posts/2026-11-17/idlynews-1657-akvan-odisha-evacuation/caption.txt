Odisha has ordered the evacuation of people from landslide-prone areas in six southern districts, Ganjam, Gajapati, Kandhamal, Rayagada, Koraput and Malkangiri, as Cyclone Akvan moves towards the Andhra Pradesh coast, PTI reports. Collectors have been told to open relief centres. Ganjam's collector says 107 pregnant women have already been moved to safety and evacuation of landslide-prone areas began on Tuesday morning.

Disaster Management Minister Suresh Pujari says 74 rescue teams are being deployed, 60 from the state's disaster rapid action force and 14 from the NDRF, with the focus on preventing landslide deaths. Last month's Cyclone Arnab killed at least 11 people in Odisha, 10 of them in landslides in Ganjam and Kandhamal. Fishermen have been told not to go to sea from 18 to 20 November. The Bhubaneswar Meteorological Centre has issued orange (12-20 cm) and yellow (7-11 cm) rain warnings for Odisha districts for 18-21 November, and says it has issued no red warning.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) expects Akvan to cross the Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Tuni around noon on 20 November as a very severe cyclonic storm.
