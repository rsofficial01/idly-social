Law Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal says the Centre plans to pilot evening courts in Delhi, Bihar and Andhra Pradesh to clear pending cases. Courts that sit during the day would hold a few extra hours in the evening, and retired judges are expected to preside, he told The New Indian Express in an interview published on 10 November. The pilot would run with the respective High Courts. No start date, hours, budget or list of courts has been announced.

Speaking to reporters in Deoria, Uttar Pradesh, on 11 November, Meghwal said the Centre would launch the evening-court pilot and that more than 96,000 cases are pending in the Supreme Court. Across all courts, about 5.64 crore cases were pending, the Law Ministry told the Rajya Sabha on 23 July, citing National Judicial Data Grid figures; 4.98 crore of them were in district and subordinate courts.

Delhi's district courts began evening sittings in December 2024, but only for pending traffic challans. The planned pilot would use evening hours for pending cases in general.
