Godzilla Minus Zero, Takashi Yamazaki's follow-up to the Oscar-winning Godzilla Minus One, opens in Indian cinemas on Friday 20 November, IMAX screens included. Nayana Bijli of PVR INOX Pictures, which is releasing the film in India with Toho, confirmed the date to Bollywood Hungama on 11 November.

India was first meant to get it on 6 November, the day it opened in North America. Ramayana: Part One has a two-week hold on India's IMAX screens, so Minus Zero moved to 20 November, the same day The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opens in IMAX.

Godzilla Minus One never had a release in Indian cinemas; it reached Netflix here in June 2024.

Minus Zero opened at No. 1 in North America with $31.6 million, and its ¥910 million first day in Japan is the biggest opening day for a live-action film there. IMAX screens took $21.3 million worldwide over the opening weekend. It is also the first Japanese production Filmed for IMAX.
