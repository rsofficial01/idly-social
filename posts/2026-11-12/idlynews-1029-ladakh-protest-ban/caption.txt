Ladakh's Chief Electoral Officer, Rahul Sharma, has barred political parties, candidates and their workers from holding protests, dharnas, demonstrations, sit-ins, hunger strikes or rallies within the municipal limits of Leh and Kargil while the model code of conduct is in force. The order, issued under the J&K Municipal Committee (Election) Rules, 2003, says such activity could intimidate or unduly influence voters.

On Wednesday 11 November, Ladakh Police removed climate activist Sonam Wangchuk from a protest site in Leh and escorted him home. Police said he had been advised to stay at his residence and that they acted under orders regulating rallies and protests within Leh Municipality during the elections.

Wangchuk, the Leh Apex Body and the Kargil Democratic Alliance want the 4 December hill council elections deferred. Wangchuk says winter temperatures in parts of Ladakh fall below minus 30°C. Ladakh MP Haneefa Jan says many residents leave for warmer places in winter, and has asked for the polls to be postponed.

Municipal and panchayat polls are on 21 November; elections to eight hill councils follow on 4 December.
