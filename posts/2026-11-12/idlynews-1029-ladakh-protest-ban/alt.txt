Leh town in Ladakh by day: whitewashed houses and poplar trees climbing a dry hillside, with the white walls and stands of the town's polo ground below, under the headline "Ladakh Bans Poll Protests in Leh, Kargil Before 21 Nov". The next cards say the Chief Electoral Officer's order bars six kinds of political protest (protests, dharnas, demonstrations, sit-ins, hunger strikes and rallies) by parties, candidates and workers in Leh and Kargil, saying they could intimidate or unduly influence voters; that police escorted Sonam Wangchuk home from a Leh protest on 11 November; and that Wangchuk cites winter lows of minus 30°C in seeking to delay the 4 December hill council polls.
