The Election Commission of India's draft voter lists are due from today, 12 November, in 13 states and three Union Territories, under its Special Summary Revision (SSR) for 2027. A draft roll is the voter list before claims and objections are settled: the stage to get a name added, object to a wrong entry or correct your details before the final rolls on 4 January 2027.

Claims and objections are open until 11 December and are to be settled by 28 December. Uttar Pradesh's draft has moved to 17 November, with claims until 16 December, under a revised schedule its Chief Electoral Officer confirmed on 10 November.

The 16 are Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Gujarat, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Manipur, Puducherry, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Uttarakhand and West Bengal.

Form 6 is for adding a name, and DT Next and The South First report that people removed in the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) can apply again. Form 7 objects to an entry such as a dead or duplicate voter; Form 8 corrects details or records a change of address. Anyone aged 18 or more on 1 January 2027 can enrol now, and those turning 18 by 1 April, 1 July or 1 October 2027 can apply in advance.

The SSR is the routine revision of the existing roll, not a fresh house-to-house enumeration like the SIR. Six more, Arunachal Pradesh, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Mizoram, Odisha and Sikkim, get draft rolls by 30 November and final rolls on 21 January 2027.
