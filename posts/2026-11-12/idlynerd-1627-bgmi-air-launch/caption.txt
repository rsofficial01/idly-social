KRAFTON India has set Thursday, 10 December for BGMI Air, a lighter version of Battlegrounds Mobile India, after it passed 14 million pre-registrations on Google Play. Pre-registration opened on Apple's App Store on 11 November, so iPhone players can sign up too.

BGMI Air keeps the core battle royale gameplay with an initial download of about 1 GB, aimed at entry-level phones and players short on storage. It installs as a separate app alongside BGMI, and existing players don't need to transfer their accounts. KRAFTON India hasn't yet named the supported phones or system requirements.
