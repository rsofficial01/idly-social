The Gauhati High Court has held that a PAN card and a voter ID (EPIC) cannot, on their own, prove Indian citizenship unless the records used to issue them are also proved. A division bench of Justices Kalyan Rai Surana and Susmita Phukan Khaund said so on 5 November while upholding a Foreigners Tribunal opinion in a woman's case, LiveLaw and Scroll report.

The bench said both cards are issued on self-declaration, without formal inquiry by the Income Tax Department or the Election Commission. It found the petitioner had not proved her link to her claimed father and grandparents, or that the family lived continuously in Assam, as Section 6A of the Citizenship Act requires. Old voter lists and a ration card did not establish that link, and the original NRC acknowledgement was not produced, the court said.

The same court called the point "well settled" in an earlier case in July 2026, Pratidin Time and Hub Network reported.
