VinFast's seven-seat electric MPV, the VF MPV 7, costs ₹20.39 lakh ex-showroom, ₹4.1 lakh less than its ₹24.49 lakh launch price in April. VinFast cut the price on 4 November and no end date has been announced; CarWale and CarDekho still list ₹20.39 lakh this month.

It comes in one fully loaded variant. VinFast quotes a 60.13 kWh battery, 517 km of range (ARAI certified), a 10–70% DC fast charge in 30 minutes, a 150 kW, 280 Nm front motor, and up to 1,240 litres of luggage space with the rear rows folded. Autocar India linked the cut to slow sales since launch.
