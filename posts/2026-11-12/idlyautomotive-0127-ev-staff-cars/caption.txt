Electric cars are now the default when central ministries and departments buy staff cars. In an office memorandum dated 10 November, the Finance Ministry's Department of Expenditure said EVs "shall be the default and preferred choice for procurement" when old petrol and diesel staff cars are condemned or replaced. The rules took effect immediately.

The net dealer price cap for an electric staff car is ₹20 lakh, twice the ₹10 lakh cap that stays for petrol, diesel and hybrid cars.

To buy a petrol, diesel or hybrid car instead, a ministry needs a justified proposal approved by its Secretary, with its Financial Adviser's concurrence, and a case-by-case decision from the Department of Expenditure. The 1 Sep 2022 rule to hire EVs stays, and hiring a non-electric car goes through the same route.
