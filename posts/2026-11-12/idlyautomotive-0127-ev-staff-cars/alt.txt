Cover: an EESL Tata Tigor EV in Kerala government service at the Secretariat charging station, Thiruvananthapuram (2020). The white sedan, seen side-on from the rear, carries an EESL decal and an "e-car, e-Mobility Project by ANERT, Govt of Kerala" decal and is parked by a blue-and-white Delta EV charger under a canopy, with trees behind. Headline: EVs Become Default for Centre's Staff Cars, ₹20 Lakh Cap. Slide 2: ₹10 lakh, the unchanged cap for petrol, diesel and hybrid staff cars; electric staff cars may cost up to twice this; the EV default applies when old cars are replaced; in force immediately under the 10 Nov memo. Slide 3: three approvals to buy a petrol, diesel or hybrid car: the ministry's Secretary, its Financial Adviser and the Department of Expenditure.
