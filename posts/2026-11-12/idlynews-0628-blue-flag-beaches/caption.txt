Ladghar and Guhagar in Ratnagiri district and Shrivardhan in Raigad district have been certified as Blue Flag beaches for the 2026-27 season by the International Blue Flag Jury, Maharashtra's Environment and Climate Change Department says. The Blue Flag season at the three beaches begins on 15 November.

Across India, 22 beaches hold the Blue Flag for 2026-27: 13 renewed and nine new, according to the department. In October 2025, state minister Aditi Tatkare had announced Blue Flag status for five Maharashtra beaches, including these three (All India Radio).

The label, run by the Denmark-based Foundation for Environmental Education, is renewed every year. Beaches must keep bathing water tested, provide lifeguards or rescue equipment, and keep toilets, bins and recycling in place.
