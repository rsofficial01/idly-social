IIT Kharagpur will offer MD degrees from the 2026-27 session after approval from the National Medical Commission (NMC). Officials told PTI it is the first IIT to do so.

There are 12 seats, two each in General Medicine, Radiology, Physiology, Pathology, Community Medicine and Biochemistry. The first batch will be based at the institute's Dr B.C. Roy Multi-Speciality Medical Research Centre. Director Suman Chakraborty says the aim is an ecosystem "where clinical excellence meets engineering ingenuity".

Admission is through NEET-PG 2026 online counselling, and officials told PTI the seats are on offer in Round 2. On the Medical Counselling Committee's revised schedule, Round 2 registration closes at 3 pm on Saturday 14 Nov, choice filling and locking close at 11:55 pm on Sunday 15 Nov, and the final result is due on 17 Nov.
