Vu has priced its new Masterpiece AEye RGB MiniLED Google TVs from ₹75,000 for the 55-inch to ₹13,00,000 for the 115-inch, on its own store listing (12 Nov). In between: 65-inch ₹95,000, 75-inch ₹1,30,000, 85-inch ₹1,75,000 and 98-inch ₹2,95,000.

FoneArena reports the range is in retail stores across India from 12 November. Vu's online store listed every size as unavailable when we checked at 10:40 IST on Thursday.

Vu calls the range RGB MiniLED and says the backlight itself is made of red, green and blue LEDs, though its own product labels list the 55- to 98-inch sets as '4K QD MiniLED TV'. Vu says the sets cover 100% of the BT.2020 colour range. The 55- to 98-inch sets reach up to 2,500 nits with up to 2,000 dimming zones and 110W Dolby Atmos sound. The 115-inch goes to 3,000 nits, 2,696 zones and a 240W, 10-driver system. An ambient light sensor adjusts brightness and shifts to warmer tones as the evening goes on, Vu says. All of them run Google TV with Gemini.
