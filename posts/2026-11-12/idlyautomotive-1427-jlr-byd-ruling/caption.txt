A court in The Hague has ruled for BYD in an early round of Tata-owned JLR's case over the Ti7, the boxy seven-seater JLR says copies the Land Rover Defender.

In August, bailiffs acting for JLR seized Ti7 files from BYD's offices in Schiedam and Hoofddorp. On Wednesday (11 Nov), after BYD objected, the judge lifted that seizure and told JLR to have the files destroyed. JLR doesn't need them to argue that the Ti7 is a copy, the court said, or to work out damages, since BYD has never sold the Ti7 in the EU.

The ruling doesn't decide whether the Ti7 copies the Defender. JLR's design and copyright claims are still before courts in The Hague and Rotterdam.

The Ti7 goes on sale in the UK at £47,995, more than £20,000 below a plug-in Defender 110 (£71,840), with deliveries from January. In India, the Defender 110 is now assembled in Pune, and BYD's luxury brand Denza makes its India debut on Friday, 13 Nov.
