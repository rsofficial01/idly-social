Nvidia's shares fell about 8% in after-hours trading on Wednesday, 11 November (US time), to $159.51, after The Wall Street Journal reported that the company's board is preparing to remove co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang. The board could meet as soon as Wednesday to decide, the WSJ reported; Reuters, citing a source, said the board was meeting on his future.

Since 8 November, a committee of independent directors has been investigating recent reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, the board said. It added that it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia's secrets were compromised. No decision had been announced by 8 AM IST on 12 November, and analysts say Nvidia has no obvious successor.

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and has led it for 33 years. Nvidia's quarterly results are due on 17 November (US time), 3:30 AM IST on 18 November.
