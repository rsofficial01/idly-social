Cover: a 2016 Bureau of Immigration India "e-Tourist Visa" entry stamp from Kolkata airport in blue ink on a passport page, with a handwritten visa number and a red "16 DEC 2016" arrival date. Headline: "Tourist Visa Becomes 'Visitor Visa'; Forms Up to 70% Shorter". Detail slides: the V-1 Visitor Visa is valid 30 days with multiple entries and covers tourism plus business meetings, medical consultations, wellness retreats and short courses in Indian languages, while V-2 is valid one, five or 10 years; medical visas run one year with multiple entries by default, AYUSH treatment gets its own M-3 visa and attendants travel on M-2 and M-4 visas; main visa categories went from 13 to 9 and sub-categories from 49 to 23 after the 27 August cut, students coming through Study in India can get an e-S1 visa for up to five years, or the course length if shorter, and the new visas are not in use yet, with no start date notified.
