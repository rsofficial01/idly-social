Two text slides follow: the three cash limits and where each applies (up to ₹50,000 by NEFT from a bank branch; ₹5,000 a transfer through BC agents or ATMs, with the monthly cap per sender; ₹10,000 a pay-out and ₹25,000 a month per receiver); and 11 November, when the RBI's Domestic Money Transfer Directions, 2026 came into force, with what every sender must show: a verified mobile number and a self-certified ID, an extra authentication step on every transfer, and the sender's details travelling with the money, flagged as cash.
