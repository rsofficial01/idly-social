Global NCAP has rated the India-made Renault Kiger four stars for adult safety, up from two, and three stars for children. The car was built in India and tested as sold in South Africa, under the Safer Cars for Africa programme, so this is not an India rating. Global NCAP published the result on 10 November.

The Kiger it first tested had two airbags, and no electronic stability control (ESC) or ISOFIX child-seat mounts as standard. Renault then made six airbags, ESC and ISOFIX standard, and the upgraded car scored 25.20 out of 34 for adults and 30.21 out of 49 for children.

The weak spots: the bodyshell and footwell were rated unstable, meaning they could not take any more load in a harder crash, and one door opened in the side-impact test. The 3-year-old dummy scored zero in the side test, and there is no switch to turn off the front passenger airbag for a rear-facing child seat. The Nissan Magnite, built on the same platform, scored five stars in 2025.

In India, the Kiger starts at ₹5.81 lakh (ex-showroom), and Renault lists six airbags and electronic stability program (ESP) as standard.
