The Air Jordan 3 'Not Nice' (IF4396-001) is coming to India. Superkicks lists it at ₹19,295 in UK 7 to 11, marked 'Coming Soon' with a 21 November start date, the same day the pair releases in the US ($215) and on Nike's SNKRS in Japan (¥31,900).

Jordan Brand built it for the holidays, but for the naughty list: burnt-looking cracked leather and red-and-black elephant print on Michael Jordan's 1988 shoe. Flip the Nike Air heel tab and it reads 'Not Nice'; the insoles say Santa 'Checked It Twice'.
