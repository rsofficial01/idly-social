From Monday 16 November, realme's after-sales network in India grows to more than 670 service centres across 550+ cities. It is built on the existing service facilities of OPPO, and the centres keep realme branding. Realme owners can also use OPPO's upgraded repair centres in select locations.

More than 1,500 certified engineers will handle repairs with genuine spare parts, realme says. A monthly Service Day for both realme and OPPO owners waives labour charges and brings discounts on screen, battery and back-panel repairs, plus free cleaning, screen protector fitting and a software update. The company has not given the discount amounts or the day of the month.
