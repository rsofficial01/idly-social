Six months in, TVK has kept 2 of its 16 headline promises, and one of those is disputed in scope.

Our reading: relief you can see came first, and the promise that needs new money has not. TVK committed first to a ₹9,063 crore crop loan waiver, of which ₹6,220 crore is released, free power raised to 200 units a bill, and wider free buses for women. Every new government since 2011 made one costly early move. Nine of TVK's promises reuse or enlarge older schemes, five from Tamil Nadu's own parties and four from other states. The four it has not started are monthly cash and jobs, led by ₹2,500 for women.
