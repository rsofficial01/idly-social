GTA VI has passed an estimated 10 million pre-orders a week before launch, market data firm Sensor Tower says: about 10.31 million copies and more than $1 billion in pre-order sales.

That is up from Sensor Tower's 3 November estimate of 7.71 million copies and over $750 million. These are third-party estimates. Take-Two has not given a pre-order count.

If the estimate holds, GTA VI is past the 8 million pre-orders CD Projekt reported for Cyberpunk 2077 in 2020, when 59% of them were PC copies. GTA VI is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC date yet.

In India, the PlayStation Store lists the game for 12:00 am IST on Thursday, 19 November, with the Standard Edition at ₹5,999 and the Ultimate Edition at ₹7,499.
