Here are five of the new motorcycles Royal Enfield, Hero MotoCorp and TVS showed at EICMA 2026 in Milan this month, and what is known about India.

Royal Enfield Continental GT 750: the new 743cc parallel twin from the Himalayan 750, with twin exhausts. No power figure, price or India date yet.

Royal Enfield Himalayan 750: 55 hp and 63 Nm, from £6,999 in the UK. Autocar India says it could debut in India at Motoverse in late November.

Hero Xpulse 450: Hero's flagship adventure bike, a 421cc liquid-cooled single with 38 Nm, a six-speed gearbox and a 21-inch front wheel. It launches overseas first; Hero says India follows.

TVS Apache RTR 125: a 14.7 hp liquid-cooled 125, developed for Europe. TVS has not confirmed it for India.

Norton 450 Urban: a 454cc, 47 PS single from TVS-owned Norton. No India date or price announced.
