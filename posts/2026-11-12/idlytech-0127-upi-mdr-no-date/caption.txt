Diwali is over, and the 0.4% UPI fee on merchant payments above ₹2,000 still has no start date. It was due on 15 October. On 13 October, NPCI's UPI and Services Steering Committee pushed it to just after Diwali with the festive season in mind, and said the date would be notified separately. As of 10 November no date had been notified, Business Today and IANS report.

When it starts, the merchant pays, not the customer. NPCI's FAQ puts it at ₹12 on a ₹3,000 payment and ₹200 on ₹50,000, with a ₹300 cap from ₹75,000. Railway, fuel, insurance and telecom payments above ₹2,000 carry a flat ₹5 instead. The Finance Ministry says about 96% of UPI merchant payments won't be affected.

NPCI bars shops from passing the fee on to you and UPI apps from adding a platform fee, though Business Today notes shops could still adjust their prices. Transfers to friends and family stay free at any amount, and small merchants collecting up to ₹1 lakh a month through UPI QR codes stay exempt.

A January start has been reported, but NPCI hasn't confirmed it.
