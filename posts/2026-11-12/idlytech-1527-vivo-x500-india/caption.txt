vivo launches the X500, X500 Pro and X500 Pro Max in India on Thursday, 26 November, at 12 PM. vivo's India launch page lists the Pro Max with a 7,800mAh battery (typical; 7,605mAh rated), a 200MP ZEISS telephoto camera and a 6.85-inch screen, and the Pro with 6,510mAh (typical), a 64MP telephoto and a 6.36-inch screen. Both run MediaTek's Dimensity 9600 Pro and charge at 90W wired. A 400 mm ZEISS telephoto extender for the Pro Max is sold separately.

India prices have not been announced. Last year's X300 Pro launched in India on 2 December 2025 at ₹1,09,999.
