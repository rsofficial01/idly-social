What six months of TVK did to a household budget in Tamil Nadu.

Our reading: families gained on power and buses, and lost ground to prices. Free power doubled to 200 units and free bus travel widened. But prices were up 6.8% in a year by September, the most of any large state that month, and the ₹1,000 women receive, unchanged since 2023, now buys ₹886 at that year's prices.

The anger is over power cuts: night road blockades from June and about 30,000 complaint calls a day in September, by one report, though power ranks only 14th of 21 themes in the 10,314 comments we read. At 100 days, 58% rated the government good or very good; we found no large poll since.
