The rest of November in India's car and bike world, led by the Pune-assembled Land Rover Defender 110.

15 Nov: deliveries of the locally assembled Defender 110 begin. The 2.0 petrol starts at ₹99 lakh ex-showroom; the 3.0 petrol and diesel cost ₹1.18 crore, against ₹1.52 crore for the imported car. JLR builds it from kits at its Pune plant.

13 Nov: BYD's luxury brand Denza shows the D9 electric MPV and the Z9 GT in India. Prices haven't been announced.

25 Nov: the Toyota Innova Hycross facelift is reported for this date by Autocar India and Acko Drive. Toyota hasn't confirmed it.

27 Nov: the new-generation Mini Cooper SE electric launches, with a Paul Smith Edition. Prices haven't been announced.

27–29 Nov: Royal Enfield's Motoverse motorcycle festival at Vagator, Goa.
