Wildwood, the new stop-motion fantasy from LAIKA, the studio behind Coraline and Kubo and the Two Strings, opens in Indian cinemas on Friday, 13 November. At 2 hours 12 minutes, it is the longest stop-motion animated film ever made, and LAIKA's first film since Missing Link in 2019.

Travis Knight directs this adaptation of Colin Meloy and Carson Ellis's 2011 novel: when a flock of crows carries off Prue McKeel's baby brother, she follows them into a forbidden forest. Carey Mulligan, Mahershala Ali, Angela Bassett and Jacob Tremblay are among the voices.

On Rotten Tomatoes, 97% of 180 critics' reviews are positive. District lists it in English with a UA13+ rating, and Friday shows are open in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata.
