The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim turned 15 on Wednesday, 11 November; it first came out on 11 November 2011. Skyrim Special Edition is now 80% off on Steam India, at ₹359 instead of ₹1,799, until 18 November (Steam gives no end time). That price is for the PC game on Steam. The Anniversary Upgrade, which adds Creation Club quests, dungeons and gear, is not discounted and costs ₹899.

Fans hoping for news of the next game got none. Bethesda first announced The Elder Scrolls VI in 2018 and only revealed its title, Sentinel, and its logo on 3 November. Ahead of the anniversary, it said it "will not be sharing anything new on Sentinel for some time". No release date or platforms have been announced.
