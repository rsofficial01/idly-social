Morning Brief, Thursday 12 November.

Two express trains derailed on Wednesday, about 10 hours apart. Near Guwahati, the locomotive and five coaches of the Kolkata-Sairang Express came off the track at Digaru station around 6:54 am, the Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR) says, adding that the loco pilot "could not control the speed". NFR said no one was injured; Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma said four people had minor injuries. At Kanpur Central, four coaches of the Gorakhpur-bound Chauri Chaura Express derailed as it left the station around 5:05 pm; about 300 passengers in those coaches were safe, railway officials said, and the rest of the train was to continue its journey.

Delhi's 24-hour air quality index was 313, "Very Poor", on Wednesday, up from 299 on Tuesday, Central Pollution Control Board data show. Stage III pollution curbs have been in force across the capital region since 6 November: BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel four-wheelers are restricted in Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad and Gautam Buddh Nagar, and non-essential construction and demolition are banned. Classes up to Class 5 run in hybrid mode, so families can choose online lessons where schools offer them; Noida and Ghaziabad issued their orders on Wednesday.

In Jammu and Kashmir's Kishtwar district, two Special Police Officers have gone missing; police and the Army are searching for them.

Iran is waiting for Washington's official reply to its seven conditions, which include lifting the US naval blockade and sanctions and releasing frozen Iranian funds, Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf told Al Jazeera. He said the Strait of Hormuz will "never return" to its pre-war status.

US-brokered talks between Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi ended on Wednesday without a breakthrough, though both sides confirmed a large prisoner exchange. US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner travel to Moscow on Thursday.
