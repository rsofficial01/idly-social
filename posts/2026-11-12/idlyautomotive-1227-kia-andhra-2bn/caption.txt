Kia India will invest about $2 billion to expand its manufacturing in Andhra Pradesh, taking its yearly production capacity from 4.3 lakh cars to 6.3 lakh. MD and CEO Gwanggu Lee announced it on Thursday, 12 November, at the CII Partnership Summit in Visakhapatnam. Kia hasn't given a timeline or a start date.

Kia's existing factory at Anantapur has built cars since 2019, starting with the Seltos, and exports to about 100 countries.

The announcement follows Kia India's best month yet: 33,462 cars dispatched in October 2026, 13.2% more than a year earlier. Its January–October total of 2,86,470 has already passed all of 2025 (2,80,286).
