The Madras High Court has reserved orders on petitions challenging the National Green Tribunal's ban on new construction approvals within 1 km of the Pallikaranai marsh in South Chennai. The First Bench of Chief Justice Sushrut Arvind Dharmadhikari and Justice G. Arul Murugan heard CREDAI-Chennai and the Tamil Nadu State Wetlands Authority on Wednesday, 11 November, and gave no date for its ruling.

The NGT's Southern Zone bench passed the 1-km order in September 2025. The Wetlands Authority has proposed a smaller interim Zone of Influence of 128.19 hectares across 36 survey numbers in Pallikaranai, Sholinganallur, Karapakkam, Perungudi, Okkiyam Thoraipakkam and Perumbakkam, where building would be regulated site by site instead of banned and natural drains and inlet and outlet channels could not be blocked. CREDAI-Chennai asked the court to set aside the 1-km ban and send the question back to the tribunal. The judges noted that the Supreme Court is already hearing the wider question of protecting wetlands.

Pallikaranai Marsh is a Ramsar-listed wetland of 1,247.54 hectares. Its Ramsar site record and Citizen Matters both describe it as a flood buffer for Chennai that soaks up rainwater running off the surrounding city.
