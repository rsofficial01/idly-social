Neeman's, the homegrown sneaker and footwear brand, had sales of about ₹180 crore in FY26 (April 2025 to March 2026). Its filings show ₹180 crore of revenue from operations, up 73% from ₹104 crore (Entrackr, 12 November); the company itself has put the year at ₹185 crore (IMAGES BoF).

In the same year Campus Activewear, about 10 times bigger, grew 11.4% to ₹1,774 crore, with its sneaker range more than doubling. Neeman's opened its 50th store, in Panjagutta, Hyderabad, in October and says it plans 500 across India.
