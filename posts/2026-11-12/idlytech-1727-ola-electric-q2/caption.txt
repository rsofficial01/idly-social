Ola Electric's revenue from operations fell 39% to ₹421 crore in July–September 2026 (Q2 FY27), from ₹690 crore a year earlier, and 7% from ₹455 crore in April–June, in results reported on Thu 12 Nov.

Its loss for the quarter was ₹362 crore: 13% narrower than ₹418 crore a year ago, but 8% wider than ₹336 crore in April–June. That is about ₹86 lost for every ₹100 of revenue (idly's calculation).

Rival Ather Energy's revenue rose 63% to ₹1,464 crore in the same quarter, and it turned a profit of ₹1.26 crore. Ola's revenue is now about 29% of Ather's.

In October, Ola was fifth in electric two-wheeler registrations with 12,096 units (5.4% share, Vahan data), behind TVS (60,429), Bajaj (49,067), Ather (31,796) and Hero's Vida (29,901).

After the quarter, Ola allotted shares in a rights issue of about ₹1,000 crore.
