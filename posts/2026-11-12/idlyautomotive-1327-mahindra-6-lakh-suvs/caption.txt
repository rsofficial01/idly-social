Mahindra has sold 6,15,280 SUVs in India from January to October 2026, 19% more than the 5,18,321 it sold in the same ten months of 2025. It is the second calendar year in a row that it has passed 6 lakh. October was its best month ever: 73,338 SUVs sent to dealers.

The fight for India's No. 2 carmaker is still open. Mahindra outsold Tata Motors in 2025, ending the year at a record 6,25,603. This year Tata is second only to Maruti Suzuki, with 6,49,477 cars and SUVs by October, 34,197 ahead of Mahindra. Tata also edged Mahindra in October, by just 556.

The Scorpio is Mahindra's biggest seller: the Scorpio N and Scorpio Classic together sold 19,353 in October, India's ninth best-selling car. Autocar India expects Mahindra to pass 7 lakh SUVs this year.
