The 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF) opens to the general public on Thursday 19 November at Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, New Delhi, and runs until Friday 27 November, according to the India Trade Promotion Organisation (ITPO), which organises it. The fair is formally inaugurated on 13 November, and 14 to 18 November are business days.

Entry costs more this year. Reports citing ITPO say a weekday adult ticket is now ₹100 (₹80 last year) and a weekend adult ticket ₹200 (₹150). Children pay ₹50 on weekdays and ₹100 on weekends, and business-day adult tickets stay at ₹500. ITPO executive director Premjit Lal has said the new rates are meant to round off prices, not to raise revenue. Senior citizens and persons with disabilities enter free on public days with a government ID.

The fair is open from 10 AM to 7:30 PM, with the last public entry at 5:30 PM. Tickets are sold online and at 55 Delhi Metro stations. Visitors enter through gates 3 and 4 on Bhairon Road and gates 6 and 10 on Mathura Road.

ITPO says more than 3,500 exhibitors will take part. Bihar, Maharashtra, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh are the partner states, and Assam, Delhi, Jharkhand and West Bengal the focus states. This year's theme is "Vikas aur Virasat".

The cover photo, released by PIB in July 2023 before the complex opened, shows Bharat Mandapam's convention centre at dusk.
