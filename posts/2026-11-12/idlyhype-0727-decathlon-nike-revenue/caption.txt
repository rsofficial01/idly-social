Decathlon's India business brought in about three times Nike India's revenue in FY25, the year to March 2025: ₹4,133 crore against about ₹1,380 crore. adidas India and Puma India each reported just over ₹3,000 crore, though their financial years don't line up exactly.

Decathlon Sports India has since reported FY26: revenue up 8.35% to ₹4,478 crore, EBITDA of ₹424 crore and a net profit of ₹63 crore, after a ₹65 crore loss in FY25. Nike India hasn't reported FY26 yet.

Decathlon runs 132 stores in India and plans about 200 by 2030. About half the footwear it sells here is made in India.
