Apple HomeView goes on sale in India on Friday, 13 November, at ₹39,900 for both the tabletop and the wall-mounted model. The wall model needs one of three Schneider Home mounts, sold separately: ₹6,900 (USB-C), ₹8,900 (AC wired) or ₹14,900 (PoE, due early 2027). With the cheapest mount, a wall-mounted HomeView costs at least ₹46,800 (idly's sum of Apple's prices).

It pairs a 7-inch screen with a speaker base, recognises up to six people by face and gives each their own home screen, shows up to nine home camera feeds at once, and controls HomeKit and Matter lights, locks and thermostats. Setup needs a compatible iPhone or iPad, and Siri AI on it works in English in India. In the US, it costs $299.
