Xbox Cloud Gaming now has a free tier: you can stream select games you own, and some free-to-play games, after watching ads, with no Game Pass subscription. Pure Xbox reported that it went public on 11 November, and Xbox's India cloud gaming page lists it "where available", with session limits.

Microsoft is also rolling out monthly cloud limits for Game Pass. Xbox's India store, read on 12 November, lists Game Pass Essential at ₹499 a month with 5 hours of cloud play, Premium at ₹699 with 10 hours and Ultimate at ₹1,089 with 15 hours. Extra hours are sold on the Xbox Store and stay in your account until used.

Microsoft says it expects the limits to affect 4% of Game Pass subscribers. The rollout is uneven so far: Pure Xbox's own Ultimate account still showed unlimited streaming on 11 November.

Xbox Cloud Gaming launched in India in November 2025.
