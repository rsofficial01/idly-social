Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, the remake of the 1996 game that made Lara Croft famous, is beating its pre-order targets, its owner says. Embracer Group, which owns developer Crystal Dynamics, said in its results on 11 November that wishlists and pre-orders "continue to exceed our targets". It gave no numbers.

The remake launches on 12 February 2027 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC (Steam), published by Amazon Game Studios. Steam India lists the Standard Edition at ₹3,799 and the Deluxe Edition at ₹5,499.

The next game, Tomb Raider: Catalyst, takes Lara across Northern India, and Amazon calls it the largest Tomb Raider game ever made. Amazon's head of games first signalled a move from 2027 to 2028 in a July interview. Amazon's own update of 9 November now lists 2028 and adds a Nintendo Switch 2 version, alongside PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Steam.
