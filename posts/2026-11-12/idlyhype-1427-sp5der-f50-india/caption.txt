Rapper Young Thug's label SP5DER and adidas Originals have two new F50 Formotion pairs, and they're coming to India. Superkicks lists both at ₹19,999 MRP, marked coming soon, for 14 Nov: the day adidas launches them worldwide through its CONFIRMED app, select stores and retailers. SP5DER's own site gets them a day early, on 13 Nov.

The pairs are Solar Green with black stripes (KZ6581) and glossy red with pink accents (KZ6578). The shoe takes the lines of the Adizero F50 III football boot and sets them on adidas running tooling. adidas prices the footwear at €200; the matching tracksuit, tees, backpack and balaclava run €70 to €140.

Round one's silver F50 Formotion, out since July, is listed at ₹18,999 on VegNonVeg.
