Resident Evil Requiem is the Ultimate Game of the Year at the Golden Joystick Awards 2026, held on Wednesday, 11 November. Capcom's survival horror game won 17% of the public vote, one point ahead of Phantom Blade Zero (16%), with Crimson Desert third.

Requiem won four awards in all: Ultimate Game of the Year, Console Game of the Year (27%), Best Game Trailer, and Best Lead Performer for Angela Sant'Albano as Grace Ashcroft. Capcom was voted Studio of the Year with 57%, and its Onimusha: Way of the Sword won Best Audio Design.

Crimson Desert won PC Game of the Year, Dispatch won Best Storytelling and Best Soundtrack, Big Walk took the Critics' Choice award and The Witcher 4 was voted Most Wanted. Self-published indie Meccha Chameleon matched Requiem's four awards, including Best Multiplayer Game. GTA 6, out 19 November, wasn't on the shortlist.

Steam India prices on 12 November: Resident Evil Requiem ₹4,399, Crimson Desert ₹4,499, Phantom Blade Zero ₹4,299, Dispatch ₹1,300, Big Walk ₹999 and Meccha Chameleon ₹309.
