The Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" is on sale at retail for the first time since 2014. In India it is ₹18,395 MRP at both Superkicks and VegNonVeg (style CT8529-105).

Nike calls the Jordan 6 Michael Jordan's "championship game shoe", and "White Infrared" is one of its original 1991 colourways. This pair brings back the Nike Air logo on the heel, which the 2010 and 2014 retros replaced with a Jumpman.

Sizes on 12 Nov: Superkicks had UK 7–12 in stock and VegNonVeg lists UK 7–11. The global release was 7 Nov at $215 on SNKRS. Resale pairs from 2014 carry the older style code 384664-123.
