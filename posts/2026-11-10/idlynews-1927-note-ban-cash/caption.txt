Ten years after the 2016 note ban, currency with the public, the cash held outside banks, stood at ₹42.56 lakh crore on 15 October 2026, RBI data show. On 28 October 2016, 11 days before the ban, it was about ₹17 lakh crore.

The ₹500 and ₹1,000 notes withdrawn on 8 November 2016 were about 86% of the cash in circulation by value. Cash with the public fell to about ₹7.8 lakh crore in December 2016. The RBI later counted 99.3% of the scrapped notes back in the banking system: ₹15.31 lakh crore of ₹15.42 lakh crore.

In the year to 15 October 2026, cash with the public rose 14.1%, by ₹5.27 lakh crore.
