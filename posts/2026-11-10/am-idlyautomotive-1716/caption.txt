The probe by US safety regulator NHTSA covers about 1.2 million Explorers from the 2021 to 2026 model years. Reports allege seat-mounted side airbags caused the burns when they deployed, and say some of those hurt were police officers. Third-degree is the most severe kind of burn.

It's an early-stage investigation. It can lead to a recall if regulators find a defect, but there's no word yet on a recall or on when NHTSA will decide, and the sources don't quote Ford on the allegations.

The Explorer isn't a mainstream model in India, so owners in the US are the ones most affected.
