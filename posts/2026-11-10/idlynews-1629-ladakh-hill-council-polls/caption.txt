Ladakh will elect all eight of its Autonomous Hill Development Councils on 4 December. Under the schedule the State Election Commission, Ladakh, announced on 10 November, nominations close on 17 November, scrutiny is on 18 November and withdrawals close on 20 November. Polling, where contested, runs from 8 am to 5 pm, and the whole process is to end by 8 December.

Ladakh had two hill councils, in Leh and Kargil. An amendment to the councils' regulation in October allows one in each of its eight districts, including the new Sankoo district, with 100 elected seats across the eight.

Kargil observed a shutdown on 10 November, called by political parties and religious groups. Reports say the protest was against holding the polls in winter and against the delimitation of seats. Groups including the Leh Apex Body and the Kargil Democratic Alliance want the vote held after winter. Separate panchayat polls in Ladakh are on 21 November.
