Elon Musk says Tesla will refund some Cybertruck deposits, as the company prepares to stop making the pickup in about eight months.

Musk defended ending production. He said Tesla holds more orders than it can build before mid-2027, but didn't say how many orders it has, how many deposits will be refunded, or which buyers will get their money back.

The Cybertruck isn't sold in India, and most of its deposit holders are in the US.
