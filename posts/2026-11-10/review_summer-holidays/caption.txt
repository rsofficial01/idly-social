Summer Holidays, the critics' verdict: 5.3/10. Critics find this 1990s childhood film gentle and well-meaning, with likeable young leads and pretty village visuals. All four flag the same weak spot: the father's sudden change of heart. Great Andhra's Venkat Arikatla: "Pleasant moments, but little drama".
Who should watch: In theatres. A gentle, simple film for families with children.

Highs: Warm 1990s childhood nostalgia · The two young leads · Pretty village visuals and pleasant music
Lows: The father's sudden change doesn't convince · A familiar, predictable story · The emotional beats need more weight
