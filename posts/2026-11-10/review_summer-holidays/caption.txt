Summer Holidays, the verdict. A small Telugu film about two boys and a summer at their grandmother's village, and critics found it warm and nostalgic.
The catch, they agree: you can see most of it coming.

Highs: Warm, nostalgic village-summer feel · Good performances, led by the kids · A rare children's film in Telugu
Lows: A predictable, routine story · The emotional beats don't fully land · Problems get solved too conveniently

Consolidated score: 5.5/10, the average of 4 published ratings from Indian outlets, each converted to /10. The Hindu: "a simplistic yet heartfelt ode to rural nostalgia".
