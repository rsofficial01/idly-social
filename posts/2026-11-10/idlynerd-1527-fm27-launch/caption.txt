Football Manager 27 launches today, 10 November, with every digital version out on the same day. On Steam India it lists at ₹4,499 for Windows and Mac. It is also on the Epic Games Store and the Xbox PC app, and Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass members get it from day one.

FM27 Console arrives on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, including Game Pass Console, with a boxed PS5 copy following on 20 November. The phone version is a separate edition, FM27 Mobile, on iOS and Android through Netflix. Another edition, FM27 Touch, comes to Apple Arcade on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Sports Interactive lists five new features: Cup Draws and Transfer Deadline Day events, an overhauled news feed, full-screen navigation with fewer clicks, and match days with dynamic crowds, weather and cut scenes.
