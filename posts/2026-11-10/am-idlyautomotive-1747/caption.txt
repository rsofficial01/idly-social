Deliveries of the Honda Rebel 300 have begun in India, with the first units reaching Honda BigWing dealerships in Delhi. The cruiser costs ₹2.22 lakh ex-showroom, or ₹2.62 lakh with Honda's E-Clutch, which lets riders start, stop and change gear without using the clutch lever.

It uses a 286cc liquid-cooled single-cylinder engine (27.6 PS, 24.5 Nm) with a six-speed gearbox, dual-channel ABS, a 690 mm seat height and an 11.2-litre tank. Honda hasn't published a national delivery schedule, so buyers should check stock with their nearest BigWing dealer.
