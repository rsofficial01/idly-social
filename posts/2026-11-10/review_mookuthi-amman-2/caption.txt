Mookuthi Amman 2, the verdict. Critics agree Nayanthara holds her own, and on very little else.
The screenplay, the VFX and the comedy take most of the blame.

Highs: Nayanthara holds it together (the only high most critics agree on)
Lows: A weak screenplay with no emotional stakes · Sloppy, overused VFX · The comedy misfires

Consolidated score: 3.8/10, the average of 4 published ratings from Indian outlets, each converted to /10. The Times of India: "an overstuffed divine mess of a sequel".
