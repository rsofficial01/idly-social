Mookuthi Amman 2, the critics' verdict: 3.3/10. The lowest-rated release of the Diwali window. Critics say Nayanthara holds the screen, but cheap-looking VFX, a weak, overstuffed screenplay and comedy that misfires sink it. NDTV's Latha Srinivasan: "desperately needed a storytelling miracle".
Who should watch: In theatres, but critics suggest it only for committed Nayanthara or Amman-film fans.

Highs: Nayanthara holds the screen · A bigger canvas and a grand look · Regina Cassandra adds some appeal
Lows: The VFX look cheap, even AI-made · A weak, overstuffed screenplay · The comedy misfires

Score: 3.3/10, the average of 9 published critic ratings, each converted to /10 (mean 3.3333, rounded half-up to one decimal). Highest: DT Next 5/10. Lowest: Rediff 2/10 and Gulte 1/5 (each 2.0/10). Only reviews published on or after the 6 Nov release count. idly has not watched the film; this post brings together published reviews.
