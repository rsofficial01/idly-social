Dyson has launched two bladeless fans in India. The Hot+Cool HF1, which heats in winter and cools in summer, costs ₹38,900. The Cool tower fan, which only cools, costs ₹27,900. Both are available on Dyson.in and at Dyson Demo stores.

Dyson says the HF1 can warm a whole room by 1°C in as little as 100 seconds, and that it is 25% quieter than its predecessor. It heats up to 32°C, has 10 airflow speeds and 70° oscillation, switches off if knocked over, and works from a remote or the MyDyson app.

The ₹27,900 Cool tower fan has no heater and no app. It has 10 airflow settings, a remote and a sleep timer.
