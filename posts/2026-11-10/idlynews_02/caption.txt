Karnataka says some E20 petrol it tested held about 31% ethanol. E20 is meant to hold 20%. Now Kerala is collecting random samples from its fuel stations to check.

The Union oil ministry rejects Karnataka's finding: it says 893 E20 samples from public sector outlets in 27 states and UTs all met the standard. Kerala hasn't said when its results will be out.
