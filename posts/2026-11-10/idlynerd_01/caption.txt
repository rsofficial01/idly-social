Ranabaali streams on Netflix from Friday, 13 November, in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam.

Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna lead, Rahul Sankrityan directs, and Ajay-Atul did the music. It made around ₹80 crore worldwide in theatres.
