Jio's new ₹10,000 prepaid plan covers three years on one recharge. That works out to about ₹278 a month.

You get 1,095 days, 2.5GB of data a day (2,737.5GB in all), unlimited calls, 100 SMS a day and unlimited 5G where eligible, plus JioHotstar, JioTV, 50GB of JioAICloud storage and 12 months of Canva Pro Lite. It's on Jio's online and offline recharge channels; check the terms in the Jio app before you buy.
