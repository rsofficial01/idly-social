Tyler, The Creator's low-top take on his Converse Bronco Boot is on sale in India. Converse India's online store lists four colours at ₹11,499 each: Cocoa Brown/Rubber/Carafe, Eden/Rubber/Partridge, Black/Vanilla Ice/Black and New Wheat/Curry/Partridge. Eden and Black come in UK sizes 4 to 11; Cocoa Brown and New Wheat start at UK 7.

Superkicks lists three of them (Eden, Black and New Wheat) at the same price, taxes included. Converse calls it Tyler's third drop in its Converse 1908 line, built on a '90s Converse boot with a rubber toe and a lug sole.

Lug-soled boots in Indian weather: are you in, or is it Chucks all year round?
