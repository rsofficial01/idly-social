Aadarsha Kutumbam House No: 47, the critics' verdict: 5.2/10. Critics enjoy the breezy first half and Venkatesh in easy form. Most say the second half, after the turn to crime and action, loses steam. The Hollywood Reporter India's Sruthi Raman: "A little too familiar to stand out.".
Who should watch: In theatres. A light family outing for Venkatesh fans; critics say the first half is the draw.

Highs: A breezy, funny first half · Venkatesh is in easy form · Satya's comedy lands
Lows: The second half loses steam · A familiar, predictable story · The music is forgettable

Score: 5.2/10, the average of 9 published critic ratings, each converted to /10 (mean 5.1667, rounded half-up to one decimal).
