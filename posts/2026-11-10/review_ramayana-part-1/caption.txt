Ramayana: Part 1, the critics' verdict: 7.0/10. They agree on the scale and on Yash's Ravana, the best thing in it. They split on Ranbir's Rama, and several fault the editing and the music.

Highs: Yash's Ravana is the film's biggest strength · The VFX and sheer scale impress · The Jatayu-Ravana face-off stands out
Lows: Ranbir's Rama feels tentative next to Ravana · Choppy editing and uneven pacing · The music doesn't match the ambition

Score: 7.0/10, the average of 5 published critic ratings, each converted to /10. Highest: Bollywood Hungama, 5/5 (published 31 Oct, before release). Lowest: Outlook, 2.5/5. The Quint: "A feast for the eyes, but not the heart".
