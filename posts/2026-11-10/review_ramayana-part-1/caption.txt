Ramayana: Part 1, the verdict. Critics agree it sets a new bar for scale in Indian cinema, and that Yash's Ravana is the best thing in it.
The doubts are about the 195-minute runtime, the music, and a Rama drawn far thinner than his rival.

Highs: Yash's Ravana steals the film · Scale and VFX set a new bar for Indian cinema · The Jatayu sequence is a standout
Lows: At 3h15m, the pacing drags in stretches · Rama gets far less depth than Ravana · The music and score underwhelm

Consolidated score: 6.9/10, the average of 7 published ratings from Indian outlets, each converted to /10. Highest: Bollywood Hungama, 5/5. Lowest: Outlook, 2.5/5. Scroll: "a sleek and sumptuous spectacle".
