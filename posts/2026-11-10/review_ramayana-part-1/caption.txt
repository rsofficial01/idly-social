Ramayana: Part 1, the critics' verdict: 7.1/10. Critics agree that Yash's Ravana and the visuals are the reasons to go, and most say the spectacle outweighs the emotion. They split on Ranbir's Rama. Rediff's Sukanya Verma: "all scale, no soul".
Who should watch: In theatres. Go for the spectacle and Yash's Ravana; critics say expect more awe than emotion.

Highs: Yash's Ravana is the standout · The visuals and sheer scale impress · The Zimmer-Rahman background score works
Lows: Spectacle outweighs emotion and character · Uneven pacing and choppy editing · The songs don't stand out

Score: 7.1/10, the average of 21 published critic ratings, each converted to /10 (mean 7.0952, rounded half-up to one decimal). Highest: Times of India 9/10, DNA India 9/10 and Koimoi 9/10. Lowest: Rediff 5/10 and Outlook 2.5/5 (each 5.0/10). Only reviews published on or after the 5 Nov release count. idly has not watched the film; this post brings together published reviews.
