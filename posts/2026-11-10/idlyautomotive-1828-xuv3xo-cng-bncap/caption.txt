Mahindra's XUV 3XO ZNG, the CNG version of its compact SUV, has a 5-star crash-safety rating from Bharat NCAP, the Indian government's crash-test programme. Mahindra announced the result on 6 November.

It scored 29.36 out of 32 for adult occupant protection and 45 out of 49 for child occupant protection. Six airbags, electronic stability control and ISOFIX child-seat mounts are standard, and if a gas leak is detected, the car switches to petrol on its own. Mahindra says it is India's first sub-compact CNG SUV with a 5-star Bharat NCAP rating.

The ZNG launched on 15 October in four variants, priced from ₹9.99 lakh to ₹12.27 lakh ex-showroom. Its twin CNG cylinders sit under the floor, leaving 270 litres of boot space. Mahindra claims 24.7 km/kg, with the same 82 kW and 200 Nm on CNG as on petrol.
