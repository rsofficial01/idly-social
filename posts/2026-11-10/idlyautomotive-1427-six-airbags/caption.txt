Six airbags on every variant, base model included, start at ₹3.70 lakh in India. These are the five cheapest cars that fit all six as standard, by starting ex-showroom price:

Maruti Suzuki Alto K10: ₹3.70 lakh
Maruti Suzuki Celerio: ₹4.70 lakh
Tata Tiago: ₹4.77 lakh
Maruti Suzuki WagonR: ₹4.99 lakh
Maruti Suzuki Eeco (passenger van): ₹5.28 lakh

Two airbags are still standard on the Maruti S-Presso (from ₹3.50 lakh) and the base Renault Kwid Evolution (from ₹4.53 lakh). The Kwid gets six only on the Climber, from ₹5.15 lakh, so it is not on the list. Taxi versions are left out.

Airbags are one part of a car's safety. They are not a crash-test rating.
