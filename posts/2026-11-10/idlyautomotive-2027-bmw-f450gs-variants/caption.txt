BMW Motorrad India has added a fourth version of the F 450 GS: the Sport, at ₹5.90 lakh ex-showroom. It sits between the Exclusive and the GS Trophy and gets the Trophy's sport suspension, Gear Shift Assistant Pro and Riding Modes Pro, in Racing Red. It does without the Easy Ride Clutch, which only the Trophy gets.

The range now reads Base ₹5.35 lakh, Exclusive ₹5.55 lakh, Sport ₹5.90 lakh and GS Trophy ₹6.15 lakh, all ex-showroom. At the April launch, the Base cost ₹4.70 lakh, the Exclusive ₹4.90 lakh and the Trophy ₹5.30 lakh. All four use the same 420cc parallel twin with 48 hp and 43 Nm.
