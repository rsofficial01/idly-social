Hoka x Marni round two: the Clifton Marni drops on Thursday, 12 November.

It's built on the Hoka Clifton 11 and comes in orange, white or black, with two extra sets of laces, metal lace hardware and a checked insole made for the shoe. Marni's site and stores get it first on 12 Nov, then Hoka and select retailers on 13 Nov. Price hasn't been announced, so check retailer listings closer to launch.
