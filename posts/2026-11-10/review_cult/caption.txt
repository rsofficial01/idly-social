#Cult, the critics' verdict: 4.1/10. Critics credit Vishwak Sen's swagger and Ravi Basrur's score, but say the gore has no impact and the suspense never builds.
Who should watch: In theatres. Only for slasher fans with a strong stomach, critics suggest.

Highs: Vishwak Sen's swagger in the action scenes · Ravi Basrur's background score · A bold, fresh premise on paper
Lows: Gore without impact · Little suspense; the killer is easy to guess · A slow start and an overlong run

Score: 4.1/10, the average of 5 published critic ratings, each converted to /10 (mean exactly 4.1). Highest: 123Telugu 2.75/5. Lowest: New Indian Express 3/10. Only reviews published on or after the 30 Oct release count. idly has not watched the film; this post brings together published reviews.
