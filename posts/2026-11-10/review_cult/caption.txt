Cult, the verdict. Vishwak Sen directs and stars in a single-location slasher, and critics credit the idea, his action and the final stretch.
What they don't find: tension. Most say the games and the gore never add up to a thriller.

Highs: Vishwak Sen's action scenes have energy · The final stretch works · A bold, single-location slasher idea
Lows: The survival games have no real tension · Gore and violence for their own sake · Predictable, with loose editing

Consolidated score: 4.3/10, the average of 5 published ratings from Indian outlets, each converted to /10. The Hindu: "ambitious but lacks finesse".
Box office: a weak start, per Deccan Chronicle and Gulte.
