Apple's MacBook Pro with a touchscreen and a tandem OLED display, which Apple calls PROLED XDR, is now in Indian stores. Apple says the new MacBook Pro reaches customers, Apple Stores and Authorised Resellers from 9 November. It is also sold on Apple's online store.

India prices (Apple):
14-inch, M5 Pro: ₹3,49,900
16-inch, M5 Pro: ₹4,09,900
M5 Max models: from ₹5,49,900
Education pricing: ₹3,31,900 (14-inch, M5 Pro) and ₹3,85,900 (16-inch, M5 Pro)

Apple says this is the first time a Mac supports touch, and that touch adds to the keyboard and trackpad rather than replacing them. The model also brings the Dynamic Island to the Mac, and Apple claims up to 24 hours of battery life.
