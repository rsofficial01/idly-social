WhatsApp Events now works for people who aren't on WhatsApp.

Create an event, add a cover photo and send the invite link to anyone. They can RSVP without installing the app or creating an account, and you see who's coming. There's also a new Events Home and custom reminders. It reaches all users in the coming weeks.
