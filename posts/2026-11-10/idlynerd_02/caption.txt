Demonte Colony 3 now has a release window: Pongal 2027.

Arulnithi and Priya Bhavani Shankar lead the third film in the horror franchise, written and directed by R. Ajay Gnanamuthu, with music by Sam CS. It was first due in September 2026 but was postponed after the Madras High Court issued an interim injunction in a production rights dispute. The makers haven't fixed an exact Pongal date yet, and promotions are expected to start soon.
