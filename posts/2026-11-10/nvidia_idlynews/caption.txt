Nvidia lost about $192 billion in market value on Monday, Nov 9, as its shares fell 4.27% to $178.67.

On Sunday (U.S. time), its board opened an independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang, run by independent directors with outside counsel. In a memo to staff, Huang wrote: "I was unfaithful to my wife." Fortune and CBS News link the affair to Alice Huang (no relation), a former Megaspeed executive whom U.S. intelligence agencies reportedly believe is a Chinese spy. No charges have been reported, and China's foreign ministry calls the "spy narrative" groundless.

The board says it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. Huang remains CEO, and no findings or timeline have been announced. On Monday, US President Donald Trump said the FBI is looking into it.

Dates to watch: Nvidia's earnings on Nov 17 (3:30 AM IST, Nov 18), and a closed-door House briefing on Nov 18 that three committee chairs have requested.
