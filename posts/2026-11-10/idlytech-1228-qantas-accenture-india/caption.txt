Qantas says around 850 corporate roles will be affected over the coming years under a new multi-year partnership with Accenture. Some of the services those teams provide will move to Accenture and, Qantas says, be delivered mostly by Accenture's teams in India, with some work staying in Australia. Qantas says that work will later be changed further with automation and AI.

The first changes are expected by mid-2027, starting with finance, procurement, people (HR) and marketing. Qantas says it runs on more than 1,000 legacy applications, and that it will consult staff and their union and redeploy people where it can. The Australian Services Union says most of the affected jobs are at Qantas's head office in Mascot, Sydney.

Chief executive Vanessa Hudson told staff the changes are about "modernising the technology behind our business and becoming more efficient". The union's Scott Cowen said: "Earlier this year, Qantas told us directly there was no plan to offshore any jobs."
