Every Diwali release, rated: 8 Indian films in theatres from 30 Oct to 9 Nov, ranked by the average of 72 published critic ratings. Ramayana: Part 1 (7.1) edges Scene (6.8); Mookuthi Amman 2 (3.3) sits clearly last.

1. Ramayana: Part 1 7.1 (21 ratings): Big-screen spectacle; expect more awe than emotion.
2. Scene 6.8 (13 ratings): Dark-comedy fans; Suriya sends up his own image.
3. Pyre 6.3 (4 ratings): A quiet, sad drama about growing old.
4. Summer Holidays 5.3 (4 ratings): Families with children; gentle and simple.
5. Aadarsha Kutumbam 5.2 (9 ratings): Venkatesh fans; the first half is the draw.
6. Nakhrewaalii 4.6 (7 ratings): An undemanding rom-com, if you like the leads.
7. #Cult 4.1 (5 ratings): Slasher fans with a strong stomach only.
8. Mookuthi Amman 2 3.3 (9 ratings): Only for committed Nayanthara fans, critics suggest.

Score: for each film, the mean of its ratings from established outlets, each converted to /10 and rounded half-up. A film needs at least 4 ratings, published on or after release. idly has not watched these films; this post brings together published reviews.
