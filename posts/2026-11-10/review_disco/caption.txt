Disco, the verdict. The lowest-rated Diwali release in our count: critics found a slick-looking Malayalam gangster film with little underneath.
All five reviews we read call out the violence and the script.

Highs: Stylish, technically polished filmmaking (the only high most critics agree on)
Lows: Violence for its own sake · A weak, muddled script · Overlong and exhausting

Consolidated score: 2.7/10, the average of 3 published ratings from Indian outlets, each converted to /10. Cinema Express: "a hollow actioner drowning in its own violence".

Is style ever enough for you when the story isn't there?

Sources: Reviews consolidated from The Indian Express, Cinema Express and The Times of India; unrated reviews from The Hindu and The Hollywood Reporter India.
