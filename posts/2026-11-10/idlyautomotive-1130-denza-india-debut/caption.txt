BYD's luxury brand Denza makes its India debut on Friday, 13 November, with the Z9 GT electric shooting brake. The D9 luxury MPV is expected alongside it, and Denza sits above BYD's current Indian range.

The top Z9 GT runs three motors. Autocar India expects up to 1,156hp for India, and Denza claims 0–100kph in 2.7 seconds. BYD claims its flash charging takes the battery from 10% to 97% in about 9 minutes, at up to 1,500kW on its Chinese-market chargers.

Prices are not out yet. Outlets expect ₹1.2–1.5 crore ex-showroom for the Z9 GT.
