Casio's G-SHOCK 2500 series is priced in India at ₹9,995 to ₹26,995; Casio India announced it on 23 October. The resin GAC-2500 starts at ₹9,995; the metal-clad GMC-2500 is the dearer build.

The watches are Casio's analogue take on the DW-5000C, the first G-Shock, from 1983. Casio keeps the original's form with softer, rounder edges. A three-cell window at 3 o'clock shows the date, stopwatch minutes or settings, and once it's set, the calendar handles month-ends and leap years by itself. Both builds are rated to 20 bar (200 m).
