The Vegam Run, the first running shoe Nike built for India, has been on sale in India since 1 November. Its price in India is ₹3,795, as reported by Esquire India and Exhibit; Nike's own release gives no price.

For now it is sold on Flipkart and at select stores. Wider sales through Nike's partner stores in India start in spring 2027, and other countries get it in 2027.

What the money buys: a breathable engineered mesh upper, full-length foam cushioning and extra rubber around the outsole for durability. It comes in pink or green, and an After Dark Tour version is due around the Mumbai race in December.

Nike says it developed the shoe with runners across India.
