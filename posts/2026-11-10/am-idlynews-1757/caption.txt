Diwali would become the 12th federal holiday in the United States under the Diwali Day Act, which Congresswoman Grace Meng and a group of US lawmakers are re-introducing in the House of Representatives.

Five Indian-origin lawmakers are among its backers: Raja Krishnamoorthi, Ro Khanna, Ami Bera, Suhas Subramanyam and Shri Thanedar. Its backers say a federal holiday would promote cultural awareness and inclusion. No date has been announced for a debate or a vote, and the bill would have to pass Congress to take effect.
