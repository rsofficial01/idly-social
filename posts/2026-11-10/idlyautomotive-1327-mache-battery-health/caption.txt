Ford says the batteries in its first Mustang Mach-E electric SUVs are ageing well. It looked at about 36,000 Mach-Es from the 2021 model year, sold in the US and Canada, and says that after about five years the average battery still holds 92% of its original capacity.

By Ford's count, more than 2,000 of those cars have passed 100,000 miles (about 1.6 lakh km) averaging above 91% battery health, and more than 30 have passed 200,000 miles (about 3.2 lakh km) at 88% on average. It says 95% of the cars sit between 87% and 97%.

These are Ford's own figures, not an independent test.
