Renault India has started handing over the Duster Strong Hybrid E-Tech 160. The first buyers got their cars at a Hyderabad showroom on 6 November, and CarWale reported on 10 November that deliveries are under way across India.

The SUV pairs a 1.8-litre petrol engine with two electric motors and a 1.4 kWh battery for a combined 160 PS, and is ARAI-certified at 29 km/l. The battery charges on the move and never needs plugging in. Renault claims it can run on electric power for up to 80% of city driving.

Introductory ex-showroom prices: Evolution ₹16.99 lakh, Techno ₹18.99 lakh and Emotion ₹19.49 lakh. They apply to cars delivered until 31 December 2026. The Duster Hybrid's rivals are the strong-hybrid Maruti Grand Vitara, Maruti Victoris and Toyota Urban Cruiser Hyryder.
