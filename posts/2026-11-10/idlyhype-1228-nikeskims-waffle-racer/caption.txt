NikeSKIMS, Nike's joint brand with SKIMS, puts its Waffle Racer on sale on 10 November at nike.com/NikeSKIMS and SKIMS.com. It reworks Nike's 1970s Waffle Racer, keeping the waffle sole and low racing shape, with new detailing on the tongue, heel and sock liner.

There are three two-tone colourways: Baroque Brown, Thunder Blue and Light Bone/Varsity Red. Highsnobiety, Just Fresh Kicks and Sneaker Bar Detroit list the price at $130; Nike's own release gives no price.

The Retro Sport apparel collection follows on 17 November on the same two sites, with select Nike, SKIMS and partner stores after that.

No India release announced. SKIMS itself is entering India with Reliance Brands (announced Aug 2026), no opening date.
