Swiggy renamed itself 'Sweety' for Diwali, a temporary rebrand built around mithai orders.

It started on Instagram, where the display name changed and the account now follows only 11 'Sweety' accounts. Then came print and outdoor ads ("Oh Sweety, ghar aana"), 'Sweety' delivery bags and staff LinkedIn posts, with Swiggy Instamart and Noise joining in. Swiggy hasn't said how long it will last, and some brand experts question changing a name everyone already knows.
