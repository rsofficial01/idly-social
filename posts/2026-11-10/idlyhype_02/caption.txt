For Diwali, Swiggy renamed itself 'Sweety', a temporary rebrand built around mithai orders.

It started on Instagram, where the display name changed and the account followed only 11 'Sweety' accounts. Then came print and outdoor ads ("Oh Sweety, ghar aana"), 'Sweety' delivery bags and staff LinkedIn posts, with Swiggy Instamart and Noise joining in. Swiggy didn't say how long it would last, and some brand experts questioned changing a name everyone already knows.
