Scene, the critics' verdict: 6.8/10. Critics enjoy Suriya sending up his own image and Jithu Madhavan's fresh take on a cop thriller. Most find it great fun; a couple say the humour wears thin and the tension runs low. Hindustan Times's Neeshita Nyayapati: "Suriya truly has fun poking fun at himself".
Who should watch: In theatres. For fans of dark comedy and of Suriya sending up his own image.

Highs: Suriya, playing against type, is having fun · Jithu Madhavan's fresh spin on a cop thriller · Naslen and Nazriya are strong support
Lows: The pacing sags in stretches · The humour wears thin · The investigation is short on tension

Score: 6.8/10, the average of 13 published critic ratings, each converted to /10 (mean 6.8462, rounded half-up to one decimal). Highest: Times of India 8/10, Cinema Express 8/10 and Koimoi 8/10. Lowest: Deccan Chronicle 2/5. Only reviews published on or after the 6 Nov release count. idly has not watched the film; this post brings together published reviews.
