Scene, the critics' verdict: 6.8/10. Critics enjoy Suriya sending up his own image and Jithu Madhavan's fresh take on a cop thriller. Five call it great fun; two say the humour wears thin. Hindustan Times's Neeshita Nyayapati: "Suriya truly has fun poking fun at himself".

Where: in theatres since 6 Nov.
Watch it if: You like dark comedy and Suriya sending up his own hero image (8 reviews).
Skip it if: You want a tense investigation: The Week and Deccan Chronicle find it short on tension.

Highs: Suriya, playing against type, is having fun · Jithu Madhavan's fresh spin on a cop thriller · Naslen and Nazriya are strong support
Lows: The pacing sags in stretches · The humour wears thin · The investigation is short on tension
