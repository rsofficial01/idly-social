Apple has released iOS 27.2.1, and its security notes describe two flaws in iMessage group chats. One could let a remote attacker join an iMessage group chat and receive its messages. The other could let someone added to a group read messages sent before they joined.

The update, out on 9 November, also fixes Bluetooth bugs, including one where a malicious Bluetooth device may be able to pair with an iPhone without user interaction. Apple has not said any of the flaws were exploited.

Which update to install:
iPhone 11 and later: iOS 27.2.1
Still on iOS 26: iOS 26.7.3
iPhone XS, XS Max and XR: iOS 18.7.12 (Bluetooth and AirPlay fixes)
It is under Settings > General > Software Update. Macs get the same Messages fixes in macOS Golden Gate 27.2.1.
