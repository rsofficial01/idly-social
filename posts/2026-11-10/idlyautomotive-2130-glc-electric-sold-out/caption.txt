Mercedes-Benz India says its electric GLC is sold out till March 2027. Managing Director and CEO Santosh Iyer said so at the SUV's launch on 4 November, when the company put bookings at more than 1,000. Mercedes is seeking more assembly kits to build more cars locally and shorten the wait.

The electric GLC costs ₹78.5 lakh ex-showroom and comes in one version, the GLC 400 4MATIC, assembled from imported kits at Mercedes' Chakan plant near Pune. It has a 94 kWh battery and a claimed 711 km of WLTP range. First deliveries were due from Diwali.
