Tata-owned Jaguar Land Rover alleges that BYD's Ti7, a boxy seven-seat plug-in hybrid SUV, copies the Land Rover Defender, in a case in the Netherlands first reported by British newspapers.

In August, court-ordered bailiffs acting for JLR seized digital records from BYD's offices in Schiedam and Hoofddorp, according to The Sunday Times. The paper also reports that JLR suspects BYD took apart a Defender to reverse-engineer its technology. BYD's lawyers called the seizure a "fishing expedition" and argued that too many files were taken.

JLR says it does not comment on ongoing legal proceedings, and BYD has not commented publicly. The case is due in court in The Hague on 11 Nov, with a preliminary ruling expected this week. No finding of infringement has been made.

The Ti7 has been on sale in China since September 2025 and reaches the UK in January from £47,995, against £68,465 for a seven-seat Defender 110. It is not sold in India, where BYD sells four electric cars.
