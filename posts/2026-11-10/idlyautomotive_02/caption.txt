Ultraviolette's X-47 Venom electric motorcycle is out at an introductory ₹2.65 lakh for the first 5,000 buyers, then ₹3 lakh.

The company claims 343 km of range (IDC), 155 km/h and 0–100 km/h in 5.9 seconds. These are its own figures, not independently tested. It has an 11 kWh battery and supports 12 kW DC fast charging. Bookings are open on Ultraviolette's website and at its UV Space Stations, and deliveries start in November.
