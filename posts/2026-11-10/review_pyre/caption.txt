Pyre, the critics' verdict: 6.3/10. Critics call Vinod Kapri's drama about an elderly couple in an emptying Uttarakhand village moving and honest, with natural performances from its non-professional leads. Several warn that it is slow and unrelentingly sad. Hindustan Times's Rishabh Suri: "a film that stays with you".
Who should watch: In theatres. For viewers who want a quiet, sad drama about growing old.

Highs: Natural performances from its non-professional leads · A moving portrait of ageing and loneliness · A vivid sense of Uttarakhand's emptying villages
Lows: The slow middle stretch · Unrelentingly heavy and downbeat · Made more for festivals than the multiplex

Score: 6.3/10, the average of 4 published critic ratings, each converted to /10 (mean 6.25, rounded half-up to one decimal). Highest: Hindustan Times 3.5/5. Lowest: Indian Express 3/5, Outlook 3/5 and Rediff 6/10 (each 6.0/10). Only reviews published on or after the 30 Oct release count. idly has not watched the film; this post brings together published reviews.
