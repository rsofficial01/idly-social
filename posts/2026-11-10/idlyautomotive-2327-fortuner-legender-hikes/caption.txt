Toyota has raised Fortuner Legender prices for the third time in 2026. From 10 November, the 4x4 mild-hybrid automatic costs ₹48.77 lakh ex-showroom (up ₹48,000), the 4x4 manual ₹45.53 lakh (up ₹45,000) and the 4x2 automatic ₹43.35 lakh (up ₹43,000), CarWale and News24 report. Toyota has not announced any feature changes with this hike.

The earlier rises came on 8 January (up to ₹71,000) and 6 May (up to ₹83,000). Added up, the top variant now costs ₹2.02 lakh more ex-showroom than its 2025 price of ₹46.75 lakh; the 4x4 manual is up ₹1.89 lakh and the 4x2 automatic ₹1.81 lakh over the same period.
