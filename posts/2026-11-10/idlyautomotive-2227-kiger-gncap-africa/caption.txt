Global NCAP says the Indian-built Renault Kiger now scores four stars for adult occupant protection and three stars for children in its Safer Cars for Africa crash test, with results published on 10 November. The earlier version, with two airbags as standard and no ESC or ISOFIX, scored two stars and one star.

Global NCAP says the updated Kiger has six airbags, ESC and ISOFIX as standard. It also says one door opened in the side-impact test, and the footwell area and bodyshell were rated unstable. The three-year-old dummy's head hit the interior in the frontal test, and head exposure cut its side-impact points to zero; the 18-month-old dummy was fully protected in both tests. Global NCAP chief executive Richard Woods said the result shows "why six airbags must become a standard requirement."

The car tested was the South African model. In India, the Kiger starts at ₹5.81 lakh ex-showroom, and Renault India lists six airbags and ESC as standard; the India-spec car was not part of this test.
