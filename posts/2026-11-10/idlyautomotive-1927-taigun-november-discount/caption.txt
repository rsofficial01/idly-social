The 2025-built Volkswagen Taigun Topline 1.0 TSI manual gets up to ₹2.7 lakh in November benefits: a ₹2 lakh cash discount, a ₹30,000 exchange bonus and a corporate offer of up to ₹40,000.

A 2025-built Topline automatic gets ₹1.5 lakh cash and a 2025-built GT Plus Chrome 1.5 TSI DSG about ₹2 lakh, while a 2026-built Topline automatic gets ₹1 lakh. Volkswagen launched the updated Taigun in April 2026 at an introductory ₹10.99 lakh ex-showroom.

Existing Volkswagen owners can take a ₹20,000 loyalty bonus or the corporate offer, not both. Benefits vary by city and stock, so confirm the car's build year with the dealer.
