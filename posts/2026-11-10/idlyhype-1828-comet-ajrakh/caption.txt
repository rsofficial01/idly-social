Bengaluru sneaker label Comet has put Ajrakh, the hand-block-printed cloth of Kutch, Gujarat, on its Aeres low-top. Comet announced the Ajrakh collection on 4 November in Cream, Red and Black, at ₹5,899 on its site.

Comet says it used original Ajrakh cloth, not a digital copy of the pattern. Printing one cloth by hand can take up to three weeks, with colours from madder root, rusted iron, lime and gum. Comet also says the shoe was eight months in the making and that each pair passes through 150 hands.

At 7 pm IST on 10 November, Comet's site listed Black in UK sizes 3 to 10; Red and Cream were marked sold out.
