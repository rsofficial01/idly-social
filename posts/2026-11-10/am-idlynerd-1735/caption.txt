UK author M John Harrison has won the 2026 Booker Prize, worth $67,000, for his post-apocalyptic novel The End of Everything. At 81, he is the oldest winner in the prize's history.

Harrison has long written science fiction and fantasy, so the win puts a genre-leaning book at the centre of one of the biggest prizes in literature. The novel's strange, fragmented style has drawn both praise and puzzlement.
