Story mfg., the British label known for clothes made with artisans in Auroville, puts its second ASICS sneaker on sale on 11 November at storymfg.com.

The shoe is the GEL-TRANSCEND FJ, with a check print inspired by old hiking blankets, recycled overlays on a chunky mesh base and the outsole of the GEL-FujiTrabuco 7 trail shoe. It comes in Black and Dark Brown at $180 (£180 on Story mfg.'s UK store). A global release through ASICS and select stores follows on 20 November.

For India: Story mfg.'s site ships worldwide, with import duties and taxes calculated at checkout. No Indian stockist or ASICS India listing has been announced.
