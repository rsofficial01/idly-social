The Gangotri temple in Uttarakhand's Uttarkashi district closed for the winter at 12:05 pm on Tuesday, 10 November. Temple authorities say more than 10 lakh people visited this season. The Gangotri temple committee called it record-breaking footfall; in September, the state government had already called the shrine's 8.41 lakh visitors its highest ever.

The temple's idol is being carried in a palanquin procession to Mukhba village, its winter seat, where it is due on Wednesday, 11 November. Kedarnath (at 8:30 am) and Yamunotri also close on 11 November, the day of Bhai Dooj, with their winter seats at Ukhimath and Kharsali. Badrinath closes on 16 November, the last of the four Char Dham shrines. The state government said in October that the four shrines had already drawn a record 56 lakh-plus visitors this year.
