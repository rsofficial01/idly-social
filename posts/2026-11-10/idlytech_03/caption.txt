Sony has brought back the headphones that came with the first Walkman in 1979.

The MDR-3R keeps the original design and 23mm drivers, swaps the 3.5mm jack for USB-C, and adds an in-line mic and remote, plus blue and orange foam pads. There's also a limited Stranger Things edition with red pads and a black headband. Price, launch date and India availability haven't been announced.
