Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, briefly fell below $90 a barrel on Tuesday, 10 November, for the first time since August, touching its lowest level since 31 August.

The fall followed US President Donald Trump saying on Monday that the Iran war is "going to be ending fairly soon" and is effectively over. Trump also says the US naval blockade stays in place for now, and some analysts still doubt a lasting peace deal. Brent was above $117 a barrel at its late-October peak.

India imports nearly 9 in 10 barrels of the crude it uses. Its crude import bill for April to September 2026 was $90.6 billion, up 46% from $61.9 billion a year earlier, PPAC data show, driven by higher prices rather than bigger volumes.
