Delhi woke up to its worst Diwali air in five years. The city's average AQI hit 376, and 14 monitoring stations recorded 'severe' pollution after firecrackers went on past permitted hours.

Unhealthy air is expected to last the next few days. Children, older adults and people with breathing or heart conditions are most at risk, and residents are advised to limit time outdoors where they can.
