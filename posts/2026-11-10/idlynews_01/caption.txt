Delhi recorded its most polluted Diwali in five years. The average AQI on Diwali day (8 Nov) was 376, the highest since 2021's 382, as firecrackers went on past permitted hours.

On Monday morning, 14 of the 51 monitoring stations reporting data were in the 'severe' band. On the CPCB scale, 301–400 is 'very poor' and 401–500 is 'severe'. The Air Quality Early Warning System expects severe air over the next few days. Children, older adults and people with breathing or heart conditions are most at risk, and residents are advised to limit time outdoors where they can.

Delhi's Diwali-day AQI: 382 in 2021, 312 in 2022, 218 in 2023, 328 in 2024, 345 in 2025 and 376 this year.
