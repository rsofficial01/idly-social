Tamil Nadu has set aside ₹2,000 crore for the Vetri Laptop Scheme. It covers undergraduate college students up to their second year, and Chief Minister Vijay has ordered it to be implemented.

Not announced yet: how students apply, which colleges take part, and when distribution starts.
