Morning Brief, Tuesday 10 November.

Red Fort blast, one year on: a car exploded near Delhi's Red Fort on the evening of 10 November 2025. The NIA's charge sheets name 13 accused, among them the alleged bomber, who died in the blast; the allegations are yet to be tested at trial. A Delhi court took cognisance of the charge sheets in August. Survivors told PTI they are still dealing with debt and lost work.

Sonia Gandhi's memoir, 'Belonging: A Journey of Love', reached bookstores today. It recalls her decision to decline the prime minister's post after the UPA's 2004 win, and she has called it "not a political book" but "a personal one". BJP spokesperson CR Kesavan said it "should have been titled Whitewashing".

Anuj Shrivatri, 22, became India's 100th chess Grandmaster on Sunday after winning the 7th ASEAN Plus Individual Open in the Philippines. He is the first Grandmaster from Madhya Pradesh.

US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine resume in Abu Dhabi today, the first round since February. On Monday, PM Narendra Modi told Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy that India is ready to contribute to all efforts for a peaceful resolution, the PMO said.

US President Donald Trump said on Monday, after meeting Qatar's prime minister, that "Iran militarily is effectively over". Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian said Iran "will not surrender to force and bullying".
