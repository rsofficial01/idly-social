The main court of the Pitt Rivers Museum in Oxford: a white arched timber roof on ornate cast-iron ribs above tiers of galleries, a tall carved totem pole and hanging textile banners, with the headline: Oxford Museum Hands Back Remains of 39 Naga Ancestors. Detail slides: 39 Naga ancestral remains handed back on 9 November, to be buried together at the Naga Solidarity Park in Kohima; 1910 to 1940, when most Naga remains reached Oxford, brought mainly by colonial administrators J.H. Hutton and J.P. Mills; and 178 Naga objects that may contain human hair, which are not part of this return.
