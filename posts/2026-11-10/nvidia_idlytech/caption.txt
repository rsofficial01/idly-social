The Nvidia File, in 10 slides: the market hit, the memo, the probe, and what it means for India 🇮🇳

Nvidia shares fell 4.27% on Monday, Nov 9, wiping about $192 billion off its market value. They closed at $178.67, and are down about 22% over the past month.

On Sunday (U.S. time), Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation, run by independent directors with outside counsel. In a memo to staff, CEO Jensen Huang wrote: "I was unfaithful to my wife." Fortune and CBS News link the affair to Alice Huang (no relation), a former Megaspeed executive whom U.S. intelligence agencies reportedly believe is a Chinese spy. No charges have been reported, and China's foreign ministry calls the "spy narrative" "entirely baseless."

The board says it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. Huang remains CEO. No findings or timeline have been announced.

If you invest from India: on Sep 30, Nvidia was about 8% of the Motilal Oswal and ICICI Prudential Nasdaq-100 funds, their biggest holding. Information, not investment advice.

Dates to watch: Nov 17, Nvidia's earnings (3:30 AM IST, Nov 18). Nov 18, the closed-door briefing three House committee chairs have requested.
