October 2026 was the best month yet for electric two-wheelers: 2.24 lakh sold at retail, up 48.5% from a year earlier. The wider two-wheeler market shrank 28.35% in the same month, a drop Rushlane links to Dhanteras and Diwali falling in November this year.

TVS led with 60,490 units, ahead of Bajaj, Ather and Hero's Vida. Ola sold 12,098, down from 16,439 last October, even as the segment grew.
