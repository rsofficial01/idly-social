Tata sold 14,669 electric cars in India in October 2026, up 78% and about two in five of the 37,049 EVs sold that month, according to FADA retail figures reported by Autocar India.

India's electric car sales rose 76% from 20,995 in October 2025, even as overall car retail fell 14% to 5,07,768 units. EVs made up 7.3% of cars sold, against 3.55% a year earlier.

Mahindra sold 8,538 EVs (up 86%) and JSW MG 5,939 (up 11%). VinFast sold 2,571, up from 142, and Tesla 291, up from 40. Kia sold 1,395, Maruti Suzuki 1,200 and Hyundai 760.
