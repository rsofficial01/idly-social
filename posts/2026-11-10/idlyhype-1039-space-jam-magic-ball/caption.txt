Space Jam opened in US cinemas on 15 November 1996, and as the film turns 30, Jordan Brand is releasing a run of sneakers built around it.

This week's headliner is the Women's Air Jordan 11 'Magic Ball' (IZ1800-200), named after the ball that changes the game in the movie. Its Off Noir upper has a colour-shifting finish with Metallic Silver accents.

Three of the pairs:
Out now: Air Jordan 1 Low 'Magic Ball', US$130
11 Nov: Women's Air Jordan 11 'Magic Ball', US$235, on Nike's SNKRS app
14 Nov: Air Jordan 11 'Green Screen', US$255

No India release has been announced for any of them yet.
