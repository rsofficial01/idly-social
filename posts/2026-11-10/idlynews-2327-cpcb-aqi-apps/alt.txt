A NASA satellite image of north India on 10 November 2026: a grey haze spreads across the plains of Punjab, Haryana, Delhi and Uttar Pradesh below the snow-capped Himalaya. The headline reads "Why Apps Showed Delhi AQI 1,500+ but CPCB Under 400". The next slides explain, as CPCB puts it, that the official index stops at 500 (with the six bands from Good, 0–50, to Severe, 401–500), that it is a 24-hour average while apps often show hourly readings, and that its older particle analysers stop at 1,000 micrograms per cubic metre, so higher hourly readings are left out.
