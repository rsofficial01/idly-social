Nakhrewaalii, the critics' verdict: 4.6/10. Critics like Ansh Duggal and Pragati Srivastava and the role-reversal idea, but find the story old and the humour thin. Rediff's Sreeju Sudhakaran: "a tiresome watch".
Who should watch: In theatres. An undemanding rom-com if you like the two leads.

Highs: Ansh Duggal and Pragati Srivastava carry it · A role-reversal premise with something to say · A few laughs land
Lows: An old, predictable story · The humour runs thin · Too close to Dream Girl

Score: 4.6/10, the average of 7 published critic ratings, each converted to /10 (mean 4.5714, rounded half-up to one decimal). Highest: Hindustan Times 6/10 and News18 6/10. Lowest: Rediff 2/10.
