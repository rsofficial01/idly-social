Superkicks lists Nike's Air Max 95 Big Bubble Premium in Black/Dark Smoke Grey/Smoke Grey/Chrome Yellow (style code IQ0229-001) at ₹16,995, in UK sizes 6 to 12. Nice Kicks and Sneaker Bar Detroit put its US release on Nike.com and at select stores on 15 November.

Sneaker sites call it the 'Batman' Air Max 95 for its black, grey and yellow colours; it isn't a DC collaboration. It's one of three shoes in that colour pack, with the Air Max Plus and Air Max 90. The Big Bubble version has larger Max Air units in the heel and forefoot, Nike says.
