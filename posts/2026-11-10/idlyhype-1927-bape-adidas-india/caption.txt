BAPE and adidas Originals' new basketball collection is now on sale in India at VegNonVeg. Five pieces are listed: the BABY MILO tee at ₹5,999, the warm-up top at ₹8,999, the reversible Adibreak pant at ₹17,999, the reversible Shark hoodie at ₹21,999 and the black varsity jacket at ₹59,999.

The collection is built on BAPE's purple camo and late-'90s and early-2000s basketball style. It went on sale worldwide on 7 November through the adidas CONFIRMED app, select retailers, BAPE stores and BAPE.com. The varsity jacket has gold studs and a chenille 'World Gone Mad' graphic on the back; the Shark hoodie is reversible mesh with a shark face on the hood.

On BAPE's US store, the tee lists at $60 and the Shark hoodie at $240, before sales tax.
