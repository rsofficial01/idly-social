Renault dispatched 601 Dusters to its dealers in October 2026, and Nissan 406 Tektons, the SUV built on the Duster's platform. That is 1,007 between them, according to model-wise figures reported by Rushlane and AutoPunditz. In the same month, Hyundai dispatched 17,955 Cretas.

Both started stronger. The Duster, back on sale since March at ₹10.49–18.49 lakh ex-showroom, peaked at 2,359 in April, its first full month. The Tekton, launched on 9 July at the same ₹10.49 lakh starting price, managed 1,670 in July. Within Nissan, the Magnite (1,656) and the Gravite (1,532) both outsold it in October.

Autocar Professional's September sales analysis pointed to two reasons: unlike the original Duster, the new one has no diesel engine, and the mid-size SUV segment now has more than 20 models. The Duster strong hybrid reached its first buyers on 6 November, so it is not in these numbers.

These are factory dispatches to dealers, not registrations.
