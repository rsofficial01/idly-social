Xiaomi launches the Xiaomi 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max worldwide tomorrow, Tuesday 17 November, at 6:30 PM IST, according to Xiaomi's global website, which lists 2 PM GMT+1. Beebom reports the event is in Paris.

Xiaomi has confirmed the 18 Pro for India: the company announced it at Qualcomm's Snapdragon event at India Mobile Congress 2026 in New Delhi, Beebom reports. Xiaomi has not given an India date or price, and only the 18 Pro, not the Pro Max, is confirmed for India. A December India launch is a tipster's claim reported by 91mobiles.

In China, where it launched in September, the 18 Pro starts at 5,999 yuan for 12GB RAM and 256GB storage, Gizchina reports. That is about ₹85,000 at today's exchange rate, by our conversion. It has two 200MP Leica cameras (main and periscope telephoto, with 3.2x optical zoom), a 50MP ultra-wide, a small second display on the back, a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chip and a 7,000mAh battery in China. A leaked Italian retail listing, Beebom reports, suggests global units get a 6,000mAh battery.
