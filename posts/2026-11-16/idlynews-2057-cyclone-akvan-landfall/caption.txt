Cyclone Akvan is forecast to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Nellore and Tuni around noon on Friday, 20 November, as a very severe cyclonic storm with winds of 120-130 km/h gusting to 145 km/h, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) says. The deep depression in the Bay of Bengal became a cyclonic storm and was named Akvan on Monday evening, according to IMD's 8:35 pm bulletin.

IMD forecasts extremely heavy rain (21 cm or more at isolated places) in coastal Andhra Pradesh on 19 and 20 November, and warns of flooded roads and waterlogging in low-lying areas. North Tamil Nadu and Puducherry may get very heavy rain on 18 and 19 November, and Odisha on 19 to 21 November.

Akvan is the third cyclone to head for the Andhra coast since 28 October, after Arnab and Murasu.
