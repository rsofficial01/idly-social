United Launch Alliance's Vulcan rocket failed about 10 minutes after lifting off from Cape Canaveral, Florida, at 11:10 pm IST on Sunday, 15 November. All 40 Amazon Leo broadband satellites on board were lost, and ULA says it does not yet know why.

Amazon Leo is Amazon's answer to SpaceX's Starlink. This was the first of 38 Vulcan launches Amazon has booked. "There is risk with any launch, and that's especially true for inaugural missions like LV-01," Amazon said.

In India, Amazon first applied for a satellite internet licence in October 2023 and still has none. Starlink, Eutelsat OneWeb and Jio Satellite hold licences, but none has started commercial service while security clearance and spectrum pricing are pending.
