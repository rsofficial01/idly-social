Redmi phones cost more in India from today, Monday 16 November. Retailers say Xiaomi has raised prices on 19 Redmi variants by ₹1,000 to ₹4,000, Moneycontrol reports, and 91mobiles reports hikes of up to ₹4,000 across the Redmi range.

The Redmi Note 17 Pro (8GB+128GB) rises by ₹4,000, from ₹36,999 to ₹40,999, and the Redmi Note 17 (6GB+128GB) by ₹3,000, from ₹27,999 to ₹30,999. The old prices are those The Tech Outlook found on Xiaomi's India site this morning. Moneycontrol and 91mobiles both point to rising memory chip costs.
