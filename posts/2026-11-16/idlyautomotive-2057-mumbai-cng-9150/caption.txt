Mumbai's CNG price went up again: ₹91.50 a kg from 15 November, ₹1.50 more than the ₹90 rate set on 1 November.

It's Mahanagar Gas's eighth CNG hike of 2026. A kg cost ₹80.50 in January, so it's up ₹11 this year.

A 10 kg refill now costs ₹915, against ₹805 in January (our conversion).

Mahanagar Gas has linked the hike to costlier gas: less domestic gas allotted to it, more expensive imported gas and the Middle East crisis, reports say. The new rate applies across Mumbai and the wider metropolitan region.
