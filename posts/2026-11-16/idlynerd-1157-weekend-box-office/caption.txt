Weekend Box Office, Friday 13 to Sunday 15 November.

Ramayana: Part 1 made ₹196 crore India net across all languages over its second weekend, trade tracker Sacnilk estimates. Sacnilk calls it the biggest second weekend in India yet, past Pushpa 2 (₹176.30 crore) and Dhurandhar 2 (₹172.70 crore). It also beat the film's own first weekend, ₹174 crore over 6 to 8 November. Sunday alone brought in ₹75 crore.

After 11 days, Ramayana stands at ₹760.60 crore India net and ₹1,384.50 crore gross worldwide, by Sacnilk's count. Bollywood Hungama's count is lower: ₹184.41 crore net for the weekend and ₹724.04 crore net in all. These are trade estimates, not the producers' figures.

Suriya's Scene added ₹24.8 crore net in its second weekend, taking it to ₹136.5 crore India net in 10 days, Sacnilk and Koimoi say, and over ₹240 crore gross worldwide. That puts it past Vishwanath & Sons as Suriya's second-biggest film, after Karuppu.

On Friday 20 November, Ramayana's two-week exclusive run on India's IMAX screens ends, and Godzilla Minus Zero opens in Indian cinemas, IMAX included.
