GTA VI had passed 12 million pre-orders as of 13 November, market data firm Sensor Tower estimates, worth more than $1 billion. On 11 November it was 10.31 million. The game launches in three days.

About three in four of those pre-orders are on PS5; the rest are on Xbox Series X|S. These are third-party estimates of digital pre-orders. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't given a count.

In India, store listings show GTA VI unlocking at 12:00 am IST on Thursday, 19 November. PlayStation Store India lists the Standard Edition at ₹5,999 and the Ultimate Edition at ₹7,499.
