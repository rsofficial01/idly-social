Tata dealers are offering benefits of up to ₹1.15 lakh on the diesel Tata Sierra this November, according to Autocar India (9 November) and Auto Punditz (3 November). The top figure is for the diesel Accomplished trims. The Pure and Adventure diesels get up to ₹95,000, the base Smart+ diesel ₹70,000, and most petrol versions up to ₹70,000. The Legend editions get ₹45,000.

The Sierra went on sale on 25 November 2025, and prices start at ₹11.49 lakh ex-showroom. In the same November offers, the Tata Curvv gets up to ₹1.25 lakh on variants from before its September 2026 Series X update (Series X variants get ₹20,000), and the Nexon up to ₹75,000 on petrol manuals.

These are maximum figures that combine several benefits, not a flat price cut, and the amounts vary by city and dealer stock. Neither report gives an end date beyond November.
