Nike Mind 002, the sneaker with moving foam nodes in its sole, is listed at ₹12,795 in India: every Mind 002 style on nike.in shows that price, and Superkicks lists the Mind 002 Flyknit at the same ₹12,795, with both colourways showing unavailable (listings checked Monday 16 November).

On 16 November, Nike showed four new Nike Mind shoes at its World of Athletes showcase: the Mind Sock, Mind Sneaker, Mind Slip and Mind Moc Flyknit. Complex and Sneaker News report they reach shops from summer 2027. Nike's announcement gives no prices.

The Mind Sock keeps the original 22 separate nodes for the most intense feel, Nike says. The Sneaker, Slip and Moc Flyknit join the nodes together for a softer feel meant for all-day wear.

Nike also showed the Vaporfly 5 racing shoe, still a prototype and due in fall 2027 according to Complex and Sneaker News, plus the Vomero 19, Devin Booker's Book 3 and new Nike Free 3.0 and 5.0 training shoes.
