Morning Brief, Monday 16 November.

The weather system in the Bay of Bengal became a deep depression late on Sunday, about 870 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said in its 2:30 am bulletin. IMD expects a cyclonic storm by this evening and a severe cyclonic storm by Tuesday evening, moving towards the Andhra Pradesh coast. Along and off that coast it forecasts winds of 120-130 km/h, gusting to 145 km/h, from the evening of 19 November to the morning of 20 November. IMD has not named a landfall point. Fishermen are told to stay out of the central Bay from 16 to 20 November.

PM Narendra Modi flies to Manila today for the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the East Asia Summit on 17 November. On Sunday, Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal said India would get "preferential trade access in the US, with lower duties than our competitor nations" under the trade deal being negotiated.

Twenty-six people were killed when a minibus and a container truck collided head-on at Rongajan, Kaziranga, in Assam on Friday evening. The minibus owner has been arrested over alleged motor vehicle violations, police say.

India will host the 2029 Champions Trophy from 9 to 28 October, under the event dates the ICC Board fixed in Bali on Sunday.

In Yemen, Houthi forces are about 60 km from Aden, the government's interim capital, AFP reports, citing military sources.
