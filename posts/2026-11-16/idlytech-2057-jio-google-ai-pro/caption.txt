Jio has closed its free Google AI Pro offer to new users. Jio's own help page now says the Google Gemini offer has ended and is no longer available on new recharges or plan upgrades.

The offer, announced by Google and Reliance on 30 Oct 2025, gave eligible Jio 5G users 18 months of Google AI Pro at no extra cost. Google valued the package at about ₹35,100 at launch. On its own, Google AI Pro costs ₹1,950 a month in India.

If you already claimed it, Jio's FAQ says you keep the full 18 months, but only while you stay on a Jio unlimited 5G plan of ₹349 or above. Jio's separate 12-month Canva offer is still on: Canva Pro Lite with the ₹349 plan, Canva Pro with the ₹399 plan.
