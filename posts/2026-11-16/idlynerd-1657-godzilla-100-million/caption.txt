Godzilla Minus Zero has crossed $100 million at the worldwide box office in its second weekend, Variety and Deadline report. Toho's sequel to the Oscar-winning Godzilla Minus One opened in Japan on 3 November and in North America on 6 November, where it has made about $49 million so far, according to The Numbers.

India gets it this Friday, 20 November, in cinemas including IMAX screens. PVR INOX Pictures, which is releasing the film in India, confirmed the date to Bollywood Hungama.
