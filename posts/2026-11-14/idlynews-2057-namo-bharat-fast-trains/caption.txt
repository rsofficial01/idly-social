Eight fast Namo Bharat trains start running on the Delhi-Meerut corridor on Monday, 16 November, the National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC) says. The limited-stop trains will halt only at Sarai Kale Khan, Anand Vihar, Ghaziabad, Begumpul and Modipuram, and NCRTC says they will cover the whole corridor in under 50 minutes.

NCRTC puts Anand Vihar to Ghaziabad at 8 minutes, Anand Vihar to Begumpul at 36 minutes and Ghaziabad to Begumpul at 28 minutes. The fast trains leave both ends, Sarai Kale Khan and Modipuram, at 8 am and 8:30 am, and again at 6 pm and 6:30 pm. Regular Namo Bharat trains keep running every 10 minutes, and NCRTC has asked passengers to check station display boards and announcements to tell a fast train from a regular one.

The corridor carries about 1.1 lakh passengers a day, NCRTC says, and the extra trains are meant to draw more people to public transport as air pollution rises.
