Sony's QSSR (Quick Spectral Super Resolution) upscaler is now live in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 on the standard PS5, through a game update released on 13 November. It is built on the same foundation as the PS5 Pro's PSSR, and Sony says it brings sharper detail, cleaner edges and a steadier picture, in both the Fidelity and Performance modes.

Marvel's Wolverine and Ghost of Yōtei have offered it as a graphics option since 1 October. Five more games are lined up: Control Resonant in a December update, No Man's Sky within the next few weeks, and ARC Raiders, THE FINALS and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam with no date yet. Sony says it is offering QSSR to all PlayStation developers.

It all arrives through game updates, and you don't need a PS5 Pro.
