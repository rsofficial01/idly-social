The Kia Seltos has crossed 7 lakh sales in India: 7,04,829 units between its launch in August 2019 and October 2026. Another 1.6 lakh-plus have been exported from India, more than any other Kia built here.

The second-generation Seltos, launched in January 2026, sold 1,13,078 units from January to October, 87% more than in the same months of 2025. October alone brought 13,423, up from 7,130 a year earlier. That follows a slump in FY2025 (April 2024-March 2025), when Seltos sales fell 27% to 72,618.

Kia India has sold 2,86,470 vehicles from January to October 2026, up 21% and already above its full-year 2025 record of 2,80,286.
