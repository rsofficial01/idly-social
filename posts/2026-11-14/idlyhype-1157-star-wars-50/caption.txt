Star Wars turns 50 in 2027, and Disney and Lucasfilm opened the anniversary year on 12 November with a line-up of brand collaborations.

LEGO's seven 50th-anniversary sets arrive from 1 January 2027 at LEGO.com and LEGO Stores, priced from $39.99 to $299.99 in the US. The 628-piece Darth Vader 2-in-1 Wall Art on our cover is $69.99. Hasbro's figures inspired by the 1977 film are on preorder now. Returning partners adidas and Mars, plus Aston Martin, Steinway and artist Hajime Sorayama (presented by Complex), are also named, with no products shown yet. India prices and dates have not been announced.

The same day, Heritage Auctions sold Darth Vader's screen-matched duelling lightsaber from The Empire Strikes Back and Return of the Jedi for $4.875 million, about ₹46 crore by idly's maths at the ECB's 13 November rate of ₹94.25 to the dollar. Heritage says that is a world auction record for any Star Wars collectible, beating the $3.875 million paid for Tom Jung's original poster painting for the first film.
