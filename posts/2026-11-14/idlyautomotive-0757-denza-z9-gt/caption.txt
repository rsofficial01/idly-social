Denza, BYD Group's luxury brand, launched the Z9 GT in India on Friday, 13 November, at ₹1.57 crore ex-showroom. The five-seat shooting brake is fully imported, and India gets only the all-electric version.

Three motors make a combined 850 kW (1,156 PS) and 1,210 Nm, for a claimed 0-100 km/h time of 2.7 seconds and a top speed of 270 km/h. The 122.49 kWh battery has a claimed range of 670 km on the older NEDC test cycle.

Denza says that peak output matches Porsche's Cayenne Turbo Electric, an SUV priced at ₹2.28 crore ex-showroom (before options) since its India launch on 30 September. Porsche quotes that peak with Launch Control engaged. On paper, the Z9 GT costs about ₹70 lakh less, though the two are different kinds of car.

Bookings are open at ₹10 lakh at Denza-only showrooms in Gurugram, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad. The warranty runs 6 years or 1.5 lakh km, and 8 years on the electric powertrain.
