Morning Brief, Saturday 14 November.

A low-pressure area formed over the southeast Bay of Bengal early on Friday and is likely to become a depression around Sunday, 15 November, and a cyclonic storm over the central Bay by Monday evening, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) said. IMD has not named a landfall area yet. Odisha has put district collectors on alert, with yellow warnings for heavy rain in Kendrapara and Jagatsinghpur on 18-19 November and in Rayagada, Gajapati, Ganjam and Puri on 19-20 November. Fishermen in Andhra Pradesh and Odisha have been told not to go to sea.

At least 25 people were killed when a minibus and a container truck collided head-on at Rangajan, near Kaziranga National Park in Assam's Golaghat district, on Friday afternoon, officials said. Police say early findings point to speeding on a highway stretch with speed limits for animal crossings. PM Narendra Modi announced ₹2 lakh for each victim's family and ₹50,000 for each injured person.

India's merchandise exports reached a record $49.47 billion in October, up about 45% on the year, the fastest growth in five years, Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal said on Friday. Petroleum products led the rise, helped by higher oil prices, and 43 of its 50 largest export markets bought more than a year earlier.

The Centre has given the commission inquiring into the ethnic violence that began in Manipur in May 2023 a sixth extension; its report is now due by 20 May 2027, a Home Ministry notification says. Separately, police say four Naga civilians were killed when suspected militants ambushed two vehicles on NH-202 in Ukhrul district on Friday.

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin will hold a three-way meeting in Shenzhen on 19 November, during the APEC summit, the White House said on Friday. It said nuclear arms reduction will be discussed; a US official said no agreement is expected.
